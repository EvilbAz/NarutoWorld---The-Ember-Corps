<div class="change-entry"><div class="change-date">27 SEPTEMBER 2026 <span>·</span> NAVIGATION UPDATE</div><h2>CLAN INDEX & INDIVIDUAL JUTSU SCHOOLS</h2><p>The Clans &amp; Lineages sidebar now shows a single flat link for each clan, with no nested Passives, Abilities or Jutsu entries. Search results and legacy Jutsu section links now lead to the individual school pages.</p></div>

Added searchable section index, volume navigation, responsive reading layout, light/dark display and source-document links.</p><p>Volume V illustrations are linked back to the original Google Doc in the web edition. The written rules and tables are included.</p></div>
