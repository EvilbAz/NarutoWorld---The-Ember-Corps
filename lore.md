## History
{:#history}

### Before the Bloom
The oldest surviving histories describe the Five Great Nations and their Hidden Villages: Konoha, Suna, Kiri, Kumo and Iwa. Clans, Kage and their armies fought wars capable of reshaping entire regions. Their descendants still carry old names, techniques and bloodlines, but those villages no longer function as the political allegiances of modern Hunters. A clan's history may be ancient; its present loyalties are its own.

### The Bloom
Nobody agrees on the exact date the old world ended. Natural chakra surged through the environment, and the natural world changed faster than people could contain it. Forests spread, rivers moved, and animals developed chakra organs, elemental abilities, armour, regeneration and sometimes intelligence. The old nations attempted to hold their territory. Their borders did not survive.

### The Age of Havens
Within generations, maps were measured less by borders than by the territories of dangerous creatures. Survivors gathered around defensible resources and rebuilt where they could. Havens now hold fragments of the old world together through trade, scavenging, diplomacy, sealing arrays and the work of Hunters. No one knows how much of the world has truly been reclaimed.

## The World Now
{:#world}

### The Wastes
Beyond settled walls lie migrating herds, predators, raiders, scavenger crews, cults, lost technology and creatures powerful enough to alter an entire region. Roads are temporary. A reliable account of a monster's habits can be worth more than a weapon, and an outdated map can get an expedition killed.

### Old-world remnants
Ruined villages, buried transport routes, abandoned workshops and sealed archives survive beneath the new landscape. Their contents attract researchers, salvagers and people hoping to reclaim a little of the world that existed before the Bloom. Old clan names endure, but they are not proof of a living nation or an intact ancestral home.

### The Havens
Settlements survive around water, farmland, industry, shelter and defensible terrain. Some trade with their neighbours; others close their gates. Alliances and rivalries change as quickly as the routes between them. Information travels with caravans, couriers and returning Hunters, so even famous names may arrive distorted.

## Haven Ember
{:#haven}

Haven Ember is a large independent settlement built around an old underground water system and the remains of a pre-Fall transport hub. Several thousand people live inside its repaired inner walls and scrap-built outer districts. Sealing arrays reinforce its defences. Farms, workshops, clinics, shrines, markets, training yards and a monster-processing hall support the population, although resources remain strained.

### The Ember Corps
The Corps is not an old-world standing army. It brings together Hunters, scouts, medics, salvagers, couriers, researchers, guides, mechanics and negotiators. Crews form around individual missions and often return with different members. Their work ranges from escorting caravans and mapping territory to researching the Bloom and confronting creatures that threaten whole settlements.

A Hunter's reputation is built through what they do, who survives to tell the story and which settlements hear it. Some become household names. Others are remembered only by the people they brought home.

## Famous Hunters & the Bingo Book
{:#reputation}

The most dangerous or widely recognised people in the Wastes appear in stories, trade-route warnings and Bingo Book records. **Fame, Infamy and combat strength are different things.** A powerful Hunter might remain unknown, while a rescuer, explorer, criminal or political figure becomes famous without being the strongest fighter in the room.

A complete worldwide register has not yet been established. Rather than invent deeds or rankings for existing characters, this archive will add named Hunters when their identities, affiliations and recorded actions have been approved for publication. Giari's advisory position and Kagetsu's character record are documented above; neither entry is presented as a worldwide fame ranking.

## Open Records
{:#open-records}

This archive is deliberately incomplete. The dates of the Bloom, the fate of particular old-world sites, the full list of Havens and the identities of every legendary Hunter remain subjects for future discoveries and approved lore submissions. Accounts may disagree, especially when the only witnesses are returning expeditions.

The [Core Rules]({{ '/rules/core/' | relative_url }}) remain the authority for mechanics, while this archive records the setting. As missions, character stories and approved community submissions become canon, their history and dossiers can be added here without rewriting the rules.
