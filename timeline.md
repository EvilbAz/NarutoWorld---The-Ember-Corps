  <section class="timeline-era">
    <div class="timeline-era-head"><span>PRELUDE</span><h2>Before 1.0</h2><p>The first weeks of Ember Corps. Hunters tested the Wastes, the Haven learned what survival would cost, and something beneath the earth began to wake.</p></div>
    <div class="timeline-stream">
      <article class="timeline-entry hunt"><time>29–30 AUG 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">HUNT</span><h3>The Dreadroad Rat King</h3><p>Aki Uchiha, Sozuki and Akira Karashi leave Ember Haven on one of its first recorded hunts. They return with the Rat King's corpse. Sozuki is carried home on a stretcher, an inch from death.</p><p class="timeline-outcome">OUTCOME · Rat King slain · 8,500 Ryō awarded to each Hunter</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry haven"><time>30 AUG 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">HAVEN</span><h3>The Ashen Anvil Opens</h3><p>With successful hunts beginning to bring trophies and coin back into Ember, the Ashen Anvil opens its doors. A dedicated armoury begins supplying Hunters with weapons and field gear.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry hunt"><time>30–31 AUG 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">HUNT</span><h3>The Hollow Crocodile</h3><p>Aki, Kagetsu Yoton, Ren Kumoito and Akira hunt an Ironhide Croc. The monster is brought back dead; Ren returns barely alive, continuing Ember's early tradition of victories paid for in blood.</p><p class="timeline-outcome">OUTCOME · Ironhide Croc slain · 8,500 Ryō awarded to each Hunter</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry crisis"><time>02 SEP 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">TERRITORIAL DISPUTE</span><h3>Four Monsters, One Hunt</h3><p>The Grave-Bone Boar and Twin-Nerve Gorilla hunts collide with a Blackrain Wyrm and a Blacksteel Paladin. A panic flare calls for backup. During the fighting, a beam of white energy erupts into the sky and unnatural weather rolls across the region.</p><p>The Hunters return battered but alive. The Haven is left with a question that will define the Prelude: did they defeat a Calamity—or awaken one?</p><p class="timeline-outcome">OUTCOME · Four major trophies · 12,000 Ryō each · 211 XP</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry crisis"><time>05 SEP 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">HAVEN INCIDENT</span><h3>The Wolves Descend</h3><p>A spar erupts into a dragon of lightning over Ember's training grounds. Tazuko Sarutobi is taken into custody and sentenced to a 100,000 Ryō life-debt after teammates are left with severe burns and wounds.</p><blockquote>There are techniques that exist for monsters, and techniques meant for men.</blockquote></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry hunt"><time>07–08 SEP 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">HUNT</span><h3>The Many-Mouthed Elk</h3><p>A hunting party disappears into the Wastes for what becomes a week in-world. Pressure mounts at home. Chogo, Hellio and Oshiri eventually return grievously wounded with the trophy of the Many-Mouthed Elk.</p><p>Isamu Hoshigaki does not return with them.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry hunt"><time>08–11 SEP 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">HUNT</span><h3>The Deadroad Hound</h3><p>Reiaske Utegare, Shirui Yotsuki, Chogo and Smoky pursue the Deadroad Hound. They return unharmed, but empty-handed: Ember records one of its first unsuccessful hunts.</p><p class="timeline-outcome">OUTCOME · Hunt unsuccessful · 25 XP · 1 DT refunded</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry hunt"><time>13–15 SEP 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">CAPTURE</span><h3>The Rubble Hyena Returns Alive</h3><p>Chogo, Jujutsuking, Akira and Tsuki achieve something that shocks the Haven: they bring a Predator-Class monster back alive for study rather than killing it in the field.</p><p class="timeline-outcome">OUTCOME · Rubble Hyena captured · 108 XP · 1 Talent Point each</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry haven"><time>17 SEP 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">SYSTEM ERA</span><h3>The Final Test Before 1.0</h3><p>For the final stretch of testing, all Capability Talent TNs are reduced to 0. Ember enters its last experimental days before the 1.0 ruleset.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry calamity"><time>20 SEP 2026</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">CALAMITY WARNING</span><h3>Haven Enryu Falls</h3><p>Carrier birds arrive from the west. Haven Enryu has fallen to a Calamity. With the reports comes a sample: white crystal, quartz.</p><p>The final message from the dead Haven is simple: <strong>it is coming for Ember next.</strong></p></div></article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="timeline-era timeline-launch">
    <div class="timeline-era-head"><span>29–30 SEP 2026</span><h2>The Crown Beneath</h2><p>Weeks of preparation end when the earth beneath Ember begins to move. This is the final event of the Prelude—and the first great scar of the 1.0 era.</p></div>
    <div class="timeline-stream">
      <article class="timeline-entry calamity"><time>THE PROCESSION</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">CALAMITY</span><h3>The Hunt Begins</h3><p>Wildlife stampedes toward Ember as enormous Digging Worms breach the defensive approaches. Hunters hold the outer wilds, protect the walls, evacuate civilians and ignite the False Hearth to lure the approaching Calamity away from the Haven.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry calamity"><time>THE CROWN RISES</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">CALAMITY</span><h3>The Crown Beneath Emerges</h3><p>A colossal armoured creature erupts from the earth. Hunters shatter its forelegs, dorsal armour, weather spires and mandibles while Ember's artillery comes online. The creature is stopped outside the walls—until a strange crystal behind its skull lights up and forces it onward.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry crisis"><time>EMBER BREAKS</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">DISASTER</span><h3>The Calamity Enters the Haven</h3><p>The Crown crashes through Ember. Hunters save the Academy, Great Gate, civilian shelters, Archives and Hospital, but cannot save everything.</p><p class="timeline-loss">LOST · The Market & Workshops · The Hunters' Graveyard</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry calamity"><time>SEVEN WINGS FALL</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">DEATH / BIJŪ</span><h3>Sei Falls. Chōmei Rises.</h3><p>An extraction lattice fires through Sei Aburame. She dies reaching for Hana. Chōmei tears free in grief, and the Hunters turn their assault on the crystal lattice trying to claim the newly freed Bijū.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry calamity"><time>CROWNLESS</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">REVELATION</span><h3>The Rhinestone Crown Shatters</h3><p>The Hunters destroy the extraction spires and break the Rhinestone Crown. Without the crystal command structure, the Calamity stops acting with purpose. Its exposed Heart becomes the final target.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry calamity"><time>THE FINAL STRIKE</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">CALAMITY SLAIN</span><h3>The Crown Beneath Dies</h3><p>The Heart shatters. The Crown Beneath collapses into the ruins of Ember and does not rise again. Sei, Chōgo, the Market, the Graveyard and countless others are gone.</p><p>Then white flowers begin to bloom from the corpse.</p></div></article>
      <article class="timeline-entry crisis"><time>THE BLOOM</time><div><span class="timeline-tag">UNKNOWN</span><h3>The Man in White</h3><p>A man in white emerges untouched from the Bloom. Hana and Banchō attack him; he allows himself to be killed, then returns whole as the flowers rewind around him.</p><p>Before leaving, he warns the Hunters that the Calamity was not the true disaster. Someone is trying to make the creatures of the Bloom obey—and Ember may be repeating the same mistake.</p></div></article>
    </div>
