# **NARUTO WORLD: EMBER CORPS**

## *FERAL ELEMENTS*

The old world had names for almost everything chakra could do. The Wastes have made some of those names useless.

Hunters use Feral Element for chakra pushed beyond its ordinary nature. Some learn it from another Hunter. Some pull the method from a ruin, a monster, or an environment that has spent years trying to kill them.

A Feral Element does more than change a technique. It scars the fight around it. Build that Scar, hold it long enough to make the battlefield yours, then spend the advantage before the world settles back into place.

# **AT A GLANCE**

* Feral Element is a Capability Talent learned from a valid Source.  
* Choose Abyss, Storm, Glass, Vacuum, Cinder or Void Release. The Talent grants one Feral Element.  
* Abyss, Storm, Glass, Vacuum and Cinder build from Elemental Mastery. Void builds from B-Rank Non-Elemental Ninjutsu.  
* Qualifying Jutsu can be Feralized. Their Rank determines Feral Intensity and the amount of Scar they create.  
* Scars have Severity 1–5 and a Fade timer. Reapplying a Scar refreshes that timer; neglected Scars fall away.  
* Severity 5 is Overrun. An unanchored Overrun Scar begins collapsing after 10 IC.  
* Feral Jutsu with Upkeep Anchor the Scars they sustain, freezing their Fade timer while the Upkeep remains.  
* Every Release follows the same combat rhythm: Establish, Build, Overrun, Exploit, Collapse, Rebuild.  
* After three Developments, Feral Dominion unlocks alongside the Apex Mutation of your chosen Release.

# **FERAL ELEMENT**

***Capability Talent***

Feral Elements come from rare knowledge and stranger environments. A Source can be another Feral user, a recovered technique, a monster specimen, a ruined training record, or a researched phenomenon from beyond Haven Ember. Once you have a suitable Source, learn the Talent through the normal Capability Talent rules.

## **Core Talent**

Choose one Feral Element. That Release becomes the Feral Element granted by this Talent.

Abyss Release requires Elemental Mastery: Suiton. Storm Release requires Elemental Mastery: Raiton. Glass Release requires Elemental Mastery: Doton. Vacuum Release requires Elemental Mastery: Fūton. Cinder Release requires Elemental Mastery: Katon.

Void Release requires access to B-Rank Non-Elemental Ninjutsu.

## **Feralizing a Jutsu**

When declaring a C-Rank or higher Ninjutsu of the matching base nature, you may Feralize it. The Jutsu keeps its normal tags and gains its Feral Release tag. Abyss remains Suiton, Storm remains Raiton, Glass remains Doton, Vacuum remains Fūton and Cinder remains Katon for elemental interactions. Void remains Non-Elemental.

Feral Conversion applies to Jutsu with a target, Area, Construct, Barrier, weapon or persistent effect that the Release can scar or transform.

## **Feral Intensity**

A Feralized Jutsu uses its Rank to determine Feral Intensity:

**C-Rank** — Feral Intensity 1\.

**B-Rank** — Feral Intensity 2\.

**A-Rank** — Feral Intensity 3\.

**S-Rank** — Feral Intensity 4\.

After calculating the Jutsu's normal final Chakra cost, increase it by 2 × Feral Intensity.

## **Native Feral Jutsu**

A Jutsu printed with a Feral Release tag is a native Feral Jutsu. It counts as Feralized, uses its Rank for Feral Intensity and applies its Release rules. Native Feral Jutsu pay their printed Chakra cost without the additional cost for Feralizing them.

# **FERAL SCARS**

Feral chakra forces the world into a state it cannot comfortably hold. Abyss creates Depth, Storm creates Charge, Glass creates Shards, Vacuum creates Dead Air, Cinder creates Ash, and Void creates Null.

Each Scar has Severity 1–5. Applying the same Scar again increases its Severity to a maximum of 5 and refreshes its Fade timer.

## **Scar States**

**Scarred — Severity 1–2** — The Release has established itself and begins rewarding continued pressure.

**Feral — Severity 3–4** — The Scar becomes a major part of the fight and unlocks the Release's stronger bonuses.

**Overrun — Severity 5** — The Release has completely taken hold. Overrun effects are the strongest payoff and the shortest-lived state without an Anchor.

## **Fade**

Every Scar tracks one Fade timer.

**Severity 1–4** — Creating or increasing the Scar sets Fade to 20 IC.

**Severity 5** — Reaching Severity 5, or applying more of the same Scar while it is already 5, sets Fade to 10 IC.

When Fade reaches 0, reduce the Scar's Severity by 1\. If any Severity remains, set Fade to 10 IC. Continue reducing it by 1 every 10 IC until the Scar reaches 0\.

Increasing a Scar refreshes Fade from its new Severity. Consuming Scar Severity leaves the current Fade timer running.

When a Scar reaches 0, remove its marker. Physical aftermath can remain after the chakra sustaining the Scar is gone: shattered glass, soot, standing water and scorched earth still look exactly as ruined as they should.

## **Anchored Scars**

A Feral Jutsu with Upkeep Anchors the matching Scars it creates on its target or inside its Area while that Upkeep is maintained. An Anchored Scar keeps its current Severity and its Fade timer stops counting down.

Several native Feral Jutsu Anchor a wider set of matching Scars in their Area. Their text states exactly what they hold.

When an Anchor ends, set the Scar's Fade to 20 IC at Severity 1–4 or 10 IC at Severity 5\. The battlefield immediately returns to the normal Fade cycle.

## **Areas, Creatures and Overlap**

A creature can carry a Scar directly while standing inside an Area carrying the same Scar. When a rule checks that creature's Scar Severity, use the higher value.

Overlapping Areas of the same Scar use the highest Severity present in the overlap. Reapplying a Scar to substantially the same Area increases the existing marker.

Each point of Scar Severity can be consumed once. Declare the creature, Area or effect supplying the Scar before resolving the benefit.

## **Timing**

Resolve a Feral Jutsu's Attack, Damage, Status Conditions and immediate effects, then apply its new Scar. Bonuses use the Scar Severity already present before that Jutsu began resolving.

A Jutsu that changes this order states the timing in its own Effects.

## **Shared Scars**

Scars belong to the battlefield. Any character with rules for the matching Feral Element can build, consume or exploit them.

# **FERAL COMBAT FLOW**

Feral Elements reward momentum rather than permanent setup. A fight naturally moves through the same six beats.

**Establish** — Land the first Scar or place a Feral Field.

**Build** — Reapply the Scar before Fade runs out and push it into Severity 3–4.

**Overrun** — Reach Severity 5 and unlock the Release's strongest state.

**Exploit** — Use an empowered Attack, Arc, Shatter, pull, ignition or Null payoff while the Scar is at its peak.

**Collapse** — Spend the Scar or let Fade pull it back down.

**Rebuild** — Move the fight, establish a new field, or start the same cycle again.

Feral Fields are the main way to hold territory. Their Upkeep Anchors a Scar and gives the Release time to reach Overrun. Ending the field sends those Scars back into Fade.

# **ABYSS RELEASE**

***Suiton → Abyss | Scar: Depth***

*Normal water moves around you. Abyssal water feels impossibly heavy, as though a few inches of black water carry the pressure of an ocean trench.*

## **Feral Conversion**

A Feralized Suiton Attack that affects an Area applies Depth equal to its Feral Intensity to that Area after resolving. A single-target Attack applies that Depth directly to the target. A persistent Suiton effect that creates or maintains water applies its Depth to the water it sustains.

## **Depth**

### **Scarred — Depth 1–2**

A creature affected by Depth suffers Special Upkeep equal to its Depth and has its Movement Speed reduced by 10% per Depth.

Abyss Attacks gain \+1 Accuracy against a creature affected by Depth 2+.

### **Feral — Depth 3–4**

A breathing creature affected by Depth 3+ gains Suffocation 1 every 15 IC.

At Depth 3, Abyss Attacks against the creature gain \+10% Final Damage and \+10% Wounding. At Depth 4, both bonuses become \+15%.

### **Overrun — Depth 5**

At Depth 5, the creature's Movement Speed is reduced by 50% and it gains Suffocation 1 every 10 IC. Abyss Attacks against it gain \+25% Final Damage, \+25% Wounding and \+2 Accuracy.

## **Apex Mutation — Hadal Adaptation**

Depth leaves you untouched. You ignore its Special Upkeep, Movement reduction and Suffocation. Whenever one of your Abyss Attacks inflicts a Wound on a creature at Depth 5, that creature gains Suffocation 1\.

# **STORM RELEASE**

***Raiton → Storm | Scar: Charge***

*Ordinary Raiton produces lightning. Storm Release teaches that lightning where to go next. Hair rises, metal hums and every marked creature becomes another piece of the circuit.*

## **Feral Conversion**

A Feralized Raiton Attack creates Charge after resolving. A single-target Attack applies Charge equal to its Feral Intensity to the target. An Attack affecting several creatures or an Area either applies the full Intensity to one affected creature, object or point, or applies Charge 1 to a number of affected creatures, objects or points up to its Feral Intensity.

## **Storm Nodes and Arcs**

A creature, object or point carrying Charge is a Storm Node.

When declaring a Storm Jutsu, choose a Storm Node within that Jutsu's normal Range to act as its origin. Measure Range and Area from that Node. After the Jutsu resolves, reduce that Node's Charge by 1\.

When a Storm Attack hits a creature carrying Charge, consume 1 Charge from that creature to Arc. Choose another creature carrying Charge within 10 yards and make an additional Attack Roll at \-3 Accuracy. On hit, the Arc deals 50% of the original Attack's Final Damage. The Arc carries no Status Conditions, Wound riders or additional Charge. One Attack creates one Arc.

## **Charge**

### **Scarred — Charge 1–2**

The target is a Storm Node. Storm Attacks gain \+1 Accuracy against a creature at Charge 2+.

### **Feral — Charge 3–4**

At Charge 3, Storm Attacks against the creature gain \+10% Final Damage and their first Arc deals 65% of the original Final Damage.

At Charge 4, the Final Damage bonus becomes \+15%. The creature suffers \-2 to Defensive Rolls against Arcs, and an Arc that hits it inflicts Paralysis 1\.

### **Overrun — Charge 5**

Storm Attacks against a creature at Charge 5 gain \+25% Final Damage and \+2 Accuracy.

When a Storm Attack hits a creature at Charge 5, consume any amount of its Charge. Each Charge consumed adds \+5% Final Damage and \+1 Paralysis to that Attack. Consuming all 5 Charge also creates an Arc against each other Storm Node within 10 yards, to a maximum of five Nodes. These Arcs use \-3 Accuracy and deal 50% of the original Attack's Final Damage.

## **Apex Mutation — Chainstorm**

Once per Action, originating a Storm Jutsu from a Storm Node leaves that Node's Charge intact. After your first Arc hits, consume 1 Charge from that target to Arc again to a different Charged creature within 10 yards. The second Arc uses \-3 Accuracy, deals 35% of the original Attack's Final Damage and ends the chain.

## **Native Feral Jutsu**

# **GLASS RELEASE**

***Doton → Glass | Scar: Shards***

*Doton users build things to last. Glass Release builds them to break. Earth flashes into brilliant blades and structures whose destruction is part of the technique.*

## **Feral Conversion**

Damaging Feralized Doton Jutsu inflict Slashing Wounds. A damaging Glass Jutsu affecting an Area applies Shards equal to its Feral Intensity to that Area. A single-target Glass Attack creates an Area 1 of Shards beneath the target.

A Glass Barrier or Construct applies Shards equal to its Feral Intensity across its occupied Area when it ends or is destroyed.

## **Shards**

### **Scarred — Shards 1–2**

An Area containing Shards counts as Rocky Terrain equal to its Shards Severity and provides suitable earth for Glass and Doton Jutsu.

When declaring a Glass Jutsu whose origin or affected Area overlaps Shards, consume Shards up to that Jutsu's Feral Intensity. Each Shard consumed reduces final Chakra cost by 1 and final Seal Speed by 1, minimum 0\.

### **Feral — Shards 3–4**

At Shards 3+, Glass Attacks originating inside or targeting the Area gain \+1 Accuracy and \+10% Wounding. Glass Barriers and Constructs created there gain \+10 DR per Shards Severity present when created.

At Shards 4, Glass Attacks also gain \+10% Final Damage.

### **Overrun — Shards 5**

Glass Attacks against creatures inside a Shards 5 Area gain \+20% Final Damage and \+2 Accuracy.

Once per Action when a Glass Attack hits a creature inside the Area, consume any number of Shards from that Area. Each Shard consumed deals an additional 4d10 Slashing Damage. If all 5 Shards are consumed at once, combine that bonus Damage with the Attack's Final Damage for Wounding and add \+25% Wounding.

## **Apex Mutation — Perfect Fracture**

Shards never impede your movement. Each Shard consumed for Glass Release's Chakra cost and Seal Speed reduction reduces both by 2 instead of 1\. Consuming 5 Shards in a single Action adds \+10% Wounding to that Action.

# **VACUUM RELEASE**

***Fūton → Vacuum | Scar: Dead Air***

*Fūton controls air. Vacuum Release controls the space left when air is gone. Its techniques create pressure differentials, silence and sudden implosions.*

## **Feral Conversion**

A Feralized Fūton Jutsu can reverse any Knockback it inflicts, pulling the target towards the technique's origin. A Vacuum Jutsu affecting an Area applies Dead Air equal to its Feral Intensity after resolving. A single-target Vacuum Jutsu creates an Area 1 of Dead Air around the target.

## **Dead Air**

### **Scarred — Dead Air 1–2**

Katon Attacks originating inside or passing through Dead Air lose 5% Final Damage per Dead Air. Projectile Attacks passing through it suffer \-1 Accuracy per Dead Air.

Vacuum Attacks gain \+1 Accuracy against creatures inside Dead Air 2+.

### **Feral — Dead Air 3–4**

At Dead Air 3+, sound stops travelling into, out of or through the Area. Breathing creatures inside gain Suffocation 1 every 15 IC.

At Dead Air 3, Vacuum Attacks against creatures inside gain \+10% Final Damage and \+2 yards of forced movement. At Dead Air 4, the Final Damage bonus becomes \+15%, forced movement becomes \+4 yards, and Blocking a Vacuum Attack uses half the defender's normal Block Percentage.

### **Overrun — Dead Air 5**

Katon Jutsu cannot originate inside Dead Air 5\. Breathing creatures inside gain Suffocation 1 every 10 IC.

Vacuum Attacks against creatures inside gain \+25% Final Damage, \+2 Accuracy and ignore 50% DR. Their forced movement increases by 5 yards. Pulling a creature into the exact centre of the Overrun Area applies Stun 5 after movement resolves.

## **Apex Mutation — Breathless**

Dead Air carries no penalty for you: you breathe, hear, speak and fire projectiles through it normally, and your Katon remains usable. Forced movement caused by your Vacuum Jutsu inside Dead Air 3+ increases by an additional 2 yards.

# **CINDER RELEASE**

***Katon → Cinder | Scar: Ash***

*Katon is about the flame. Cinder Release is about what survives it. Soot, embers and half-dead fire turn the battlefield into fuel for the next ignition.*

## **Feral Conversion**

After a damaging Feralized Katon Jutsu resolves, apply Ash equal to its Feral Intensity. An AoE applies that Ash to its Area. A single-target Attack can apply the Ash directly to the creature.

When a Cinder Jutsu affects a creature or Area carrying Ash, consume Ash from one matching source up to the Jutsu's Feral Intensity. Each Ash consumed adds \+5% Final Damage and \+3 Ignite. A Jutsu with no printed Ignite gains Ignite equal to 3 × Ash consumed.

## **Ash**

### **Scarred — Ash 1–2**

An Area containing Ash imposes Visibility equal to half its Ash Severity, rounded up. Ash carried directly by a creature moves with them.

Cinder Attacks gain \+1 Accuracy against a creature affected by Ash 2+.

### **Feral — Ash 3–4**

At Ash 3+, effects that reduce Ignite remove half as much Ignite, rounded down. Cinder Attacks against the creature gain \+10% Final Damage and \+1 Accuracy.

Whenever a creature affected by Ash 3+ takes Ignite Damage, it takes additional Damage equal to NDB × its Ash Severity. This additional Damage does not Wound. At Ash 4, the Cinder Final Damage bonus becomes \+15%.

### **Overrun — Ash 5**

At Ash 5, the creature's Ignite holds its Severity instead of naturally reducing. Cinder Attacks against it gain \+25% Final Damage and \+2 Accuracy.

When a Cinder Attack hits a creature at Ash 5, consume all 5 Ash to add another \+25% Final Damage and immediately resolve its current Ignite once after the Attack without reducing that Ignite.

## **Apex Mutation — The Last Ember**

Ash never interferes with your vision. Each Ash you consume adds \+4 Ignite instead of \+3. Consuming 5 Ash from a single creature or Area leaves Ash 1 on that source after the Action resolves.

# **VOID RELEASE**

***Non-Elemental → Void | Scar: Null***

*Void is the sixth Feral Element only by convenience. It has no nature of its own. It is raw chakra trained to make other chakra lose coherence.*

## **Feral Conversion**

Feralized Non-Elemental Ninjutsu become Void Release. Medical Ninjutsu, Genjutsu and Sealing Ninjutsu remain their own disciplines.

A single-target Void Jutsu applies Null equal to its Feral Intensity directly to the target. An AoE Void Jutsu applies that Null to its Area. A Void Jutsu that directly interacts with an active Ninjutsu effect can place its Null on that effect instead.

A creature standing in an Area of Null uses that Area's Severity for creature-based Null effects. Null placed directly on a maintained effect modifies that effect's Upkeep.

## **Null**

### **Scarred — Null 1–2**

A creature affected by Null has the final Chakra cost of its Jutsu increased by 1 per 2 Null, rounded up.

Null placed directly on a maintained Ninjutsu effect increases that effect's Upkeep by 1 per 2 Null, rounded up. Void Attacks gain \+1 Accuracy against a creature at Null 2+.

### **Feral — Null 3–4**

At Null 3+, the creature suffers \-2 to Chakra Exhaustion Rolls and Void Attacks against it gain \+10% Final Damage.

At Null 4, the Void Final Damage bonus becomes \+15%, and numerical DR or Block Percentage granted by Chakra-based techniques or abilities is reduced by 25%.

### **Overrun — Null 5**

At Null 5, Chakra cost and Upkeep reductions stop at their printed values. Void Attacks against the creature gain \+25% Final Damage and \+2 Accuracy.

Once per Action when a Void Attack hits a creature at Null 5, consume all 5 Null. After the Attack resolves, end one Chakra-based effect with Upkeep currently benefiting that creature. S-Rank effects remain stable against this collapse.

## **Apex Mutation — Absolute Null**

Your Void Attacks ignore 25% of DR granted by Chakra-based effects, increasing to 50% against a creature at Null 3+. Consuming Null 5 to end an Upkeep leaves Null 1 on the creature after the Action resolves.

***Chakra 50 | Damage 42d10 | Range 40 | Area 20 | Speed 24 | Seal Speed 28***

***Tags: Attack, AoE, Energy, Non-Elemental, Void***

Blackout deals \+50% Damage to Chakra-created Barriers and Constructs.

Increase Blackout against creatures based on Null already affecting them: Null 1–2 grants \+10% Final Damage; Null 3 grants \+20% Final Damage; Null 4 grants \+30% Final Damage and 25% Chakra-based DR penetration; Null 5 grants \+40% Final Damage and 50% Chakra-based DR penetration.

After Blackout resolves, choose one creature it hit at Null 5\. Consume 5 Null from that creature or the Area supplying its Null, then end one Chakra-based Upkeep benefiting that creature. A single Blackout makes this choice once. The Area then gains Null 1\.

Choose up to three active Ninjutsu effects, Barriers or Constructs affected by Blackout and apply Null 3 directly to each. An effect with Upkeep increases that Upkeep from the applied Null.

# **DEVELOPMENTS**

Each time you Deepen Feral Element, choose one Development.

**Feral Armament** — When making an Attack with a weapon created or empowered by a Jutsu that qualifies for your Feral Conversion, pay 3 additional Stamina to apply 1 matching Scar to the target or an Area 1 beneath them on hit. One Attack Action creates one Scar this way.

**Lesser Mutation** — D-Rank Jutsu can be Feralized at Feral Intensity 1\.

**Deep Scarring — Increase the initial Scar applied when one of your Feral Jutsu resolves by 1, to the normal maximum of 5\. Recurring increases from an Upkeep use the amount printed in that Jutsu.**

**Predatory Efficiency — The additional Chakra cost for Feralizing a converted Jutsu becomes 1 × Feral Intensity.**

**Long Memory — When one of your Feral Jutsu creates or increases a Scar at Severity 1–4, set that Scar's Fade to 30 IC instead of 20\. Severity 5 uses Fade 10\.**

**Territorial Instinct** — When you declare a Feral Attack that interacts with a matching Scar at Severity 3+, gain \+1 Accuracy on that Attack.

## **Mastery — Feral Dominion**

After three Feral Element Developments, gain Feral Dominion and your chosen Release's Apex Mutation.

When declaring a Feral Jutsu that targets, occupies, originates from or consumes a matching Scar at Severity 3+, reduce its final Seal Speed by 2, minimum 0\.

The first converted Jutsu you Feralize during each Action has its additional Chakra cost from Feralizing reduced by 2, minimum 0\.

# **RULES REFERENCE**

## **Scar Cycle**

**Create** — Apply Severity and set Fade: 20 IC at Severity 1–4, 10 IC at Severity 5\.

**Increase** — Raise Severity and refresh Fade from the new Severity.

**Anchor** — Freeze Severity and pause Fade while the sustaining Upkeep remains.

**Consume** — Spend Severity for an effect; the current Fade timer keeps running.

**Fade** — At Fade 0, lose 1 Severity and set Fade to 10 IC. Repeat until the Scar reaches 0\.

## **Resolution Order**

* Declare the Jutsu and Feralize it if applicable.  
* Calculate the Jutsu's normal costs, then add its additional Chakra cost for being Feralized.  
* Check Scars already present for Accuracy, Damage, Wounding and other bonuses.  
* Resolve the Attack, defenses, Damage, Status Conditions and immediate effects.  
* Resolve any Scar consumption or cash-out written into the Jutsu.  
* Apply the Jutsu's new Scar and set or refresh Fade.

## **Scar Checks**

For a creature standing in a matching Scar Area while also carrying that Scar directly, use the higher Severity.

When consuming Scar, remove Severity from the creature, Area or effect named by the rule. A single point of Severity pays for one benefit.

## **Anchors**

A Feral Jutsu with Upkeep Anchors the matching Scars it creates. Native Feral Fields also state which existing Scars they Anchor.

When an Anchor ends, the Scar returns to Fade at 20 IC for Severity 1–4 or 10 IC for Severity 5\.

## **Multiple Feral Users**

Matching Scars are shared terrain. Abyss users can build the same Depth, Storm users can share Nodes, Glass users can consume the same Shards, and every other Release follows the same rule.

## **End of a Scene**

Combat timing ends when the scene ends. Any unspent Feral Scar loses its mechanical effects at that point. The physical aftermath remains as the fiction demands.
