## Class Benefits
{:#class-benefits}

Rank tells people how far you've come.

Class tells them what they trust you to do when things go wrong.

Most Hunters settle naturally into a role over time, shaped by their talents, temperament and the kinds of jobs they keep surviving.

To officially join a hunter class, you must complete a number of tasks, by filling in a class sheet, that can be found [ HERE ] once you qualify, and officially join the ranks of your chosen class, you gain it’s benefits as listed below.

## Hunter Classes
{:#hunter-classes}

#### Pathfinder
{:#pathfinder}

Scouts, trackers and navigators. Pathfinders read broken branches, chakra residue, weather and old roads like other people read books. If something needs finding, following, or reaching alive, you send a Pathfinder.

##### Always a Way
{:#always-a-way}

You have learned that getting lost is usually just another way of saying you haven't looked hard enough yet.

You gain +3 Survival and Awareness when tracking, navigating, locating something, or identifying environmental hazards.

Additionally, once per Hunt or Mission, after you or an ally fails a roll involving navigation, tracking, searching, terrain or environmental danger, you may declare that you noticed something they missed. Reroll the check with +5.

If successful, you may also ask the GM for one useful piece of environmental information relevant to the situation: a safer route, an escape path, nearby shelter, signs of passage, unstable terrain, a likely hiding place, or something similar.

#### Slayer
{:#slayer}

The monster killers. Slayers specialise in putting dangerous things into the ground quickly and permanently. Heavy hitters, duelists and shinobi whose first answer to a problem is usually violence.

##### Bring it Down
{:#bring-it-down}

You have stopped thinking about monsters as enormous creatures. They're collections of things that can break.

Whenever you deal enough damage to potentially cause a Wound, treat your damage as +25% higher for determining whether a Wound occurs.

#### Anchor
{:#anchor}

Protectors and defenders. Anchors hold lines, guard caravans, protect settlements and make sure the rest of the team gets home. When everyone else runs, an Anchor is usually the one standing in the way.

##### Nobody Gets Through
{:#nobody-gets-through}

When you're standing between danger and somebody else, moving you becomes the difficult part.

Whenever an enemy attacks an ally within 3 yards of you, you may spend 1 Willpower to Intercept without paying the normal additional cost or penalty associated with doing so, causing the attack to be targeting you, and nobody else..

Additionally, when you successfully defend against an attack or Intercept one for another character, you may Hold the Line until after your next Action. While holding the line, you gain RES/2 DR, and +5 to all defensive rolls.

#### Trapper
{:#trapper}

Hunters who win before the fight begins. Seals, snares, explosives, poisons, ambushes and prepared terrain. A good Trapper turns an impossible Hunt into a fair one.

You don't fight fair. You fight prepared.

Whenever you successfully complete Prepare during a Hunt, you create one additional Preparation.

Additionally, once per battle you may reveal a reasonable piece of equipment, environmental preparation, seal, trap, marked position or precaution that you could have established beforehand.

Declare its purpose when revealed. It may provide one of the following:
- +5 to one relevant Skill Roll.
- +3 Accuracy to one attack exploiting the preparation
- Inflict Immobilization 5 on a target that enters or triggers it.
- Reduce an environmental or enemy-created numerical penalty by 5 for yourself and nearby allies.

The preparation must make narrative sense and cannot retroactively create rare, unique or prohibitively expensive equipment.

#### Scavenger
{:#scavenger}

Salvagers, harvesters and survivalists. Scavengers know what is worth carrying home, how to strip a beast clean, and how to turn ruins into food, tools, medicine and money.

Other Hunters see wreckage.You see tomorrow's supplies.

Whenever you complete a Hunt, Mission, or meaningful expedition from which salvage could reasonably be recovered, you may identify one additional useful Salvage or Trophy component beyond what would normally be available.

Additionally, once per Week's Rest, you may convert useful junk, monster material or spare equipment into a Field Supply.

A Field Supply may be expended at any time to produce one ordinary consumable item or piece of equipment worth up to an amount determined by the crafting/equipment rules, provided it is something you could reasonably have assembled from your supplies.

During a Hunt, you may instead expend a Field Supply to gain +5 to one Prepare, Crafting, Survival or Medicine roll by improvising exactly what the team needs

#### Chirurgeon
{:#chirurgeon}

Medics, anatomists and beast scholars. Chirurgeons keep Hunters alive, study what they're fighting and know exactly which part of a creature stops working when you hit it hard enough.

Hunters have a very different definition of stable than most doctors.

Your Medicine checks made to treat Wounds or stabilize injured characters gain +3.

Additionally, once per battle when you reach and treat an ally who is Severely Wounded, Critically Wounded or at 0 HP, you may perform emergency field treatment.

That character immediately:
- Regains enough HP to reach 1 HP if below it.
- Removes one non-permanent physical Status Effect caused by injury.
- Ignores the penalties of one Wound until the end of the battle. The Wound itself is not removed.

If used on yourself, this requires 1 Willpower.

#### Hound
{:#hound}

Pursuit specialists. Fast, relentless and difficult to escape. Hounds chase fleeing beasts, missing people, raiders and anything else unfortunate enough to leave a trail.

Running only makes the trail clearer.

When pursuing a target you can see, track, smell, sense, or otherwise reliably follow, increase your Movement Speed by 50%.

You also gain +3 Survival and Awareness specifically to locate or pursue creatures you have previously encountered.

#### Breaker
{:#breaker}

The answer to things that refuse to die. Breakers specialise in armour, fortifications, giant beasts and overwhelming obstacles. When somebody says "we can't get through that," a Breaker takes it personally

Armor, stone, bone, chakra constructs.They're all just materials with limits.

Your attacks ignore 2 points of Damage Reduction and treat defensive objects, armor, barriers, structures and similar targets as though their effective durability were 25% lower.

Additionally, once per battle before dealing damage, you may declare Crack It Open. For that attack:
- Ignore 50% of the target's Damage Reduction.
- Damage is treated as +50% higher against barriers, armor, objects and structures.
- If the attack successfully Wounds a creature, you may decide it’s location
