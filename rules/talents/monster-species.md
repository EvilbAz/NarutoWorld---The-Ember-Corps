# Jackal Species
{:#jackal-species}

Role: Pursuit / Tracking

Jackals are relentless pursuit hunters. They excel at tracking, repositioning around wounded prey, and turning a retreat into a bad decision.

## Profile
{:#profile}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 |

Basic Attack: Speed 8 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: -1

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-2}

### Blood-Scented Pursuit
{:#blood-scented-pursuit}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Jackal can establish and follow a physical scent trail from blood, fur, clothing, carcasses, campsites and similar evidence where one plausibly remains. Its Basic Attack gains +1 Accuracy against Bleeding creatures or creatures that have suffered a Wound this scene.

### Cut the Line
{:#cut-the-line}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: When an enemy voluntarily moves farther away from the Jackal, it may move up to 2 yards after that Action resolves without changing Initiative. This can occur once between the Jackal's Actions.

### Hamstring Bite
{:#hamstring-bite}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: When the Jackal's Basic Attack fully hits a creature that moved at least 5 yards since its last Action, reduce all movement that creature makes before its next Action by 2 yards, minimum 0.

### Relentless Chase
{:#relentless-chase}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: Increase the Jackal's Movement by 50% while it moves toward a Wounded or Bleeding creature. It ignores mundane Difficult Terrain during that movement.

### Pack Execution
{:#pack-execution}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Jackal's Basic Attacks against Wounded or Bleeding creatures gain +2 Accuracy and deal +25% Final Damage.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-2}

### Longroad Legs
{:#longroad-legs}

Type: Stackable

Increase the monster's Movement by 0.1 yard per IC.

### Harrier
{:#harrier}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% against a target that has suffered a Wound or moved at least 5 yards since its last Action.

### Carrion Nose
{:#carrion-nose}

Type: Utility

Blood-Scented Pursuit becomes exceptionally precise. When the Jackal meaningfully helps a Track or investigation using scent, grant +5 to the relevant Survival or Research roll, and on success the Jackal can distinguish the freshest plausible trail from older overlapping trails.

### Scavenger's Eye
{:#scavenger-s-eye}

Type: Utility

Outside Initiative, the monster can quickly identify whether mundane meat, hides, tracks, remains, discarded equipment or disturbed ground are fresh, spoiled, recently moved or deliberately tampered with. It never creates additional salvage.

### Cut Them Off
{:#cut-them-off}

Type: One-Time

Cut the Line may move up to 3 yards instead of 2, and the Jackal gains +2 Defence against movement-based reactions during that movement.

### Blood in the Air
{:#blood-in-the-air}

Type: One-Time

Increase Blood-Scented Pursuit's Accuracy bonus against Bleeding or Wounded creatures by +1. Pack Execution therefore grants a total of +4 Accuracy against those targets.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques}

### Carrion Nose
{:#carrion-nose-2}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 4

Effects: Choose a creature, trail, body or disturbed site the monster can physically examine. It makes a focused scent search; gain +5 to one immediately related Survival, Awareness or Research roll and, on success, learn the freshest plausible direction of travel.

### Harrier's Cut
{:#harrier-s-cut}

Speed: 8

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Make a Basic Attack. If the target moved at least 5 yards since its last Action, increase Final Damage by 20% and reduce any movement it makes before its next Action by 2 yards, minimum 0.

### Last-Mile Pack
{:#last-mile-pack}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 9

Range: 15 yards

Effects: The Jackal darts around one target, snapping from changing angles. Make one Attack using the monster's Accuracy for 80% Monster Damage. On a hit, end the target's current voluntary movement and knock it Prone if it moved at least 5 yards during that Action.

### Run to Ground
{:#run-to-ground}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: The monster moves up to twice its normal Movement for this Action and makes an Attack for 130% Monster Damage. Against a Wounded or Bleeding target, gain +2 Accuracy and inflict Knockback 3 on a full hit.

## Temperament
{:#temperament}

Jackals are driven by pursuit, scent and useful scavenging. They respond well to clear quarry and active work, but become difficult when repeatedly forced to remain idle as disposable guards or made to destroy food, salvage or a trail without purpose.

# Lynx Species
{:#lynx-species}

Role: Reaction / Counterattack

Lynxes are reactive hunters built around speed, awareness and sudden retaliation. They are at their best when an enemy commits first.

## Profile
{:#profile-2}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 3 | 2 | 5 |

Basic Attack: Speed 7 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Slashing

Command AP Modifier: -2

Movement: Ground; climbs ordinary surfaces at half Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-3}

### Pre-Impact Read
{:#pre-impact-read}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Lynx gains +1 Defence against the first Attack declared against it after each of its Actions.

### Counterstep
{:#counterstep}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: After successfully Dodging or Parrying an Attack, the Lynx may move 1 yard without changing Initiative. This can occur once between its Actions.

### Capacitor Coat
{:#capacitor-coat}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: After successfully Dodging or Parrying a Melee Attack, the Lynx becomes Charged. Its next Basic Attack before the end of its next Action deals +15% Final Damage, then Charged ends.

### Static Grounding
{:#static-grounding}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: The Lynx gains +2 Defence against Lightning and electrical Energy Attacks and reduces Paralysis inflicted by those effects by 1, minimum 0.

### Perfect Counter
{:#perfect-counter}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Trigger: The Lynx successfully Dodges or Parries an Attack from a creature within its melee reach.

Effects: Make an immediate Attack against that creature for 80% Monster Damage.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-3}

### Static Claws
{:#static-claws}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% against a target that used a Lightning or Energy effect since its previous Action.

### Needle Ears
{:#needle-ears}

Type: Stackable

Gain +1 on Awareness checks made through sound, vibration or electrical disturbance.

### Storm Listener
{:#storm-listener}

Type: Utility

Rain, fog and darkness do not penalize the monster's Awareness when the thing being detected could reasonably be heard. It can distinguish natural thunder from nearby chakra-driven electrical discharge.

### High Perch
{:#high-perch}

Type: Utility

The monster can climb ordinary vertical surfaces at its normal Movement and can safely leap gaps up to twice its normal Movement without a check when a plausible landing exists.

### Capacitor Fur
{:#capacitor-fur}

Type: One-Time

Capacitor Coat's Charged bonus becomes +30% Final Damage instead of +15%.

### Pre-Impact Read
{:#pre-impact-read-2}

Type: One-Time

Pre-Impact Read grants +2 Defence against the first Attack declared against the Lynx after each of its Actions instead of +1.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-2}

### Thunder-Ear
{:#thunder-ear}

Speed: 7

Base Command AP: 4

Range: 20 yards

Effects: The monster listens for movement, breathing, speech, impact or electrical disturbance. Make an Awareness check with +5; on success it may immediately Point Out one Hidden creature it could plausibly hear.

### Forked Pounce
{:#forked-pounce}

Speed: 8

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Move up to 5 yards, then Attack for 100% Monster Damage. On a hit, arc static to a different creature within 5 yards and make a second Attack at -2 Accuracy for 60% Monster Damage.

### Grounding Guard
{:#grounding-guard}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Range: 2 yards

Effects: Trigger: the monster or an ally is hit by an electrical Energy effect. Reduce that effect's Final Damage by 20% and reduce Paralysis inflicted by 1, minimum 0.

### Thunderclaw
{:#thunderclaw}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 11

Effects: Make an Attack for 125% Monster Damage. On a full hit, inflict Paralysis 3 for 10 IC. If the monster is Charged, increase the damage to 150% and Paralysis to 4, then Charged ends.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-2}

Lynxes are watchful, reactive hunters. They respond best to patient handling and room to choose their moment. Repeatedly using one as deliberate bait, or ignoring clear warning behaviour and forcing it back into obvious danger, can trigger Rebellion.

# Boar Species
{:#boar-species}

Role: Breach / Frontline

Boars are creatures of commitment: heavy, direct and practical. They break formations, clear roads and turn their bodies into moving obstacles.

## Profile
{:#profile-3}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 |

Basic Attack: Speed 10 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: +1

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-4}

### Driving Mass
{:#driving-mass}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: Reduce Knockback suffered by the Boar by 2, minimum 0. It gains +2 against effects that would forcibly reposition it.

### Headlong Charge
{:#headlong-charge}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: If the Boar moved at least 3 yards in a straight line immediately before its Basic Attack, that Attack deals +15% Final Damage. On a full hit, also inflict Knockback 2.

### Trample Through
{:#trample-through}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: Once per Move Action, the Boar may attempt to pass through the space of one human-sized hostile creature. Contest the Boar's Accuracy against that creature's Defence. On success, move the creature 1 yard to a legal space and continue moving. On failure, the Boar's movement ends before entering that space.

### Living Wallback
{:#living-wallback}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: Adjacent allies may treat the Boar as Cover 1. The Boar may Take the Hit for an ally within 2 yards at Speed 5 when it can physically interpose itself.

### Battering Ram
{:#battering-ram}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Boar counts as one Size larger for carrying and forced movement. Its Attacks deal +50% Final Damage against unattended objects, Cover and mundane structures. A full hit after Headlong Charge inflicts Knockback 4 and Prone instead of Knockback 2.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-4}

### Ironhide
{:#ironhide}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Driving Mass
{:#driving-mass-2}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, increase Knockback or forced movement caused by Boar Monster Techniques by 1 yard.

### Root Work
{:#root-work}

Type: Utility

Outside Initiative, halve the time needed to clear brambles, loose rubble, shallow earth, mud or similar mundane obstructions when the monster can simply dig or shove through them.

### Hauler
{:#hauler}

Type: Utility

The monster can carry or drag one additional human-sized willing or unconscious creature, or an equivalent mundane load, without slowing ordinary overland travel.

### Broad Back
{:#broad-back}

Type: One-Time

Living Wallback provides Cover 2 instead of Cover 1.

### Planted Feet
{:#planted-feet}

Type: One-Time

Driving Mass reduces Knockback by 3 instead of 2, and its bonus against forced repositioning becomes +3.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-3}

### Boar Road
{:#boar-road}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 7

Line: 8 yards

Effects: The monster charges along the Line and makes an Attack against each creature in it for 80% Monster Damage. On a hit, inflict Knockback 3.

### Furrow the Earth
{:#furrow-the-earth}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Create a 3-yard-wide path up to 12 yards long through loose earth, rubble or brush. The path becomes Rocky Terrain 3 for 20 IC; shallow traps, wires or caches may be exposed with an appropriate check.

### Wallback
{:#wallback}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 7

Range: 2 yards

Effects: Trigger: an ally is hit by an Attack the monster could physically intercept. Move up to 2 yards, become the target, and defend normally. If it cannot interpose itself, this cannot be used.

### Breakthrough Charge
{:#breakthrough-charge}

Speed: 15

Base Command AP: 12

Line: 12 yards

Effects: Move along the Line and Attack each creature for 120% Monster Damage. On a hit inflict Knockback 6; on a full hit also inflict Stun 4. Unattended mundane obstacles take +50% Final Damage.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-3}

Boars are stubborn working beasts that respond well to direct routes, obvious obstacles and committed movement. Pointless destruction or repeatedly driving one into the same obviously suicidal charge can trigger Rebellion.

# Mantis Species
{:#mantis-species}

Role: Precision / Counter

Mantises are precision predators. They favor stillness, reach, clean counters and attacks that punish exposed limbs or careless approaches.

## Profile
{:#profile-4}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 4 | 3 | 3 |

Basic Attack: Speed 9 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Slashing

Command AP Modifier: +0

Movement: Ground; climbs rough natural surfaces at half Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-5}

### Predator's Reach
{:#predator-s-reach}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: Increase the reach of the Mantis's Basic Attack by 1 yard.

### Hooking Guard
{:#hooking-guard}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Mantis gains +1 Parry against Melee Attacks. After successfully Parrying a Melee Attack, it may move the attacker 1 yard to a legal adjacent space.

### Patient Killer
{:#patient-killer}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: If the Mantis has not voluntarily moved since its previous Action, its next Basic Attack deals +15% Final Damage.

### Scything Reflex
{:#scything-reflex}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Trigger: The Mantis successfully Parries a Melee Attack.

Effects: Make an immediate Attack against that attacker for 60% Monster Damage.

### Killing Zone
{:#killing-zone}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Trigger: A hostile creature voluntarily enters or leaves the Mantis's melee reach.

Effects: Make an Attack against that creature for 80% Monster Damage. This can occur once between the Mantis's Actions and is an Independent Action.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-5}

### Razor Forelimbs
{:#razor-forelimbs}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, gain +1 Accuracy with the Basic Attack against a creature that attacked the monster since its previous Action.

### Patient Killer
{:#patient-killer-2}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the next Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% if the monster has not voluntarily moved since its previous Action.

### Still as Grass
{:#still-as-grass}

Type: Utility

When the monster remains motionless and has suitable cover or vegetation, grant +3 to an allied Stealth or ambush preparation check involving it.

### Fine Manipulators
{:#fine-manipulators}

Type: Utility

Despite its anatomy, the monster can carefully cut rope, webbing, cloth, vines and similar non-reinforced material without damaging a restrained creature or nearby object.

### Hooking Guard
{:#hooking-guard-2}

Type: One-Time

Hooking Guard grants +2 Parry against Melee Attacks instead of +1. The attacker may still be shifted 1 yard after a successful Parry.

### Long Reach
{:#long-reach}

Type: One-Time

Increase the reach of the Basic Attack and Mantis Monster Techniques by 1 additional yard. This stacks with Predator's Reach.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-4}

### Scything Counter
{:#scything-counter}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Trigger: the monster successfully Parries a Melee Attack. Make an immediate Attack against that attacker for 70% Monster Damage.

### Prayer-Still
{:#prayer-still}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 4

Effects: The monster becomes braced until its next Action. It gains +3 Defence, but loses the bonus immediately if it voluntarily moves.

### Severing Arc
{:#severing-arc}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 8

Effects: Make an Attack for 115% Monster Damage. On a full hit, choose: inflict Bleed 3, or reduce the target's Movement by 20% for 20 IC.

### Thousand-Cut Step
{:#thousand-cut-step}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Move up to 4 yards without provoking movement-based reactions, then make two Attacks against the same target at -2 Accuracy, each for 70% Monster Damage.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-4}

Mantises are still, precise predators. They tolerate lethal work, but react poorly to frantic handling, needless torment and commands that repeatedly force them to abandon a clean position for spectacle.

### Hazard Skin

Base Command AP: 8

Salamanders are hardy and unusually tolerant of hostile environments. They can be trained for hazardous work, but repeatedly exposing one to toxins, fire, corrosion or attrition purely to test what it can survive can trigger Rebellion.

### Mauling Hold

### Unmoving Guardian

### Mountain With Teeth
{:#mountain-with-teeth}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Bear counts as one Size larger for Grapples, carrying and forced movement. When it successfully initiates a Grapple, knock that target Prone. Its Basic Attacks against creatures it is Grappling deal +25% Final Damage.

### Heavy Paws

### Powerful Frame
{:#powerful-frame}

Gain +3 on physical checks to lift, drag, break or brace mundane objects.

### Trail Shelter
{:#trail-shelter}

Type: Utility

### Bodyguard

### Mauler

Base Command AP: 5

Area: 8 yards

Effects: Enemies that can hear the bear must make Resistance against Monster Save TN. On failure, they suffer -2 Accuracy against creatures other than the bear until the bear's next Action. This is a fear-based effect.

### Guardian Rush
{:#guardian-rush}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 8

Effects: Move up to 6 yards toward an ally, then either make a Basic Attack or become Cover 3 for that ally until the bear's next Action.

### Avalanche Maul

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Make an Attack for 140% Monster Damage. On a full hit, knock the target Prone and inflict Stun 4. Against a Grappled target, damage becomes 160% instead.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-6}

Bears form strong protective routines around familiar people. They readily hold a line or cover a retreat, but forcing one to abandon a helpless creature it has been trained to guard can trigger Rebellion.

### River Predator

## Temperament
{:#temperament-21}

Panthers value autonomy, patience and a clean hunt. They object to confinement, humiliating busywork, and pointless cruelty. A trained Panther may be aloof, but responds well to clear purpose, room to stalk, and handlers who do not waste its effort.

### Medical Training
{:#medical-training}

Medical Ninjutsu requires a level of Chakra control and anatomical knowledge that most Hunters never need. A trained medic is not simply someone who can restore HP; they are the person who can look at a body falling apart and know which failure matters first.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-8}

You may learn D- and C-Rank Medical Ninjutsu.

You also gain Triage:

[Speed 5, Utility, Touch] Triage — Examine a willing, helpless, or unconscious living creature. Learn its current HP category, Fatigue Level, and the severity and location of any physical Wounds you could reasonably diagnose. This does not automatically reveal curses, poisons, hidden seals, or supernatural conditions that require their own checks.

#### Developments
{:#developments-8}

Combat Medic — When one of your Medical Ninjutsu restores HP to an ally during battle, that ally may either remove 2 Stun or regain 1 AP, up to their normal maximum.

Anatomist — After successfully using Triage or a Medicine check to examine a living creature, gain +2 Accuracy on your next Attack against that creature before the end of the scene. This represents anatomical knowledge and does not apply when the creature's anatomy is completely alien to what you examined.

Chakra Surgeon — Gain access to B-Rank Medical Ninjutsu, subject to the normal requirements for B-Rank techniques other than the Medical Training restriction.

Emergency Medicine — Once per battle, when a creature within reach is reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, you may immediately perform a Speed 5 Interrupt to attempt whatever Medicine action the Core Rules use to stabilize that creature.

Field Pharmacy — During a Week's Rest, prepare two ordinary medical consumables you could normally create or purchase. These must be non-unique items permitted by the Equipment and Crafting rules.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-7}

Surgeon of the Corps — Gain access to A-Rank Medical Ninjutsu if you otherwise meet their normal requirements. In addition, once per battle when you successfully stabilize or restore HP to a character at 0 HP, they may immediately ignore the numerical penalties of one physical Wound until the end of their next Action. The Wound is not removed.

### Natural Chakra
{:#natural-chakra}

Natural Chakra is the chakra of the living world. A shinobi normally produces chakra from within; a Sage learns to draw balanced Natural Chakra from the world around them.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-9}

Choose a Sage Tradition from a creature, Summon Tribe or other valid Source capable of teaching Natural Chakra. Gain Sage Rank 1, a maximum Sage Chakra of 4, Gather Natural Chakra, Sage Mode, your Tradition's Signature Trait, and one Sage Art or Sage Kata you meet the requirements for.

#### Developments
{:#developments-9}

Natural Chakra uses Sage Arts and Sage Kata as its Developments. Each time you Deepen this Talent, increase your Sage Rank by 1 and learn one additional Sage Art or Sage Kata you qualify for; the selected Art or Kata is that Development. The Art or Kata granted by the Core Talent is not a Development. You may Deepen Natural Chakra up to four times, to a maximum Sage Rank of 5. Mastery is gained after the third Development as normal; the fourth Deepening remains available after Mastery and still costs a Talent Point.

Use the Natural Chakra & Sage Mode rules for Sage Chakra, Gather Natural Chakra, Sage Mode, Sage Traditions, Sage Arts and Sage Kata.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-8}

Perfect Communion — After three Natural Chakra Developments, reduce Gather Natural Chakra's base Speed by 1 and its minimum Speed by 1. Reduce Sage Mode's Upkeep by 1, minimum 0. The first time you enter Sage Mode in each battle, gain 1 Sage Chakra after Sage Mode resolves, up to your maximum.

### Exotic Venoms
{:#exotic-venoms}

Most poisoners work with substances a competent field medic has seen before. You have learned to find, preserve, identify, and weaponize things that should never have been put in a bottle.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-10}

You may acquire, harvest, craft, and use Exotic Venoms permitted by the Equipment and Crafting rules.

Once per Hunt, when a defeated or studied creature could reasonably provide a venom, toxin, poisonous organ, paralytic secretion, or similar component, you may identify one additional toxin reagent that can be recovered from it. This reagent is still subject to normal harvesting and crafting requirements.

You also gain Identify Toxin:

[Speed 5, Utility] Identify Toxin — Examine a poison, poisoned wound, venomous organ, or contaminated sample you can safely access. Make an appropriate Medicine, Survival, or Crafting check. On success, learn its broad effect, delivery method, and the kind of treatment or antidote that would normally oppose it.

#### Developments
{:#developments-10}

Toxicologist — Gain +5 Medicine on checks made specifically to identify, treat, or create antidotes for poisons and venoms you have encountered before.

Rapid Application — Actions used specifically to apply a poison to a weapon, tool, trap, or prepared delivery system have their final Speed reduced by 3, minimum 1.

Refined Dose — Poisons you personally prepare impose +2 to the relevant Resistance TN, if they use one. A creature cannot suffer this bonus from more than one of your doses at a time.

Stable Mixture — A poisoned weapon or delivery tool you prepared does not lose its applied dose merely because its first Attack misses, unless the item or poison rules explicitly say the dose is destroyed on use.

Field Harvester — Gain +3 on Hunt Prepare, Survival, or Crafting rolls made specifically to recover or preserve poisonous monster components. The additional toxin reagent from the Core Talent may be recovered even if another party member also possesses Exotic Venoms, but each character still needs a distinct recoverable component.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-9}

Signature Toxin — During a Week's Rest, you may designate one poison you personally create as your Signature Toxin. Until your next Week's Rest, its relevant Resistance TN increases by an additional +1 and any numerical duration it has increases by 20%, rounding down. You may only maintain one Signature Toxin formula at a time.

### Seal Training

Suppression Specialist — Gain +2 Accuracy on Sealing Ninjutsu used specifically to suppress, bind, contain, or interfere with an active Chakra effect, summon, spirit, transformation, or creature when the technique is capable of doing so.

Storage Specialist — Retrieval and storage Actions made through your Sealing Ninjutsu have their final Speed reduced by 2, minimum 1.

### Master Artisan

A Master Artisan understands that the difference between scrap and treasure is often whether anyone present knows what they are looking at. At this level, materials other craftsmen treat as dangerous curiosities become ingredients.

Material Savant — Master two additional Wondrous Materials from the available list.

Signature Work — Choose one permanent item you personally crafted. Magnum Opus — You may work with every Wondrous Material currently available to ordinary Master Artisans. ### Interrogator
{:#interrogator}

Some people crack under threats. Others talk because somebody made them comfortable. The useful skill is knowing which person is sitting in front of you before you choose the tool.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-14}

You gain Read the Subject:

[Speed 10, Utility, Social] Read the Subject — After at least a brief meaningful exchange with a creature capable of communicating with you, make an Espionage check opposed by their Espionage or another appropriate defensive social roll.

On success, choose one:

Learn whether they are deliberately withholding something relevant to the current subject.

Identify the person, topic, place, or consequence that produces the strongest visible emotional reaction during the exchange.

Identify the broad kind of leverage currently driving them: fear, loyalty, greed, duty, pride, desperation, or something similarly apparent.

Determine whether one statement they made conflicts with information you have already independently confirmed.

Read the Subject does not compel speech, reveal thoughts, or tell you that a statement is objectively true. It reads behaviour, inconsistencies, and pressure.

#### Developments
{:#developments-14}

Rapport — Gain +3 Espionage when gathering information from a willing, cooperative, or conversational target rather than threatening or restraining them.

Pressure — Once per scene, after a target refuses to answer, deliberately evades a question, or ends an exchange, you may immediately make another Read the Subject check against them with +3.

Cross-Examination — When a target contradicts something they previously told you during the same scene, gain +5 on your next Read the Subject check against them.

Field Debrief — Once per Hunt or Mission, when you use testimony from a witness, survivor, captive, or local informant as a meaningful part of a Research, Track, or Study Weakness roll, add +5 to that roll.

Closed Book — Gain +3 on Espionage, Willpower, or other rolls made specifically to resist interrogation, coercive questioning, or attempts to extract information from you without Genjutsu.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-13}

Break the Story — Once per scene, after you have succeeded on at least two Read the Subject checks against the same target, you may ask one direct question. The target is never compelled to answer. If they answer, you automatically recognize whether the response is a deliberate lie, a deliberate evasion, or something they sincerely believe. This does not reveal what the truth actually is. Against another player character, use this only when the normal social/PvP rules permit such information to be established.

A weapon stops being equipment after enough years in your hands. Its weight becomes posture, its reach becomes distance, and the space around you starts being measured in cuts.

Choose one weapon family or closely related group of weapons you are proficient with. The choice should describe one coherent fighting discipline, such as swords, spears, axes, bows, staves, or thrown weapons, rather than all melee or all ranged weapons.

Attacks made with your chosen weapon family gain +1 Accuracy.

#### Developments

Tempo — After successfully Blocking or Parrying with your chosen weapon family, reduce the final Speed of your next Attack with that weapon family by 2, minimum 1.

Weapon Control — Gain +3 on rolls made specifically to Disarm, bind, trap, contest, or otherwise control an enemy's weapon using a weapon from your chosen family, where the relevant combat action permits such a roll.

Footwork — After a full hit with your chosen weapon family or a successful Block or Parry using it, move up to 1 yard without AP or additional Speed. This movement must follow a legal path and cannot pass through occupied spaces.

Extension of the Body — When declaring an Attack with your chosen weapon family, choose one: gain +3 Accuracy, or reduce the Attack's final Speed by 3, minimum 1. Declare the choice before rolling.

### Sensor Training
{:#sensor-training}

Most Hunters use their eyes until the world gives them a reason not to. Sensors learn to feel Chakra itself: presence, motion, pressure, and the faint signatures left behind when somebody thought they were gone.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-16}

You gain Chakra Sense:

[Speed 10, Chakra 5, Utility, Concentration] Chakra Sense — Until the end of your next Action, you can sense unsuppressed Chakra sources within 30 yards even without line of sight, unless a barrier or effect specifically blocks Chakra sensing.

You learn the number of distinct sources you can sense and their rough direction. This does not automatically reveal identity, exact position, elemental affinities, techniques, or whether a source is hostile.

When a creature is deliberately suppressing its Chakra, use Chakra Control in place of Awareness for any opposed roll required to detect its signature.

#### Developments
{:#developments-16}

Wide Field — Increase Chakra Sense range to 100 yards. Beyond 30 yards you learn only the number and broad direction of signatures unless another Development says otherwise.

Signature Memory — After sensing the same creature for at least 20 IC or spending meaningful time around them, you may memorize their Chakra signature. Gain +5 on later Chakra Control rolls made specifically to recognize that creature by Chakra. You may retain a number of memorized signatures equal to 3 + XP/1000; replacing one is your choice.

Fine Sense — Within 30 yards, Chakra Sense also tells you whether a sensed creature is currently moulding significant Chakra and gives a rough comparison of its Chakra presence to your own: much weaker, weaker, similar, stronger, or much stronger.

Combat Sensor — While Chakra Sense is active, gain +2 on Defensive Rolls against Attacks made by creatures you can sense but cannot clearly see. Sensing a creature this way prevents you from being treated as completely unaware of that creature, but does not remove concealment, cover, invisibility, or other visual penalties.

Residual Chakra — You may use Chakra Sense to examine fresh Chakra residue left on a creature, object, or location. Gain +3 on Track, Research, Awareness, or Espionage rolls where identifying or following that residue is directly relevant.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-15}

Mind's Eye — While conscious, you passively sense unsuppressed Chakra sources within 15 yards without activating Chakra Sense or paying its Chakra cost. You learn only presence and rough direction through this passive sense. Suppressed signatures still require the normal opposed roll, and Fine Sense or Wide Field information still requires active Chakra Sense.

### Chakra Suppression
{:#chakra-suppression}

The easiest Chakra signature to find is one that does not know it is shouting. You have learned to close the coils down, bury the pressure, and move through the world without advertising what you are.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-17}

You gain Still the Coils:

[Speed 10, Utility, Self, Concentration] Still the Coils — Suppress your Chakra signature until you end this effect or perform an Action that spends Chakra.

While suppressed, Chakra sensing does not automatically detect you. When a sensor attempts to locate or identify your signature, oppose their sensing roll with Espionage and gain +10 to your result.

Still the Coils conceals your Chakra presence; it does not make your body invisible, silence you, erase footprints, or prevent ordinary Awareness from finding you.

#### Developments
{:#developments-17}

Quiet Circulation — Still the Coils becomes Speed 5. The bonus on opposed rolls to conceal your signature becomes +15.

Muffled Technique — A Utility or Defensive Action that spends Chakra does not end Still the Coils. An Attack that spends Chakra always ends it unless another rule explicitly says otherwise.

False Reading — When you activate Still the Coils, you may choose to leave behind a deliberately misleading surface signature. A sensor who detects you but does not beat your opposed Espionage roll reads you as non-shinobi or as possessing a much weaker Chakra presence. A sensor who beats the roll recognizes the deception.

Ghost Trail — Gain +5 on Espionage or Survival rolls made specifically to conceal, break, or falsify a trail of Chakra residue you left behind.

Cold Start — The first Ninjutsu you declare after ending Still the Coils has its final Seal Speed reduced by 2, minimum 0.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-16}

Zero Presence — While Still the Coils is active, your bonus on opposed rolls to hide your Chakra becomes +15. Once per battle, when an Action that spends Chakra would end Still the Coils, you may keep the suppression until that Action fully resolves. Observers can still perceive the Action by ordinary means; this only delays the reappearance of your Chakra signature.
