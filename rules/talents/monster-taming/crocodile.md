# Crocodile Species
{:#crocodile-species}

Role: Ambush / Grapple

Crocodiles are ambush grapplers. They are difficult to dislodge, frightening in water and built to turn one clean bite into control.

## Profile
{:#profile-7}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 4 | 3 | 1 |

Basic Attack: Speed 11 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: +2

Movement: Ground; swims at normal Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-8}

### River Predator
{:#river-predator}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Crocodile swims at twice its normal Movement and may remain submerged for an entire scene without Suffocation checks. It gains +1 on Stealth checks while substantially submerged.

### Lockjaw
{:#lockjaw}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: When the Crocodile's Basic Attack hits by 5 or more, it may immediately attempt a Grapple. This costs no Command AP and adds no Speed.

### Low Profile
{:#low-profile}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: While Prone or substantially submerged, ranged Attacks originating more than 5 yards away suffer -1 Accuracy against the Crocodile.

### Crushing Hold
{:#crushing-hold}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: A creature Grappled by the Crocodile suffers -1 Defence against it. The Crocodile's Basic Attacks against that creature deal +15% Final Damage.

### Drag Below
{:#drag-below}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: While in water and Grappling a creature, the Crocodile may carry that creature at full Movement. A submerged target that cannot breathe immediately begins the normal Suffocation rules. The Crocodile counts as one Size larger for Grapples.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-8}

### Armoured Scutes
{:#armoured-scutes}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Crushing Jaws
{:#crushing-jaws}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% against Grappled targets.

### River Hunter
{:#river-hunter}

Type: Utility

River Predator's swimming Movement becomes 2.5 times normal Movement. The Crocodile also gains +3 Awareness on checks made to detect creatures moving through the same body of water.

### Waterline Ambush
{:#waterline-ambush}

Type: Utility

When substantially submerged or concealed in marsh, grant +3 to an allied Stealth or ambush preparation check involving the monster.

### Lockjaw
{:#lockjaw-2}

Type: One-Time

The monster gains +3 on checks to maintain a Grapple it initiated.

### Low Profile
{:#low-profile-2}

Type: One-Time

Low Profile imposes -2 Accuracy instead of -1 against ranged Attacks from more than 5 yards away.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-7}

### Jaw Lock
{:#jaw-lock}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Make an Attack for 100% Monster Damage. If it hits by 5 or more, immediately Grapple the target.

### Death Roll
{:#death-roll}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 8

Requires: a Grappled target

Effects: Make an Attack for 120% Monster Damage; on a hit, knock the target Prone and move both creatures up to 2 yards.

### Drag Under
{:#drag-under}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 7

Requires: the monster to be in water and Grappling a target

Effects: Move up to the monster's full Movement, carrying the target. The target immediately begins normal Suffocation rules if submerged and unable to breathe.

### Blackwater Ambush
{:#blackwater-ambush}

Speed: 15

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: The monster may move up to 8 yards from concealment, water or Cover and Attack for 150% Monster Damage. If the target had not detected the monster, this Attack gains +3 Accuracy.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-7}

Crocodiles are patient ambush predators. They respond well to stillness, water and decisive attacks, but become difficult when repeatedly forced into noisy pursuit, dragged away from secure territory without purpose, or made to kill purely for sport.
