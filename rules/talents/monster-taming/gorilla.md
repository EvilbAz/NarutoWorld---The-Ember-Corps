# Gorilla Species
{:#gorilla-species}

Role: Grapple / Utility

Gorillas are powerful brawlers and protectors. They excel at climbing, throws, grapples and physically controlling the space around allies.

## Profile
{:#profile-11}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 3 | 4 | 1 |

Basic Attack: Speed 11 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: +2

Movement: Ground; climbs at half Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-12}

### Great Arms
{:#great-arms}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Gorilla gains +2 on Grapple checks and physical checks made to lift, throw, hold or brace a creature or object.

### Long Arms
{:#long-arms}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: Increase the Gorilla's melee reach by 1 yard.

### Hurling Strength
{:#hurling-strength}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Speed: 14

Tags: Attack

Requires: A Grappled target or unattended object

Effects: Throw the target up to 4 yards. A creature takes 60% Monster Damage and lands Prone; an unwilling creature may oppose with Grapple Defence.

### Protective Instinct
{:#protective-instinct}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: Adjacent allies may treat the Gorilla as Cover 1. It gains +3 on checks made to break a Grapple affecting an adjacent ally or pull that ally free.

### King of the Line
{:#king-of-the-line}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Gorilla counts as one Size larger for Grapples, carrying and forced movement. It may maintain one Grapple with each arm against up to two human-sized creatures. While Grappling only one creature, it gains +2 Defence against forced movement.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-12}

### Great Arms
{:#great-arms-2}

Type: Stackable

Each rank gives +1 to Grapple checks and physical checks made to lift, throw, hold or brace a creature or object.

### Hammering Blows
{:#hammering-blows}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% against Prone creatures or unattended objects.

### Climber
{:#climber}

Type: Utility

The monster climbs rough walls, trees and ruins at normal Movement when handholds exist, and gains +3 to Athletics-like physical tasks.

### Builder's Hands
{:#builder-s-hands}

Type: Utility

The monster can manipulate ordinary tools, tie ropes, move debris and perform coarse construction despite its size; grant +3 to one relevant Prepare or Survival check.

### Long Arms
{:#long-arms-2}

Type: One-Time

Increase melee reach by 1 additional yard. This stacks with Long Arms.

### Protective Instinct
{:#protective-instinct-2}

Type: One-Time

Protective Instinct's bonus to break a Grapple affecting an adjacent ally becomes +5, and its Cover increases to Cover 2.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-11}

### Hammerfist
{:#hammerfist}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Attack for 110% Monster Damage. On a full hit, inflict Knockback 3 or Prone.

### Hurl
{:#hurl}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 8

Requires: a Grappled target or unattended object

Effects: Throw it up to 5 yards. A creature takes 80% Monster Damage and lands Prone; an unwilling creature may oppose with Grapple Defence.

### Grasping Guard
{:#grasping-guard}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Range: 2 yards

Effects: Trigger: an enemy moves past the monster toward an ally. Attempt a Grapple. On success, the triggering movement ends.

### King's Crash
{:#king-s-crash}

Speed: 16

Base Command AP: 12

Area: 4 yards

Effects: The gorilla slams the ground; creatures in Area defend separately. On a hit take 110% Monster Damage and suffer Knockback 4; on a full hit also become Prone.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-11}

Gorillas are social, practical and highly responsive to familiar handlers. They work hard when given clear physical tasks, but react poorly to pointless bullying or being made to destroy useful tools, shelter or supplies without purpose.
