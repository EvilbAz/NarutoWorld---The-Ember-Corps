# Wyrm Species
{:#wyrm-species}

Role: Elemental / Bruiser

Wyrms are hardy draconic generalists. They combine scales, unusual movement and a chosen breath affinity, but lack the extreme specialization of other species.

## Profile
{:#profile-17}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 |

Basic Attack: Speed 9 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Slashing

Command AP Modifier: +0

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-18}

### Lesser Breath
{:#lesser-breath}

Unlock: Core Talent

Speed: 11

Cone: 4 yards

Tags: Attack, Energy

Effects: When the monster is acquired, choose Fire, Water, Wind, Earth or Lightning as the Wyrm's Breath Affinity. Attack each creature in the Cone for 60% Monster Damage as Energy damage. Lesser Breath carries the chosen elemental tag and is an Independent Action.

### Scale Ward
{:#scale-ward}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Wyrm gains 15% damage reduction against effects carrying its Breath Affinity's elemental tag and +2 Defence against Attacks carrying that tag.

### Draconic Movement
{:#draconic-movement}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: Choose one: Burrow through ordinary soil at half Movement, or Glide to ignore ordinary falling damage. If another rule already grants the chosen option, improve it as that rule describes instead.

### Greater Breath
{:#greater-breath}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: Lesser Breath becomes Cone 6 and deals 80% Monster Damage. On a full hit, apply the Breath Affinity's rider: Fire inflicts Burns 1; Water inflicts Knockback 1; Wind inflicts Knockback 2; Earth inflicts Immobilization 1; Lightning inflicts Paralysis 1.

### Winged Elder
{:#winged-elder}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Wyrm gains true flight at half normal Movement. Lesser Breath now deals 100% Monster Damage while retaining Greater Breath's Cone and elemental rider.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-18}

### Thick Scales
{:#thick-scales}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Deep Breath
{:#deep-breath}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, increase the Range, Line or Cone size of Wyrm Breath Species Features and Monster Techniques by 1 yard.

### Elemental Scent
{:#elemental-scent}

Type: Utility

Choose Fire, Water, Wind, Earth or Lightning when selected. Grant +3 to Awareness or Research checks to identify recent physical traces of that element or its chakra effects. May be selected again for another element.

### Burrow or Glide
{:#burrow-or-glide}

Type: Utility

Choose one improvement: gain the Draconic Movement option you do not possess, improve Burrow to normal Movement, or allow Glide to travel up to 2 horizontal yards for every 1 yard descended. This Utility Trait may be selected again for a different improvement.

### Breath Affinity
{:#breath-affinity}

Type: One-Time

The Wyrm's chosen Breath Affinity becomes especially potent. Increase any numerical Burns, Knockback, Immobilization or Paralysis rider caused by its Breath Species Features and Monster Techniques by 1, and gain +2 Resistance against environmental effects of that element.

### Winged Wyrm
{:#winged-wyrm}

Type: One-Time

If Winged Elder is not yet unlocked, gain true flight at half normal Movement and require the Glide option from Draconic Movement or Burrow or Glide. Once Winged Elder is unlocked, flying Movement becomes normal Movement instead.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-17}

### Wyrm Breath
{:#wyrm-breath}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 7

Line: 8 yards

Effects: Attack each creature in the Line for 100% Monster Damage as Energy damage carrying the Wyrm's Breath Affinity elemental tag. On a full hit: Fire inflicts Burns 1; Water inflicts Knockback 1; Wind inflicts Knockback 2; Earth inflicts Immobilization 1; Lightning inflicts Paralysis 1.

### Scale Ward
{:#scale-ward-2}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Trigger: the monster is hit. Reduce Final Damage by 20% and gain +2 Defence until its next Action against attacks sharing the Breath Affinity's element.

### Coiling Rush
{:#coiling-rush}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Move up to 5 yards and Attack for 105% Monster Damage. On a full hit, inflict Knockback 3 or Prone.

### Elder Breath
{:#elder-breath}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Line 12 or Cone 7, chosen when used. Attack each target for 115% Monster Damage as Energy damage. If the Wyrm has Breath Affinity, the technique carries that elemental tag. On a full hit: Fire inflicts Burns 2; Water inflicts Knockback 2; Wind inflicts Knockback 3; Earth inflicts Immobilization 2; Lightning inflicts Paralysis 2.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-17}

Wyrms are proud, territorial and highly intelligent monsters. They respond best to confident handlers and a stable den or territory. Humiliating treatment, using one as a trophy, or repeatedly violating established training boundaries can trigger Rebellion.
