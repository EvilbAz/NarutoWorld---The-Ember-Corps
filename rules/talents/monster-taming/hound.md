# Hound Species
{:#hound-species}

Role: Tracking / Teamwork

Hounds are reliable hunting partners. They specialize in scent, teamwork, takedowns and protecting the person they have accepted as their handler.

## Profile
{:#profile-20}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 4 |

Basic Attack: Speed 8 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: -1

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-21}

### Tracking Hound
{:#tracking-hound}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: A meaningful scent sample such as blood, clothing or fur lets the Hound establish a physical trail where one plausibly remains. It gains +3 Survival when following that trail.

### Handler's Guard
{:#handler-s-guard}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: While within 2 yards of you, the Hound gains +1 Defence and you gain +1 Defence against Melee Attacks.

### Takedown Bite
{:#takedown-bite}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: When the Hound's Basic Attack hits by 5 or more, choose one: knock the target Prone, or immediately attempt a Grapple. This costs no Command AP and adds no Speed.

### Handler Guard
{:#handler-guard}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Trigger: You are hit by a Melee Attack.

Effects: The Hound may move up to 3 yards toward you. If it becomes adjacent, reduce that Attack's Final Damage by 10%.

### Perfect Teamwork
{:#perfect-teamwork}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: When you fully hit a creature, the Hound's next Basic Attack against it before the end of its next Action gains +2 Accuracy and +20% Final Damage. When the Hound fully hits a creature, your next Attack against it before the end of your next Action gains +1 Accuracy. Neither benefit stacks with itself.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-21}

### Keen Nose
{:#keen-nose}

Type: Stackable

Gain +1 on Survival or Awareness checks that rely on scent.

### Pack Pressure
{:#pack-pressure}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% against a creature adjacent to one of the monster's allies.

### Tracking Hound
{:#tracking-hound-2}

Type: Utility

Tracking Hound grants +5 Survival instead of +3. When the Hound successfully locates a Hidden creature by scent, it may immediately Point Out that creature.

### Working Dog
{:#working-dog}

Type: Utility

Choose one of Awareness, Espionage, Survival or Athletics. When the hound meaningfully assists that kind of task, grant +3 to the allied check. This Utility Trait may be selected again for another skill.

### Handler's Guard
{:#handler-s-guard-2}

Type: One-Time

Handler's Guard grants the Hound +2 Defence instead of +1. The tamer's +1 Defence remains unchanged.

### Takedown Bite
{:#takedown-bite-2}

Type: One-Time

When Takedown Bite triggers, the Hound may both knock the target Prone and immediately attempt the Grapple instead of choosing one.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-20}

### Tracking Fang
{:#tracking-fang}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 6

Requires: a meaningful scent trail

Effects: The hound may pursue a target it cannot currently see along a plausible physical route, gaining +5 Survival and immediately Pointing Out the target if it locates them in Stealth.

### Takedown
{:#takedown}

Speed: 9

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Attack for 90% Monster Damage. On a hit by 5 or more, knock the target Prone and attempt a Grapple.

### Handler Guard
{:#handler-guard-2}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Range: 3 yards

Effects: Trigger: the tamer is hit by a Melee Attack. The hound moves up to 3 yards toward them; if it becomes adjacent, reduce that Attack's Final Damage by 20%.

### Pack Hunt
{:#pack-hunt}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 11

Effects: Attack for 120% Monster Damage. If at least one ally is adjacent to the target, gain +2 Accuracy; on a full hit, that ally may move 2 yards without changing Initiative.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-20}

Hounds are strongly handler-focused and thrive on familiar routines, work and pack contact. Deliberate abandonment, disposable use, or knowingly sending one to die simply to save the tamer effort can trigger Rebellion.
