# Moth Species
{:#moth-species}

Role: Control / Scouting

Moths are fragile evasive support monsters. They specialize in flight, dust, concealment, sensory disruption and battlefield utility.

## Profile
{:#profile-12}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 4 | 5 |

Basic Attack: Speed 7 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: -2

Movement: Flight

## Species Features
{:#species-features-13}

### Dust Wake
{:#dust-wake}

Unlock: Core Talent

Speed: 8

Area: 3 yards

Tags: Utility

Effects: Create Visibility 2 in the Area until the Moth's next Action. The Moth ignores Visibility created by its own Species Features.

### Fluttering Ghost
{:#fluttering-ghost}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: After moving at least 3 yards while airborne, the Moth gains +1 Defence against ranged Attacks until its next Action.

### Powder Sense
{:#powder-sense}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: The Moth and adjacent allies ignore up to 2 points of Visibility created by the Moth. The Moth also gains +2 Awareness against creatures moving inside its dust.

### Lulling Dust
{:#lulling-dust}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: The first time each hostile creature begins an Action inside Visibility created by the Moth, it must resist against Monster Save TN. On failure, it suffers -2 Awareness until the end of that Action. A creature can be affected once per Moth Action.

### Moonwing Shroud
{:#moonwing-shroud}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: Dust Wake becomes Area 5 and Visibility 3. When used, choose any number of allies in the Area; those allies ignore that Dust Wake's Visibility.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-13}

### Powdered Wings
{:#powdered-wings}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, increase the Visibility penalty created by Moth Species Features and Monster Techniques by 1.

### Drifting Flight
{:#drifting-flight}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases flying Movement by 0.1 yard per IC.

### Night Sense
{:#night-sense}

Type: Utility

The monster ignores up to 4 points of darkness-based Visibility penalty and grants +3 to one Awareness check involving light, vibration or air movement.

### Pollen Reader
{:#pollen-reader}

Type: Utility

The monster can identify disturbed dust, pollen, ash and airborne residue, granting +3 to one Track, Research or Survival check where those traces matter.

### Soft Landing
{:#soft-landing}

Type: One-Time

The monster and one adjacent willing creature ignore ordinary falling damage from up to 10 yards; greater falls reduce their effective distance by 10 yards.

### Evasive Flutter
{:#evasive-flutter}

Type: One-Time

After the Moth successfully Dodges an Attack while airborne, it may move 1 yard without changing Initiative. This cannot happen more than once between its Actions.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-12}

### Dust Veil
{:#dust-veil}

Speed: 8

Base Command AP: 5

Area: 5 yards

Effects: Create Visibility 3 until the monster's next Action. The monster ignores its own Dust Veil.

### Lulling Powder
{:#lulling-powder}

Speed: 9

Base Command AP: 7

Cone: 5 yards

Effects: Targets resist against Monster Save TN. On failure, inflict Paralysis 2 and -2 Awareness for 10 IC.

### Moonlit Mark
{:#moonlit-mark}

Speed: 7

Base Command AP: 5

Range: 15 yards

Effects: Dust clings to one creature for 20 IC. That creature cannot benefit from more than 3 points of Visibility against the monster and allies who can see the glowing dust.

### Wingstorm
{:#wingstorm}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 11

Area: 6 yards

Effects: Make an Attack against each hostile creature for 70% Monster Damage and inflict Knockback 3 on a hit. Smoke, loose dust and mundane airborne obscurants in the Area are dispersed.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-12}

Moths are curious creatures drawn to light, air currents and strange traces. They are easily distressed by uncontrolled flame and repeated exposure to toxins; deploying one purely to be burned, poisoned or destroyed as a disposable distraction can trigger Rebellion.
