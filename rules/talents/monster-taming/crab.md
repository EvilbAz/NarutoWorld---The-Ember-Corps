# Crab Species
{:#crab-species}

Role: Defence / Grapple

Crabs are defensive controllers. Their shell and pincers dominate narrow spaces, punish movement and hold targets in place.

## Profile
{:#profile-19}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 2 | 5 | 1 |

Basic Attack: Speed 11 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: +2

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-20}

### Shell Wall
{:#shell-wall}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Crab gains 10 DR against physical damage. An adjacent ally may treat it as Cover 1 against Attacks originating from the opposite side.

### Vice Grip
{:#vice-grip}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Crab gains +1 on checks to initiate or maintain a Grapple with its pincers. When its Basic Attack hits by 5 or more, it may immediately attempt a Grapple for no Command AP and no additional Speed.

### Amphibious
{:#amphibious}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: Shallow water, surf and seabeds do not impose mundane Movement penalties on the Crab. It may swim at half normal Movement and remain underwater for an entire scene without Suffocation checks.

### Sideways Guard
{:#sideways-guard}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: After successfully Dodging an Attack, the Crab may move 1 yard without changing Initiative. This can occur once between its Actions.

### Control Zone
{:#control-zone}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Trigger: A hostile creature voluntarily leaves the Crab's melee reach.

Effects: Attempt a Grapple. On success, the triggering movement ends. Shell Wall also becomes 15 DR against physical damage and Cover 2, and the Crab reduces Knockback suffered by 2, minimum 0.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-20}

### Shell Layers
{:#shell-layers}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Vice Grip
{:#vice-grip-2}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, gain +1 on checks to initiate or maintain a Grapple with the pincers.

### Amphibious
{:#amphibious-2}

Type: Utility

Amphibious improves the Crab's swimming Movement from half to full normal Movement and grants +3 on checks to resist currents or aquatic forced movement.

### Burrowing Crab
{:#burrowing-crab}

Type: Utility

Outside Initiative, the monster can dig shallow pits, trenches or hiding hollows in sand or loose soil at half normal working time.

### Sideways Guard
{:#sideways-guard-2}

Type: One-Time

Sideways Guard may move 2 yards instead of 1 after a successful Dodge.

### Wall of Shell
{:#wall-of-shell}

Type: One-Time

Increase the Cover granted by Shell Wall by 1. This makes it Cover 2 before Elder Feature and Cover 3 after Control Zone.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-19}

### Pincer Hold
{:#pincer-hold}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Attack for 80% Monster Damage and attempt a Grapple on hit. A target Grappled this way suffers -2 to actions that require the held limb.

### Shell Wall
{:#shell-wall-2}

Speed: 4

Base Command AP: 5

Effects: Until the monster's next Action, gain +3 Defence and become Cover 3, but Movement is halved.

### Undertow
{:#undertow}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 7

Requires: water or loose sand

Effects: Attack a creature within 5 yards for 70% Monster Damage; on hit pull it 4 yards toward the crab.

### Crushing Clamp
{:#crushing-clamp}

Speed: 15

Base Command AP: 11

Requires: a Grappled target

Effects: Attack for 140% Monster Damage. On a full hit, inflict Immobilization 4 until the Grapple ends.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-19}

Crabs are territorial and settle quickly into guarding a chosen space. They will hold that space under heavy pressure, but repeatedly forcing one to abandon creatures or positions it has been trained to shelter can trigger Rebellion.
