# Hyena Species
{:#hyena-species}

Role: Pack Offence / Pursuit

Hyenas are relentless opportunists. They thrive on injured prey, morale pressure and repeatedly opening the same target for allies.

## Profile
{:#profile-15}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 |

Basic Attack: Speed 9 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: +0

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-16}

### Pack Opportunist
{:#pack-opportunist}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Hyena's Basic Attacks gain +1 Accuracy against a creature adjacent to one of its allies.

### Endless Chase
{:#endless-chase}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: Increase the Hyena's Movement by 25% while it moves toward a Wounded or Bleeding creature.

### Mob Them
{:#mob-them}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: Once between the Hyena's Actions, when an ally hits a creature within 3 yards of the Hyena, it may move up to 2 yards toward that creature after the Attack resolves without changing Initiative.

### Cackling Pressure
{:#cackling-pressure}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: When the Hyena fully hits a creature, that creature suffers -1 Defence against the next Attack made by one of the Hyena's allies before the target's next Action.

### Feeding Frenzy
{:#feeding-frenzy}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Hyena's Basic Attacks against Prone, Wounded or Bleeding creatures deal +25% Final Damage. On a full hit against such a target, it also suffers -2 Defence until its next Action. This does not stack with Cackling Pressure.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-16}

### Bonecracker
{:#bonecracker}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% against a target that has suffered a Wound this scene.

### Endless Chase
{:#endless-chase-2}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases Movement by 0.1 yard per IC while moving toward a Wounded or Bleeding enemy.

### Carrion Wise
{:#carrion-wise}

Type: Utility

Grant +3 to Survival or Research checks involving carcasses, scavengers, feeding sites, disease signs or determining whether remains were disturbed.

### Laughing Signal
{:#laughing-signal}

Type: Utility

The monster can coordinate simple warnings and directions over long distances by distinctive calls; allies familiar with it can distinguish danger, retreat, found trail and rally signals.

### Pack Opportunist
{:#pack-opportunist-2}

Type: One-Time

Pack Opportunist grants +2 Accuracy instead of +1, and Basic Attacks benefiting from it deal +10% Final Damage.

### Unnerving Cackle
{:#unnerving-cackle}

Type: One-Time

Enemies within 5 yards suffer -2 to Resistance against fear-based effects created by the monster.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-15}

### Cackling Feint
{:#cackling-feint}

Speed: 9

Base Command AP: 5

Range: 8 yards

Effects: One target resists against Monster Save TN. On failure it suffers -2 Defence against the next Attack made against it before its next Action. This is a fear-based effect.

### Harrier Bite
{:#harrier-bite}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Attack for 105% Monster Damage. If an ally hit the target since the monster's previous Action, gain +2 Accuracy.

### Bonecrush
{:#bonecrush}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 9

Effects: Attack for 130% Monster Damage. Against a Prone or Wounded target, increase damage to 150%.

### Feeding Frenzy
{:#feeding-frenzy-2}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Make an Attack for 100% Monster Damage. If it hits, up to two allies within 3 yards may each move 2 yards toward the target without changing Initiative. The target suffers -2 Defence until its next Action.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-15}

Hyenas are food-motivated pack hunters and learn quickly through shared hunts and consistent reward. Starving one of expected rewards, isolating it from familiar packmates without cause, or repeatedly wasting kills after encouraging it to hunt can trigger Rebellion.
