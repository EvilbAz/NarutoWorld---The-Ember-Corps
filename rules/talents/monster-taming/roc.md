# Roc Species
{:#roc-species}

Role: Mobility / Rescue

Rocs are aerial control monsters. They trade raw durability for flight, transport, diving attacks and the ability to reposition allies or enemies.

## Profile
{:#profile-8}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 |

Basic Attack: Speed 8 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: -1

Movement: Flight

## Species Features
{:#species-features-9}

### Sky Carrier
{:#sky-carrier}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Roc has true flight and may carry one willing human-sized creature while flying at half Movement.

### Dive Hunter
{:#dive-hunter}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: If the Roc moved at least 5 yards immediately before making a Basic Attack, that Attack deals +15% Final Damage.

### Wingover
{:#wingover}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: After moving at least 4 yards while airborne, the Roc gains +1 Defence against ranged Attacks until its next Action.

### Airlift
{:#airlift}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: The Roc may carry one willing human-sized creature at full Movement. Carrying that creature no longer reduces its Movement from Sky Carrier.

### Sky Tyrant
{:#sky-tyrant}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: If the Roc begins at least 5 yards above or away from a target and moves at least 5 yards before its Basic Attack, that Attack deals an additional +25% Final Damage. On a full hit, also inflict Knockback 3 and Prone.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-9}

### Broad Wings
{:#broad-wings}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases flying Movement by 0.1 yard per IC.

### Dive Hunter
{:#dive-hunter-2}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% if the monster moved at least 5 yards immediately before attacking.

### Sky Carrier
{:#sky-carrier-2}

Type: Utility

The Roc may carry one additional human-sized willing creature. When carrying more than one creature, fly at half Movement unless Airlift or another rule says otherwise. This Utility Trait may be selected again for another passenger.

### Far Sight
{:#far-sight}

Type: Utility

Gain +3 on Awareness checks based on long-distance vision, weather observation or locating large landmarks from the air.

### Wing Buffer
{:#wing-buffer}

Type: One-Time

The monster and adjacent allies reduce forced movement caused by wind by 3 yards, minimum 0.

### Wingover
{:#wingover-2}

Type: One-Time

Wingover grants +2 Defence against ranged Attacks instead of +1.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-8}

### Talon Snatch
{:#talon-snatch}

Speed: 9

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Move up to 5 yards, then Attack for 90% Monster Damage. On a hit by 5 or more, the monster may carry a human-sized target 2 yards in any direction; unwilling targets may oppose with Grapple Defence.

### Gale Wing
{:#gale-wing}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 7

Cone: 6 yards

Effects: Creatures in the cone make a Defence against the monster's Accuracy. On failure, suffer Knockback 4; unattended smoke, dust and similar light obscurants are dispersed.

### Rescue Lift
{:#rescue-lift}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Range: 4 yards

Effects: Trigger: a willing ally would be hit by an Area effect the Roc could physically lift them out of. Move that ally up to 4 yards to a legal space; if this leaves the Area, the Attack no longer targets them.

### Heavenfall Dive
{:#heavenfall-dive}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: The Roc must begin at least 5 yards above or away from the target. Move up to 10 yards and Attack for 150% Monster Damage. On a full hit, inflict Knockback 5 and Prone.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-8}

Rocs require space, height and a clear route to move. They can be trained to enter danger for transport or rescue, but deliberate confinement or repeatedly forcing one into a kill-zone with no route out can trigger Rebellion.
