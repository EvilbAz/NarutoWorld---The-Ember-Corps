# Tortoise Species
{:#tortoise-species}

Role: Fortress / Protection

Tortoises are fortress monsters. They are poor pursuers and weak attackers, but exceptionally difficult to remove and excellent at sheltering allies.

## Profile
{:#profile-10}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 1 | 5 | 1 |

Basic Attack: Speed 11 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: +2

Movement: Ground; swims at half Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-11}

### Living Fortress
{:#living-fortress}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Tortoise gains 10 DR. Adjacent allies may treat it as Cover 2 against Attacks originating from the opposite side.

### Brace
{:#brace}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: If the Tortoise has not voluntarily moved since its previous Action, it gains an additional 5 DR and reduces Knockback suffered by 2, minimum 0.

### Living Shelter
{:#living-shelter}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: One willing adjacent human-sized ally may ride on the Tortoise's shell. While aboard, that ally moves with the Tortoise without spending Movement to remain with it. Mounting or dismounting is an ordinary object-interaction Action.

### Bastion
{:#bastion}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: Allies using the Tortoise as Cover gain +1 to Defensive Rolls against ranged and Area Attacks when its shell can physically interpose itself.

### Unbreakable
{:#unbreakable}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: Living Fortress becomes 20 DR and Cover 3. While below half maximum Vitality, the Tortoise gains an additional 5 DR. It cannot be knocked Prone by ordinary forced movement and ignores the first 3 yards of Knockback from each effect.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-11}

### Ancient Shell
{:#ancient-shell}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Layered Scutes
{:#layered-scutes}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, gain +1 Defence against Attacks originating more than 5 yards away.

### Living Shelter
{:#living-shelter-2}

Type: Utility

Outside Initiative, the shell can protect a small group from ordinary rain, wind, falling debris and similar environmental exposure when its size makes that reasonable.

### Pack Platform
{:#pack-platform}

Type: Utility

The shell may carry one additional human-sized willing creature or equivalent mundane load. This passenger also moves with the Tortoise under Living Shelter.

### Fortress Shell
{:#fortress-shell}

Type: One-Time

While Brace is active, gain an additional 10 DR instead of 5.

### Sheltering Bulk
{:#sheltering-bulk}

Type: One-Time

Increase the Cover granted by Living Fortress by 1. This makes it Cover 3 before Elder Feature and Cover 4 after Unbreakable.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-10}

### Shell Up
{:#shell-up}

Speed: 4

Base Command AP: 4

Effects: Until the monster's next Action, gain +4 Defence and 25 DR, but Movement becomes 0 and it cannot make Attacks.

### Guardian Shell
{:#guardian-shell}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 7

Range: 2 yards

Effects: Trigger: an adjacent ally is hit by an Attack. The ally gains 20% damage reduction against that Attack as the tortoise interposes its shell.

### Rolling Bastion
{:#rolling-bastion}

Speed: 15

Base Command AP: 8

Effects: Move up to 6 yards in a straight line. Creatures whose spaces are crossed defend against the monster's Accuracy; on failure take 80% Monster Damage and suffer Knockback 3.

### Mountain That Walks
{:#mountain-that-walks}

Speed: 17

Base Command AP: 12

Limit: Once per battle

Effects: For 20 IC, the monster gains 30 DR and counts as Cover 4. Up to two willing human-sized allies may ride on its shell when this is declared; while they remain aboard, they move with the Tortoise and do not spend movement to remain with it.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-10}

Tortoises settle strongly into protective routines. Once trained to shelter a person or position, repeatedly forcing one to abandon that charge to danger without necessity or warning can trigger Rebellion.
