# Salamander Species
{:#salamander-species}

Role: Control / Survival

Salamanders are durable utility monsters, comfortable in hostile environments and difficult to keep injured or displaced.

## Profile
{:#profile-5}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 3 | 4 | 2 |

Basic Attack: Speed 10 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: +1

Movement: Ground; swims at normal Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-6}

### Regenerative Flesh
{:#regenerative-flesh}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: At the start of each of the Salamander's Actions, it restores 2% of its maximum Vitality.

### Wall Clinger
{:#wall-clinger}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Salamander may move across walls and ceilings at half Movement without a check when the surface can physically support it.

### Mucous Balance
{:#mucous-balance}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: The Salamander gains +2 Resistance against Poison, Burns and corrosive environmental effects and reduces forced movement suffered by 2 yards, minimum 0.

### Tail Shedding
{:#tail-shedding}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Limit: Once per battle

Effects: After the Salamander would become Grappled, Prone or Immobilized, it may immediately end one of those conditions after the triggering effect resolves.

### Elder Regeneration
{:#elder-regeneration}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: Regenerative Flesh restores 5% of maximum Vitality instead of 2%. Once per battle, at the start of an Action, the Salamander may also remove up to 2 total levels of Bleed, Burns or Paralysis from itself.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-6}

### Regenerative Flesh
{:#regenerative-flesh-2}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the percentage of maximum Vitality restored by Regenerative Flesh and Elder Regeneration by 1 percentage point.

### Clinging Toes
{:#clinging-toes}

Type: Stackable

Gain +1 Defence against forced movement, Knockback and effects that would knock the monster Prone.

### Wall Clinger
{:#wall-clinger-2}

Type: Utility

Wall Clinger allows movement across walls and ceilings at normal Movement instead of half Movement.

### Hazard Skin
{:#hazard-skin}

Type: Utility

Choose heat, cold, acid, or ordinary environmental toxins when this Trait is selected. The monster ignores mundane exposure from that hazard. This Utility Trait may be selected again for a different hazard.

### Tail Shedding
{:#tail-shedding-2}

Type: One-Time

Tail Shedding may be used twice per battle, and may also end Paralysis when triggered.

### Mucous Coat
{:#mucous-coat}

Type: One-Time

Mucous Balance's Resistance bonus becomes +4 instead of +2.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-5}

### Adhesive Lunge
{:#adhesive-lunge}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Make an Attack for 90% Monster Damage. On a full hit, inflict Immobilization 3 for 10 IC as adhesive mucus or gripping limbs hold the target.

### Shed and Slip
{:#shed-and-slip}

Speed: 3 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 5

Effects: Trigger: the monster fails a defence against a Grapple or Immobilization effect. Reroll that defence and use the higher result; if it still fails, reduce the resulting Immobilization by 2.

### Caustic Spit
{:#caustic-spit}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 8

Range: 10 yards

Effects: Make a ranged Attack for 100% Monster Damage as Energy damage. On a full hit, the target suffers -2 to Parry until its next Action.

### Regenerative Molt
{:#regenerative-molt}

Speed: 15

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: The monster sheds damaged tissue and restores 25% of its maximum Vitality. Remove up to 3 total levels of Bleed, Burns or Paralysis from it. Once per battle.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-5}

Salamanders are hardy and unusually tolerant of hostile environments. They can be trained for hazardous work, but repeatedly exposing one to toxins, fire, corrosion or attrition purely to test what it can survive can trigger Rebellion.
