# Leech Species
{:#leech-species}

Role: Sustain / Support

Leeches are sustain monsters. They cling, drain, transfer vitality and purge harmful substances rather than winning through direct offence.

## Profile
{:#profile-18}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 |

Basic Attack: Speed 9 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: +0

Movement: Ground; swims at normal Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-19}

### Blood Feeder
{:#blood-feeder}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: When the Leech's Basic Attack deals damage, it restores Vitality equal to 15% of Final Damage dealt, up to 5% of its maximum Vitality per Action.

### Clinging Body
{:#clinging-body}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Leech gains +2 on checks to maintain Grapples. It may remain attached to a willing creature without impeding that creature's Movement.

### Filter Feeder
{:#filter-feeder}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: The Leech gains +2 Resistance against Poison and disease-like biological effects. While attached to a willing creature, that creature also gains +2 Resistance against those effects.

### Purging Bite
{:#purging-bite}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Speed: 10

Range: Touch

Tags: Utility

Effects: Touch a willing creature and reduce one Poison, Burns or Bleed status by 1, minimum 0. The Leech loses Vitality equal to 5% of its maximum.

### Red Feast
{:#red-feast}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: Blood Feeder restores Vitality equal to 30% of Final Damage dealt, up to 10% of maximum Vitality per Action. Whenever Blood Feeder restores Vitality, one adjacent or attached living ally also restores HP equal to half the amount restored, to a maximum of 5% of the Leech's maximum Vitality per Action.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-19}

### Blood Reservoir
{:#blood-reservoir}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Efficient Feeder
{:#efficient-feeder}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases Vitality restored by Leech Species Features and Monster Techniques by 4%.

### Toxin Taster
{:#toxin-taster}

Type: Utility

The monster can detect whether a touched liquid, wound or food contains an obvious biological poison or contaminant, granting +3 to a relevant Medicine or Toxicology check.

### Medicinal Saliva
{:#medicinal-saliva}

Type: Utility

When the monster assists mundane wound treatment outside Initiative, grant +3 to one Medicine check. It cannot replace required tools or medical jutsu.

### Clinging Body
{:#clinging-body-2}

Type: One-Time

Clinging Body's bonus to maintain Grapples becomes +3. While attached to a willing creature, forced movement affecting that creature does not automatically detach the Leech.

### Filter Feeder
{:#filter-feeder-2}

Type: One-Time

Filter Feeder's Resistance bonus becomes +5 for the Leech and +3 for a willing creature it is attached to.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-18}

### Blood Draw
{:#blood-draw}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Attack for 80% Monster Damage. On hit, restore Vitality to the monster equal to 25% of Final Damage dealt, up to 10% of its maximum Vitality.

### Transfusion
{:#transfusion}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 7

Range: 0 yards

Effects: Transfer up to 15% of the monster's current Vitality to an adjacent living ally as restored Vitality. This cannot restore HP or repair Wounds.

### Purge
{:#purge}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 8

Range: 0 yards

Effects: Touch a willing creature. Reduce one Poison, Burns or Bleed status by 2, minimum 0. The monster loses Vitality equal to 5% of its maximum per level removed.

### Red Feast
{:#red-feast-2}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Attack for 120% Monster Damage. Restore Vitality equal to 40% of Final Damage dealt, then distribute that restored amount between the monster and one adjacent ally. Once per battle.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-18}

Leeches treat blood as food and respond strongly to feeding routines. They can be trained to drain enemies and aid familiar allies, but deliberately feeding one on helpless allies for cruelty, spectacle or convenience can trigger Rebellion.
