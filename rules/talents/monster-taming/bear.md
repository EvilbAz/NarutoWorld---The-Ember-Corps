# Bear Species
{:#bear-species}

Role: Guardian / Grapple

Bears are slow, brutally durable protectors. They excel at bodyguarding, grappling and forcing enemies to deal with them first.

## Profile
{:#profile-6}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 3 | 3 | 1 |

Basic Attack: Speed 11 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: +2

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-7}

### Guardian's Bulk
{:#guardian-s-bulk}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: Adjacent allies may treat the Bear as Cover 1 against Attacks originating from the opposite side. The Bear gains +2 on checks to initiate or maintain Grapples.

### Mauling Hold
{:#mauling-hold}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: When the Bear's Basic Attack hits by 5 or more, it may immediately attempt a Grapple. This costs no Command AP and adds no Speed.

### Get Behind Me
{:#get-behind-me}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Speed: 5 Interrupt

Trigger: An ally within 3 yards is targeted by an Attack the Bear could physically intercept.

Effects: Move the Bear up to 2 yards. If it can interpose itself, it becomes the target and defends normally.

### Unmoving Guardian
{:#unmoving-guardian}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: While Grappling a hostile creature or adjacent to an ally, the Bear gains 15 DR and reduces forced movement suffered by 2 yards, minimum 0.

### Mountain With Teeth
{:#mountain-with-teeth}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Bear counts as one Size larger for Grapples, carrying and forced movement. When it successfully initiates a Grapple, knock that target Prone. Its Basic Attacks against creatures it is Grappling deal +25% Final Damage.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-7}

### Thick Fur
{:#thick-fur}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Heavy Paws
{:#heavy-paws}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% against Prone or Grappled targets.

### Powerful Frame
{:#powerful-frame}

Type: Utility

Gain +3 on physical checks to lift, drag, break or brace mundane objects.

### Trail Shelter
{:#trail-shelter}

Type: Utility

Outside Initiative, the bear can rapidly dig, clear or reinforce a rough campsite or emergency shelter, granting +3 to one relevant Survival check.

### Bodyguard
{:#bodyguard}

Type: One-Time

Get Behind Me becomes Speed 4 instead of Speed 5. When used to protect the tamer, the Bear may move up to 3 yards instead of 2.

### Mauler
{:#mauler}

Type: One-Time

Mauling Hold triggers on any full hit rather than requiring the Basic Attack to hit by 5 or more.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-6}

### Mauling Grip
{:#mauling-grip}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Make an Attack for 100% Monster Damage. On a hit by 5 or more, Grapple the target; while maintained, the target suffers -2 Defence against the bear.

### Roaring Challenge
{:#roaring-challenge}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 5

Area: 8 yards

Effects: Enemies that can hear the bear must make Resistance against Monster Save TN. On failure, they suffer -2 Accuracy against creatures other than the bear until the bear's next Action. This is a fear-based effect.

### Guardian Rush
{:#guardian-rush}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 8

Effects: Move up to 6 yards toward an ally, then either make a Basic Attack or become Cover 3 for that ally until the bear's next Action.

### Avalanche Maul
{:#avalanche-maul}

Speed: 16

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Make an Attack for 140% Monster Damage. On a full hit, knock the target Prone and inflict Stun 4. Against a Grappled target, damage becomes 160% instead.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-6}

Bears form strong protective routines around familiar people. They readily hold a line or cover a retreat, but forcing one to abandon a helpless creature it has been trained to guard can trigger Rebellion.
