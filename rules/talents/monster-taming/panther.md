# Panther Species
{:#panther-species}

Role: Stealth / Assault

Panthers are extreme offensive hunters. They are accurate, fast and lethal from concealment, but their poor Defence makes bad positioning costly.

## Profile
{:#profile-21}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 5 | 1 | 4 |

Basic Attack: Speed 8 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Slashing

Command AP Modifier: -1

Movement: Ground; climbs at half Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-22}

### Silent Predator
{:#silent-predator}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Panther gains +2 on Stealth checks involving stalking or ambushes. Its Basic Attacks made while Hidden or from Cover gain +1 Accuracy.

### Pounce
{:#pounce}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: If the Panther moves at least 4 yards directly toward a target before making a Basic Attack, a full hit also knocks that target Prone.

### Fade After Blood
{:#fade-after-blood}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: After the Panther fully hits with a Basic Attack while it began the Action Hidden or behind Cover, it may move 1 yard without changing Initiative. This can occur once per Panther Action.

### Ambush Killer
{:#ambush-killer}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: The Panther's Basic Attacks made while Hidden or from Cover deal +20% Final Damage.

### Apex Stalker
{:#apex-stalker}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Limit: Once per battle

Effects: The Panther's first Attack against a creature that has not detected it gains +3 Accuracy and +25% Final Damage. On a full hit, inflict Bleed 3. After the Attack resolves, the Panther may move up to 3 yards toward legal Cover without changing Initiative.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-22}

### Killing Claws
{:#killing-claws}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases the Basic Attack's Final Damage by 4% when made from Stealth, Cover, or after moving at least 5 yards.

### Predator's Focus
{:#predator-s-focus}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, gain +1 Accuracy with the Basic Attack against a target that has not yet acted since the Panther's previous Action.

### Silent Paws
{:#silent-paws}

Type: Utility

Grant +3 to Stealth checks involving stalking, moving quietly or preparing an ambush with the panther.

### Night Hunter
{:#night-hunter}

Type: Utility

Ignore up to 4 points of darkness-based Visibility penalty for Awareness and movement.

### Pounce
{:#pounce-2}

Type: One-Time

When Pounce triggers, the Basic Attack also deals +10% Final Damage.

### Fade After Blood
{:#fade-after-blood-2}

Type: One-Time

Fade After Blood may move 2 yards instead of 1. If that movement ends in legal Cover or concealment, the Panther may immediately attempt to Hide.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-21}

### Shadow Pounce
{:#shadow-pounce}

Speed: 8

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Move up to 6 yards and Attack for 110% Monster Damage. If the Panther began the Action Hidden or behind Cover, gain +2 Accuracy.

### Throat Claw
{:#throat-claw}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 8

Effects: Attack for 125% Monster Damage. If the target is Prone, Grappled, or has not detected the Panther, increase this to 150% Monster Damage.

### Vanish Between Blows
{:#vanish-between-blows}

Speed: 4

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Move up to 4 yards to Cover or concealment and immediately attempt to Hide if the normal conditions for Hide are met. This Action does not by itself break existing Stealth.

### Nightfall Kill
{:#nightfall-kill}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Make an Attack for 160% Monster Damage at -2 Accuracy. If the Panther began this Action Hidden, remove the Accuracy penalty. On a full hit, inflict Bleed 4.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-21}

Panthers value autonomy, patience and a clean hunt. They object to confinement, humiliating busywork, and pointless cruelty. A trained Panther may be aloof, but responds well to clear purpose, room to stalk, and handlers who do not waste its effort.
