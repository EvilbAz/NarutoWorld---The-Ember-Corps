# Elk Species
{:#elk-species}

Role: Escort / Mobility

Elk are mobile guides and protective escorts. They excel at travel, terrain crossing, carrying allies and using antlers to redirect danger.

## Profile
{:#profile-16}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

Basic Attack: Speed 8 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: -1

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-17}

### Sure-Footed Guide
{:#sure-footed-guide}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Elk ignores mundane Movement penalties from shallow snow, mud, roots, undergrowth and steep natural slopes where a hoofed animal could reasonably pass. It may carry one willing human-sized rider at half Movement.

### Antler Catch
{:#antler-catch}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: After successfully Parrying a Melee Attack, the Elk may move the attacker 1 yard to either side of itself if a legal space exists.

### Escort Step
{:#escort-step}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: When the Elk takes a Move Action, one willing adjacent ally may move up to half the distance the Elk moved after it finishes, without changing that ally's Initiative. The ally must follow a legal path and end within 2 yards of the Elk.

### Protective Herd
{:#protective-herd}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: The Elk's rider and allies within 2 yards gain +1 Defence against Melee Attacks and forced-movement effects while the Elk is conscious and able to move.

### Great Crossing
{:#great-crossing}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Elk may carry up to two willing human-sized allies at full Movement. It ignores mundane Difficult Terrain and may cross ordinary gaps up to 2 yards without a check when a physically plausible landing exists.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-17}

### Long Stride
{:#long-stride}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases Movement by 0.1 yard per IC.

### Branching Guard
{:#branching-guard}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, gain +1 Parry against Melee Attacks made by creatures that moved at least 3 yards before attacking.

### Wayfinder
{:#wayfinder}

Type: Utility

Grant +3 to Survival checks for navigation, weather sense, finding safe trails or selecting an overland route.

### Sure-Footed
{:#sure-footed}

Type: Utility

Sure-Footed Guide also grants +3 Defence against forced movement and effects that would knock the Elk Prone while standing on natural terrain.

### Rider
{:#rider}

Type: One-Time

The Elk may carry one additional human-sized willing rider. Before Great Crossing, carrying the additional rider reduces Movement by half; Great Crossing removes this penalty.

### Antler Catch
{:#antler-catch-2}

Type: One-Time

Antler Catch also grants +2 Parry against Melee Attacks made by creatures that moved at least 3 yards immediately before attacking.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-16}

### Antler Guard
{:#antler-guard}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Range: 2 yards

Effects: Trigger: an ally is targeted by a Melee Attack. The elk moves up to 2 yards and Parries on that ally's behalf if it can interpose its antlers.

### Trailblazer
{:#trailblazer}

Speed: 9

Base Command AP: 5

Effects: The elk and one adjacent willing ally move up to 6 yards. That movement ignores mundane Difficult Terrain.

### Stampede Line
{:#stampede-line}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 9

Line: 10 yards

Effects: Move along the Line and Attack creatures in it for 90% Monster Damage. On a hit, inflict Knockback 4.

### Great Crossing
{:#great-crossing-2}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 11

Effects: The elk moves up to twice its normal Movement and may carry up to two willing human-sized allies during this movement. It can cross ordinary gaps, shallow water and rough natural terrain without checks if a physically plausible route exists.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-16}

Elk are herd-minded, route-oriented monsters that settle best with familiar trails and calm handling. Needless destruction of habitat, slaughter around them, or repeatedly forcing them to trample safe ground without purpose can trigger Rebellion.
