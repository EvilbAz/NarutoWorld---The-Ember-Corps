# Serpent Species
{:#serpent-species}

Role: Control / Poison

Serpents are control and counter monsters. They use venom, coils, shedding and patient positioning rather than raw mass.

## Profile
{:#profile-9}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 4 | 4 | 2 |

Basic Attack: Speed 10 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: +1

Movement: Ground; swims at half Movement

## Species Features
{:#species-features-10}

### Venomous Fangs
{:#venomous-fangs}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: When the Serpent's Basic Attack fully hits, the target must resist against Monster Save TN. On failure, inflict Poison 1 for 20 IC.

### Coiling Body
{:#coiling-body}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Serpent gains +2 on checks to initiate or maintain Grapples. It may pass through openings a human-sized creature could not, provided its body can physically fit.

### Constrictor
{:#constrictor}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: A creature Grappled by the Serpent suffers Immobilization 2. The Serpent's Basic Attacks against a creature it is Grappling deal +15% Final Damage.

### Shed Skin
{:#shed-skin}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Limit: Once per battle

Effects: At the start of the Serpent's Action, end one Grapple, Immobilization, Poison or Burns effect affecting it.

### King's Coil
{:#king-s-coil}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: The Serpent counts as one Size larger for Grapples. Creatures it Grapples suffer -2 Defence, and Venomous Fangs inflicts Poison 2 instead of Poison 1.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-10}

### Potent Venom
{:#potent-venom}

Type: Stackable

Each rank gives +1 to the Resistance TN of Poison inflicted by any Serpent Species Feature or Monster Technique. For every 3 ranks, those Poisons also gain +1 Severity.

### Flexible Spine
{:#flexible-spine}

Type: Stackable

Gain +1 Defence against Grapples, restraints and effects that would forcibly reposition the monster.

### Scent Tongue
{:#scent-tongue}

Type: Utility

The monster can track living creatures by scent and heat traces where plausible, granting +3 to one relevant Survival or Awareness check.

### Narrow Body
{:#narrow-body}

Type: Utility

The monster can pass through openings and terrain a human-sized creature could not, so long as its body could physically fit; it may carry only what its anatomy reasonably allows.

### Shed Skin
{:#shed-skin-2}

Type: One-Time

Shed Skin may also end Paralysis. After using it, the Serpent may move 1 yard without changing Initiative if a legal path exists.

### Coiling Guard
{:#coiling-guard}

Type: One-Time

Gain +2 Parry while Grappling a creature. On a successful Parry while Grappling, the monster may maintain its current Grapple without any additional check caused solely by that Attack.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-9}

### Venom Fang
{:#venom-fang}

Speed: 10

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Make an Attack for 90% Monster Damage. On a full hit, the target resists against Monster Save TN; on failure inflict Poison 2 for 20 IC.

### Coil
{:#coil}

Speed: 12

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Make an Attack for 70% Monster Damage. On a hit, attempt a Grapple. While the Grapple is maintained, the target suffers Immobilization 3.

### Shed Through
{:#shed-through}

Speed: 3 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 5

Effects: Trigger: the monster is hit by an Attack that would inflict a physical status or restraint. Reduce one inflicted Bleed, Immobilization or Paralysis by 2, minimum 0, after damage resolves.

### King's Constriction
{:#king-s-constriction}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 12

Requires: a Grappled target

Effects: Attack for 140% Monster Damage as Blunt damage. On a full hit, inflict Suffocation 2 and Immobilization 4 until the Grapple ends.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-9}

Serpents are cautious, reciprocal creatures. They respond best to patient handling and consistent signals. Deliberately betraying a trained Serpent's trust or using it to deceive allies it has learned to recognize can trigger Rebellion even when the immediate fight is already won.
