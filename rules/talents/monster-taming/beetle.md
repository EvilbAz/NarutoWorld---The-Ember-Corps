# Beetle Species
{:#beetle-species}

Role: Siege / Defence

Beetles are armoured laborers and siege bodies. They trade speed for carrying power, stubborn defence, burrowing and horn-driven control.

## Profile
{:#profile-13}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 2 | 4 | 1 |

Basic Attack: Speed 11 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Blunt

Command AP Modifier: +2

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-14}

### Carapace
{:#carapace}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: The Beetle gains 10 DR against physical damage.

### Burrower
{:#burrower}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Beetle may burrow through ordinary soil and loose earth at half Movement, leaving a crawlable tunnel when the terrain can support it.

### Horn Drive
{:#horn-drive}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: When the Beetle's Basic Attack fully hits, choose one: inflict Knockback 2, or move the target up to 2 yards to a legal space.

### Siege Body
{:#siege-body}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Effects: The Beetle counts as one Size larger for carrying, bracing and forced-movement contests. Its Attacks deal +50% Final Damage against unattended objects, Cover and mundane structures.

### Immovable Engine
{:#immovable-engine}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: Carapace becomes 15 DR against physical damage. The Beetle reduces Knockback suffered by 4, minimum 0, and gains +3 against forced movement. Destroying a mundane obstacle during its movement does not stop that movement if a legal path remains.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-14}

### Carapace Layers
{:#carapace-layers}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Horn Leverage
{:#horn-leverage}

Type: Stackable

For every 2 ranks, increase Knockback or forced movement caused by Beetle Monster Techniques by 1 yard.

### Burrower
{:#burrower-2}

Type: Utility

Burrower functions at normal Movement instead of half Movement and may also excavate packed ordinary earth when the terrain can support a tunnel.

### Pack Beast
{:#pack-beast}

Type: Utility

The monster can carry or drag up to two human-sized willing or unconscious creatures, or an equivalent mundane load, without slowing ordinary overland travel.

### Armoured Underside
{:#armoured-underside}

Type: One-Time

Increase the physical DR granted by Carapace by 5.

### Anchor Claws
{:#anchor-claws}

Type: One-Time

Immovable Engine reduces Knockback by an additional 2 and grants an additional +2 against forced movement.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-13}

### Horn Toss
{:#horn-toss}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Attack for 100% Monster Damage. On a full hit, move the target up to 4 yards and knock it Prone.

### Burrow Break
{:#burrow-break}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: If standing on diggable ground, the monster burrows and emerges within 6 yards. Its next Basic Attack before the end of the Action gains +2 Accuracy.

### Carapace Guard
{:#carapace-guard}

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Trigger: the monster or an adjacent ally is hit by a physical Attack. Reduce Final Damage by 20%; if protecting an ally, the monster takes unreducible Vitality damage equal to half the amount prevented.

### Siege Horn
{:#siege-horn}

Speed: 16

Base Command AP: 12

Line: 10 yards

Effects: Move through the Line and Attack each creature for 110% Monster Damage. Unattended objects and Cover take +75% Final Damage; a destroyed mundane obstacle does not stop the movement.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-13}

Beetles are steady working monsters that respond well to repeated hauling, digging and breaching routines. Needless destruction or constantly abandoning trained tasks halfway through for no reason can make them increasingly difficult to handle.
