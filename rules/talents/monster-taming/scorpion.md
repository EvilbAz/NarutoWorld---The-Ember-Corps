# Scorpion Species
{:#scorpion-species}

Role: Control / Poison

Scorpions are punishing control monsters with armour, pincers and venomous counterattacks. They are slow but dangerous to approach carelessly.

## Profile
{:#profile-14}

| Toughness | Aggression | Defence | Agility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 4 | 4 | 1 |

Basic Attack: Speed 11 | Melee | 100% Monster Damage | Piercing

Command AP Modifier: +2

Movement: Ground

## Species Features
{:#species-features-15}

### Venom Tail
{:#venom-tail}

Unlock: Core Talent

Effects: When the Scorpion's Basic Attack fully hits, the target must resist against Monster Save TN. On failure, inflict Poison 1 for 20 IC.

### Pincer Guard
{:#pincer-guard}

Unlock: Development 1 — Lesser Feature

Effects: The Scorpion gains +1 Parry against Melee Attacks and +1 on checks to maintain Grapples.

### Vibration Hunter
{:#vibration-hunter}

Unlock: Development 2 — Greater Feature

Effects: While touching the same solid ground, the Scorpion can detect moving creatures within 8 yards despite darkness or light concealment. This reveals location and movement, but not identity or detailed information through solid barriers.

### Retaliatory Sting
{:#retaliatory-sting}

Unlock: Development 3 — Elite Feature

Speed: 4 Interrupt

Trigger: The Scorpion successfully Parries a Melee Attack from a creature within reach.

Effects: Make an immediate Attack against that creature for 70% Monster Damage. A full hit applies Venom Tail.

### Deathstalker
{:#deathstalker}

Unlock: Development 4 — Elder Feature

Effects: Venom Tail inflicts Poison 2 instead of Poison 1. When the Scorpion's Basic Attack fully hits a Poisoned creature, it may immediately attempt a Grapple for no Command AP and no additional Speed.

## Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits-15}

### Venom Gland
{:#venom-gland}

Type: Stackable

Each rank gives +1 to the Resistance TN of Poison inflicted by any Scorpion Species Feature or Monster Technique. For every 3 ranks, those Poisons also gain +1 Severity.

### Chitin Plates
{:#chitin-plates}

Type: Stackable

Each rank increases maximum Vitality by 4% of the monster's unmodified maximum Vitality.

### Sand Walker
{:#sand-walker}

Type: Utility

Loose sand, scree and shallow rubble do not reduce the monster's Movement, and it can bury itself in loose ground outside Initiative when physically possible.

### Vibration Sense
{:#vibration-sense}

Type: Utility

Vibration Hunter's range increases to 15 yards and grants +3 Awareness when identifying or locating a creature detected through those vibrations.

### Pincer Guard
{:#pincer-guard-2}

Type: One-Time

Pincer Guard grants +2 Parry and +2 to maintain Grapples instead of +1 to each.

### Raised Tail
{:#raised-tail}

Type: One-Time

After the monster successfully Parries a Melee Attack, its next Monster Technique against that attacker gains +2 Accuracy before its next Action ends.

## Monster Techniques
{:#monster-techniques-14}

### Venom Sting
{:#venom-sting}

Speed: 11

Base Command AP: 6

Effects: Attack for 90% Monster Damage. On a full hit, target resists against Monster Save TN; on failure, inflict Poison 2 for 20 IC.

### Pincer Lock
{:#pincer-lock}

Speed: 13

Base Command AP: 7

Effects: Attack for 70% Monster Damage and attempt a Grapple on hit. While maintained, the target suffers -2 Dodge.

### Sand Burial
{:#sand-burial}

Speed: 14

Base Command AP: 7

Requires: loose soil, sand or rubble

Effects: Move up to 5 yards underground and emerge; creatures adjacent to the emergence point suffer Immobilization 2 for 10 IC on a failed Defence against the monster's Accuracy.

### Deathstalker
{:#deathstalker-2}

Speed: 15

Base Command AP: 12

Effects: Make two Attacks against one target: a pincer strike for 70% Monster Damage and a sting for 80%. If both hit, the target must resist Poison 4 for 20 IC.

## Temperament
{:#temperament-14}

Scorpions are defensive, deliberate and conservative with their venom. They respond poorly to careless handling around allies, pointless attacks on helpless creatures, or repeated commands to waste venom purely for intimidation.
