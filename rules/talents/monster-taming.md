# Monster Taming
{:#monster-taming-2}

Monster Taming is the practice of acquiring, training and fielding dangerous creatures. Your monster might be captured during a Hunt, raised from an egg or young, bred for work, rescued, purchased, inherited, or obtained through another appropriate method. However you found it, the result is one named Tamed Monster that grows alongside you.

Your Tamed Monster is a separate creature with its own position, Initiative, Vitality and IC track. Its Independent Actions do not spend your AP; Monster Techniques spend Command AP from your AP pool.

## At a Glance
{:#at-a-glance}

- At 1,000 Total XP, your Tamed Monster has Growth Rank 1 and one Monster Trait.

- At 1,500 Total XP it gains a second Trait; at 2,000 it gains a third; it gains another Growth Rank and Trait every 500 Total XP thereafter.

- Every Species has Toughness, Aggression, Defence and Agility ratings. The four ratings total 12.

- Every monster Action has its own printed Speed. Agility never changes an Action's Speed.

- Agility affects Movement, Initiative and the Command AP you pay for Monster Techniques.

- Basic Attacks and Independent Actions do not spend your AP. Monster Techniques spend Command AP.

- Each Monster Taming Development grants one Tamer Development, one Monster Technique, and the next Species Feature. Three Developments grant Master Handler.

# Talent Progression
{:#talent-progression}

Monster Taming is a Capability Talent. Your Source must plausibly give you access to a suitable creature, egg or young, breeder, handler, captured specimen, or another way to acquire and train the monster. Beast Seal: Release is the field-sealing technique used to safely transport and deploy it.

## Monster Taming
{:#monster-taming-3}

### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-7}

Choose one Standard Monster Species and create one named Tamed Monster using its profile. Decide how you acquired it: capture, breeding, rescue, purchase, inheritance, reward, or another appropriate method. The method does not change its statistics. Your monster immediately gains that Species' Core Instinct. You may purchase Beast Seal: Release without meeting its normal rank requirement. While your monster is deployed, its prepared Beast Seal lets you sense its rough direction and whether it is conscious, incapacitated, or in immediate danger. You and your monster may communicate simple trained intent whenever normal communication is possible.

### Developments
{:#developments-7}

Each time you take a Monster Taming Development, choose one Tamer Development below, learn one Monster Technique from your monster's Species, and unlock its next Species Feature. You may take each Tamer Development and learn each Monster Technique only once unless stated otherwise. A Standard Species has four Monster Techniques and four Development-locked Species Features. You may normally take at most four Monster Taming Developments.

### Quick Release
{:#quick-release}

Effects: When using Beast Seal: Release to deploy your Tamed Monster, its Chakra cost becomes 20, its final Speed becomes 12, and its final Seal Speed becomes 12. If released after Initiative has begun, its first normal Action occurs 7 IC after appearing instead of 10 IC.

### Efficient Command
{:#efficient-command}

Effects: Reduce the Command AP cost of your Tamed Monster's Monster Techniques by 1 after applying Agility, minimum 2.

### Coordinated Assault
{:#coordinated-assault}

Effects: When your Tamed Monster fully hits a creature with an Attack, your next Attack against that creature before the end of your next Non-Interrupt Action gains +2 Accuracy. This benefit cannot stack with itself.

### Handler's Read
{:#handler-s-read}

Effects: Gain +3 Awareness and Survival when your Tamed Monster can meaningfully assist through its senses, tracking ability or position. You may act on information your monster communicates from things it can perceive but you cannot. This does not remove Visibility, Cover or other penalties from your own Attacks.

### Protective Training
{:#protective-training}

Effects: While you are within 3 yards of your Tamed Monster, both of you gain +1 to Defensive Rolls. This bonus ends while either creature is unconscious, helpless or otherwise unable to protect the other.

### Emergency Release
{:#emergency-release}

Limit: Once per battle

Effects: When using Beast Seal: Release to deploy your Tamed Monster, reduce its final Speed and final Seal Speed by 5, minimum 1. This stacks with Quick Release.

### Homeward Seal
{:#homeward-seal}

Effects: During a Week's Rest, establish a return formula at a permanent safe location where your Tamed Monster is normally kept, such as a kennel, stable, den, breeder's compound or laboratory. With enough uninterrupted time to perform the ritual, you may transport yourself there along with a number of willing creatures touching you up to 1 + your Monster Taming Developments. Homeward Seal is travel, not a combat teleport, and cannot normally be performed during Initiative.

### Mastery — Master Handler
{:#mastery-master-handler}

After taking three Monster Taming Developments, gain Master Handler. Once per battle, when your Tamed Monster declares a Monster Technique, you may ignore its Command AP cost. Its Speed and all other requirements are unchanged. When you do, choose one: gain +2 Accuracy on your next Attack before the end of your next Action; gain +2 to Defensive Rolls until the end of your next Action; or move up to 2 yards after the Technique resolves without spending AP or changing Initiative.

Monster Taming does not grant a second Tamed Monster or an Apex-class Monster. If another reward or rule grants you an additional Tamed Monster, create it normally. You may still have only one Standard Tamed Monster deployed at a time unless a rule explicitly says otherwise.

# Fielding Your Monster
{:#fielding-your-monster}

## Beast Seal: Release
{:#beast-seal-release}

Chakra: 25

Speed: 15

Seal Speed: 15

Range: 2 yards

Tags: Utility, Sealing

Effects: Release your Tamed Monster from its prepared Beast Seal into an unoccupied space within Range. The Beast Seal is a trained transport and safety tool; it does not control the monster's mind or force obedience.

A Tamed Monster remains deployed until recalled, reduced to 0 Vitality, or the scene ends. You may recall it as a Speed 5 Action; the monster may voluntarily return to its Beast Seal as a Speed 5 Action. Recalling and releasing it again during the same scene does not restore Vitality, remove statuses, or refresh once-per-battle effects.

## Entering Initiative
{:#entering-initiative}

If your Tamed Monster is already present when Initiative is rolled, roll its Initiative normally. If it is released after Initiative begins, its first normal Action occurs 10 IC after it appears. It may use valid defensive Interrupts before then; those Interrupts advance its next Action normally.

## Defeat and Recovery
{:#defeat-and-recovery}

A Tamed Monster reduced to 0 Vitality is immediately pulled back into its Beast Seal by the seal's safety tether. You cannot deploy it again until you complete a Week's Rest, at which point it returns at full Vitality. Reaching 0 Vitality means the monster is too injured to continue fighting; it does not automatically die. Permanent death requires an explicit story event rather than ordinary combat damage.

# Growth
{:#growth}

Your Tamed Monster uses your Total XP. Growth Rank begins at 1 when you reach 1,000 Total XP and increases by 1 for every additional 500 Total XP.

Growth Rank: 1 + floor((Total XP - 1,000) / 500), minimum 1

Growth Rank is also the number of Monster Traits your monster has earned. If you learn Monster Taming after 1,000 Total XP, immediately select every Trait your monster currently qualifies for.

| Total XP | Growth Rank | Monster Traits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1 | 1 |
| 1,500 | 2 | 2 |
| 2,000 | 3 | 3 |
| 2,500 | 4 | 4 |
| 3,000 | 5 | 5 |
| 3,500 | 6 | 6 |
| 4,000 | 7 | 7 |
| 4,500 | 8 | 8 |
| 5,000 | 9 | 9 |
| 5,500 | 10 | 10 |
| 6,000 | 11 | 11 |
| 6,500 | 12 | 12 |
| Every +500 XP | +1 | +1 |

# The Four Species Ratings
{:#the-four-species-ratings}

Every Standard Species has four ratings from 1 to 5, totaling 12: Toughness, Aggression, Defence and Agility. These ratings define the Species' base profile; Traits customize the individual partner.

## Derived Profile
{:#derived-profile}

Base Vitality: 650 + 125 × (Growth Rank - 1), then apply Toughness

Base Accuracy: 21 + (2 × Growth Rank) + 1 for every 2 Growth Ranks, to a maximum of +6; then apply Aggression.

Base Defence: 14 + (2 × Growth Rank) + 1 for every 2 Growth Ranks after the first, to a maximum of +4; then apply Defence.

Movement: Agility base + 0.1 yard per IC for each Growth Rank after the first

Initiative: 1d20 + Agility contribution + floor(Growth Rank / 3)

Monster Skill Bonus: 8 + (2 × Growth Rank)

Monster Save TN: 17 + (2 × Growth Rank)

The Defence value is used for all of the monster's defensive rolls, including Dodge and Parry. A partner initiating a Grapple uses Accuracy; one resisting or maintaining control against a hostile Grapple uses Defence, unless a Trait or Technique says otherwise.

| Rating | Toughness | Aggression / Defence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 75% Vitality | -4 |
| 2 | 90% Vitality | -2 |
| 3 | 100% Vitality | +0 |
| 4 | 115% Vitality | +2 |
| 5 | 130% Vitality | +4 |

Agility uses the same 1-5 rating, but its effects are shown separately:

| Agility | Base Move | Command AP | Initiative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1.0 yd/IC | +2 | +2 |
| 2 | 1.2 yd/IC | +1 | +4 |
| 3 | 1.4 yd/IC | +0 | +6 |
| 4 | 1.6 yd/IC | -1 | +8 |
| 5 | 1.8 yd/IC | -2 | +10 |

Command AP modifiers apply only to Monster Techniques and other effects that explicitly list a Command AP cost. The adjusted cost cannot be lower than 2 AP. Agility never changes the Speed of an Action.

# Actions and Command AP
{:#actions-and-command-ap}

## Independent Actions
{:#independent-actions}

A Tamed Monster has its own IC track and no AP pool of its own. It may use Move, Dodge, Parry, Search, Point Out, Hide when legal, stand up, ordinary object interactions, and its printed Basic Attack without spending your AP. These Actions use their normal or printed Speed and advance the monster on its IC track normally.

A Tamed Monster can Parry with its natural weapons as though they were appropriate unarmed weapons. It cannot use player equipment, Jutsu, Talents, Uniques or Clan abilities unless a Species rule explicitly grants them.

## Command AP
{:#command-ap}

Monster Techniques are coordinated trained Monster Actions. When your monster declares one, you pay its listed Base Command AP from your AP pool, modified by the Species' Agility. Paying Command AP does not take an Action and does not change your Initiative.

Command AP is a cost, not AP spent to reduce Speed. Neither you nor your monster may spend AP to reduce a monster Action's Speed or Surge a monster roll unless a rule explicitly says otherwise. Command AP still counts as AP you spent for effects that care about AP expenditure.

If you cannot pay the Command AP, the Monster Technique cannot be declared. If you are unconscious or otherwise unable to direct your monster, your monster may continue to use Independent Actions and its Basic Attack, but cannot use Monster Techniques. Outside Initiative, ignore Command AP costs.

# Monster Damage
{:#monster-damage}

Tamed Monsters use the normal Ember Corps damage structure: Damage Dice + (Damage Bonus × Attack Speed). Their Damage Dice and Monster Damage Bonus come from Growth Rank rather than Attributes.

Monster Damage Dice: (4 + ceiling(Growth Rank / 2))d8

Monster Damage Bonus (MDB): 3 + (Growth Rank / 2)

When an Attack deals 100% Monster Damage, roll the Monster Damage Dice and add MDB × that Attack's Speed. If an effect deals 70%, 120%, or another percentage of Monster Damage, calculate 100% Monster Damage first, then apply the listed percentage to Final Damage, rounding down.

| Total XP | Growth Rank | Damage Dice | MDB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 1 | 5d8 | 3.5 |
| 2,500 | 4 | 6d8 | 5.0 |
| 4,000 | 7 | 8d8 | 6.5 |
| 6,500 | 12 | 10d8 | 9.0 |

Each Species profile lists its Basic Attack Speed. Every Species Feature and Monster Technique that grants an Action lists its own Speed; use the Speed printed on that Action.

# Monster Rules
{:#monster-rules}

- A Tamed Monster is a living creature and can be targeted by effects that target creatures or allies. It does not inherit your Attributes, Accuracy, Defence, Damage Bonuses, equipment, Talents, Uniques, Clan effects or temporary modifiers unless a rule explicitly says so.

- A Tamed Monster uses Vitality instead of HP or Wounds. At 0 Vitality its safety seal recalls it. It does not suffer Wounds, and effects that require the target to suffer a Wound do not trigger against it.

- Bleed, Burns, Poison, Paralysis, Immobilization, Suffocation and other statuses affect a Tamed Monster normally unless a Species rule says otherwise.

- Tamed Monsters are Tireless. They do not make Stamina or Chakra Exhaustion rolls for their listed Actions. External effects may still inflict statuses or penalties normally.

- A Standard Tamed Monster is mechanically human-sized for reach, targeting, Grapple and carrying rules unless a Species Feature, Trait or Technique says otherwise. Its appearance may be larger or smaller without changing its mechanical Size.

- Natural claws, teeth, horns, shells and similar anatomy cannot be Disarmed. Special movement modes are listed in the Species profile or granted by Species Features and Traits.

- You may have only one Standard Tamed Monster active at a time unless a rule explicitly overrides this limit.

# Species Features
{:#species-features}

Every Standard Species has five innate Species Features. You gain the Core Instinct with the Monster Taming Core Talent. Monster Taming Developments 1–4 then unlock the Lesser, Greater, Elite and Elder Features in order.

Species Features are part of your monster's profile, not learned Monster Techniques. They cost no Command AP unless stated otherwise. Actions and Interrupts granted by Species Features are Independent Actions and remain available even if you cannot issue commands.

# Monster Traits
{:#monster-traits}

Every Growth Rank grants one Trait from the monster's Species. Traits are chosen when earned and are permanent unless a rebuild or rules update allows them to change.

## Stackable Trait
{:#stackable-trait}

May be selected any number of times. Each selection adds one rank, and all ranks stack exactly as written.

## Utility Trait
{:#utility-trait}

May normally be selected once. If a Utility Trait asks you to choose from several options, you may select it again only to take a different option unless it explicitly says otherwise.

## One-Time Trait
{:#one-time-trait}

May be selected once. "One-Time" limits how often you may select the Trait, not how often its effect may be used. Any use limit is stated in the Trait itself.

# Temperament and Rebellion
{:#temperament-and-rebellion}

A Tamed Monster is a living creature, not equipment or a mind-controlled tool. Ordinary danger, injury, defeat, retreat, or an imperfect command does not cause Rebellion. A trained monster can be expected to take real risks beside its handler.

Rebellion occurs if you deliberately attack your own Tamed Monster, knowingly use it as a disposable suicide piece when a reasonable alternative exists, or knowingly violate its Temperament. Accidental friendly fire does not automatically cause Rebellion; intent and obvious disregard matter.

On Rebellion, your monster withdraws into its Beast Seal or otherwise refuses to continue working with you, and cannot be fielded again until your next Week's Rest. Repeated or extreme mistreatment may have story consequences, but permanent loss of a paid Talent should only occur through an agreed story event or rebuild.
