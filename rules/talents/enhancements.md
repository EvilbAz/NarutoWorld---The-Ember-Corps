## Enhancements
{:#enhancements}

### Celerity
{:#celerity}

You are not merely fast on your feet. Your entire combat rhythm happens a fraction of a second ahead of everyone else's.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent}

All of your Actions and Interrupts have their final Speed reduced by 1. This cannot reduce the Speed of an Interrupt below 3. You may choose not to apply this reduction, or only partially apply it, to a given Action.

#### Developments
{:#developments}

Quick Hands — Actions primarily involving drawing, stowing, reloading, manipulating equipment, or performing handseals have their final Speed reduced by an additional 1, minimum 1.

Quick Feet — After resolving a non-Movement Action, you may move up to 2 yards without AP or additional Speed. This is still movement and must follow a legal path.

Quick Reflexes — Defensive Interrupts have their final Speed reduced by an additional 1, but cannot be reduced below 3 by this Development.

Flow State — If your current non-Interrupt Action has a different primary purpose from your previous non-Interrupt Action — Attack, Defense, Utility, or Movement — reduce its final Speed by 1, minimum 1.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery}

Too Fast to Follow — The Core Talent reduction from Celerity becomes 2 instead of 1. The normal minimums still apply.

### Generalist
{:#generalist}

You have never been satisfied doing only one job. Somewhere along the way you picked up a worrying number of other people's tricks.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-2}

Gain any two non-Automatic Rank Benefits you currently or previously qualified for.

#### Developments
{:#developments-2}

Cross-Training — Gain one additional non-Automatic Rank Benefit you currently or previously qualify for. You may select this Development only once.

Broad Curriculum — Gain an additional XP/300 Skill Points. This scales as your total XP increases.

Improvised Expertise — Once per scene, after making a Skill Check with a Skill in which you do not possess Specialization, add +5 before the result is resolved.

Reassignment — During a Week's Rest, you may replace one Rank Benefit granted by Generalist with another non-Automatic Rank Benefit you currently or previously qualify for.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-2}

Master of None — Gain one additional non-Automatic Rank Benefit you currently or previously qualify for. During each Week's Rest, you may now replace any number of Rank Benefits granted by Generalist rather than only one.

### Prodigy
{:#prodigy}

Some Hunters need years before a lesson becomes instinct. You have the irritating habit of arriving there first.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-3}

Gain one non-Automatic Rank Benefit from any Rank, including a Rank you have never held.

#### Developments
{:#developments-3}

Precocious — Gain one additional non-Automatic Rank Benefit from one Rank above your current Rank or lower.

Fast Study — Reduce the required training time for learning Jutsu and Capability Talents by 25%, minimum one training action or the shortest unit the relevant rule normally permits.

Seen It Once — Once per Mission or Hunt, after you witness another character successfully perform a non-Heritage Jutsu, Skill use, or combat technique, gain +3 to your next attempt at the same broad type of action during that scene.

Unfairly Talented — Whenever you spend Willpower to reroll a Skill Check or a roll made specifically to learn, imitate, understand, or reproduce a technique, add +3 to the reroll.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-3}

Ahead of the Curve — Gain one additional non-Automatic Rank Benefit from any Rank. Prodigy still never grants Automatic Rank Benefits.

### Skill Specialist
{:#skill-specialist}

You did not merely learn a skill. You kept going until everyone else started sending the difficult cases to you.

#### Core Talent
{:#core-talent-4}

Gain an additional XP/200 Skill Points. This scales as your total XP increases.

#### Developments
{:#developments-4}

Deep Study — Choose one Skill. Increase your maximum ranks in that Skill by 2.

Specialized Practice — Choose one Skill in which you possess Specialization. When your Specialization directly applies, gain an additional +2.

Reliable — Choose one Skill. Once per scene, when the natural d20 result for that Skill is lower than 7, treat the die as though it rolled a 7.

Applied Expertise — Once per scene, after succeeding on a Skill Check, you may grant yourself or an ally who can act on your work +3 to one directly related Skill Check made before the end of the scene.

#### Mastery
{:#mastery-4}

Recognized Expert — Choose one Skill when you gain this Mastery. Once per scene, after rolling that Skill but before the result is resolved, you may treat the natural d20 result as a 15.
