# Uniques
{:#uniques}

Uniques are unusual talents, habits, gifts, and kinds of training that set one shinobi apart from another. They grant abilities and advantages that ordinary XP purchases usually cannot.

At character creation you receive 6 Unique Points (UP). Heritage Cost from Volume III is paid from this same pool. Any UP you do not spend may be saved.

Creation Uniques cost 1 UP each, may only be taken during character creation, and you may have no more than two. Clan Creation Uniques follow the same rules and count toward that maximum.

After character creation, gain 1 additional UP at 2,000, 3,000, 4,500, 6,000, and 7,000 total XP. UP cannot be spent during combat or an event.

Some Uniques list Upgrades Into or Upgrades From. If you own the earlier Unique, you may replace it with the later one by paying only the difference in UP cost. The old Unique and its effects are removed and the new Unique takes its place. You never have to follow an upgrade path; if you can take the later Unique normally, you may buy it directly for its full cost. Buying a later Unique does not grant a Creation Unique you did not choose at character creation. 

Unless an entry says otherwise, a Unique may be taken only once.

## Creation Uniques

### Analyst
{:#analyst}

***Upgrades Into:** Applied Knowledge*

*You have a habit of watching before you act. Gain \+5 Awareness. Rather than gaining AP every 20 IC in combat, you gain 60% as much (rounded up) every 10 IC. When the same creature uses the same broad kind of Attack against you consecutively—Katon into Katon, Weapon Taijutsu into Weapon Taijutsu, and so on—gain \+1 on your Defensive roll and 1 AP, stacking for each consecutive use. Track this separately for each attacker; changing attack type resets it.*

*You gain 5 Research ranks and 5 to your Max Research Ranks. Your tendency to stop and think gives you \-4 Athletics and \-2 to Dodge.*

*Additionally, you reduce the Downtime TN of any roll that would use your Research by an amount equal to your Research Ranks \+ 10\.*

### Berserker
{:#berserker}

*You are difficult to frighten, difficult to stop, and not especially interested in avoiding a clean hit when you can answer with one of your own. Add RES × 2 to maximum Vitality.*

*Berserk Counter — Speed 0 Interrupt: when an enemy attacks you, you may take no Defense. If the Attack does not incapacitate you, immediately use a Taijutsu Attack you know against that attacker. The counter gains \+3 Accuracy, is made at half its normal final Speed, rounded up, to a minimum of 3, cannot have its Speed reduced further, and its Base Speed cannot exceed the Base Speed of the Attack that triggered it. Pay all normal costs.*

*For every 25% of maximum Vitality you are missing, your Taijutsu deals \+5% Final Damage, maximum \+20%. When reduced to 10%  Vitality or blow, choose whether to surrender or keep fighting; if you keep fighting, you cannot willingly stop until every hostile creature you are fighting has been incapacitated or you are. Suffer \-5 Stealth.*

### Chakra Sensitive
{:#chakra-sensitive}

*Even before formal sensor training, chakra has always been something you could feel. Increase current and maximum Chakra Control ranks by 3\.*

*Your passive sensing range is CHA × 2 yards. Within it, you can tell when an unfamiliar chakra source appears or disappears, feel whether a visible creature is maintaining Upkeep, and recognize a chakra signature you have personally sensed before.*

*Scan — Speed 8: make Chakra Control opposed by Chakra Control or Espionage against any creatures you choose in range. A creature suffers a penalty equal to the total Upkeep it is maintaining. On success, know its exact location and whether its chakra feels weaker, stronger, or roughly comparable to yours. A creature you detect this way is not Hidden from you until the end of your next Action unless an effect specifically conceals chakra sensing. Suffer \-4 Resistance.*

*Upgrades Into: Chakra Sensor*

### Chosen One
{:#chosen-one}

*You were always special. **The** special , one could say \- no matter how much you may dream of normalcy.* 

*You have something special about you \- and that’s always been clear. This Unique may be traded for a unique, special power that only your character has\! Discuss with the Storyteller team with what exactly you’re hoping for, and ensure you have open and clear communication with them regarding your expectations. The scope of this Unique should remain in the **Talent** power level, without becoming something overtly oppressive.* 

*To apply for this Unique, write a story. Consider what makes your character special. It should be something immediately identifiable. Imagine your character is in a Manga or Anime, and this special ability / power / thing is what you think of when you think of the character.*

### Competitive
{:#competitive}

*Winning matters to you more than it probably should. Gain \+2 on d20 rolls and \+5% Final Damage with Attacks.*

*Whenever you miss with an Attack, gain \+1 Accuracy and suffer \-1 on other d20 rolls, stacking to \+5 Accuracy and \-5 on other d20 rolls. Both reset when you hit with an Attack. Whenever your Fatigue level increases, gain 5 AP and \+5 on your next Stamina or Chakra Exhaustion roll before the end of your next Action.*

### Defender
{:#defender}

*You have trained to make hitting you a poor decision. Gain DR equal to RES/3 and Block becomes a Speed 4 Interrupt. Whenever a damage-dealing adjacent unarmed Attack hits you, the attacker takes RES/2 damage after its Attack resolves; this damage cannot Wound.* 

*When you block an attack, if your last action was also a block against an attack of the same broad discipline, increase the block effectiveness by 10%, this stacks up to 5 times. If this would, for whatever reason, take you above 100% block effectiveness, any negative damage heals your VIT at 50% effectiveness. So at 110% damage reduction, if you blocked 100 Damage, you would recover 5 VIT. This cannot take you above 100% VIT.*

*Treat damage you take as 10% lower when determining Wounds, or 25% lower when you Block. Your caution carries into your offense: your Attacks count as 10% lower for determining Wounds.*

*Upgrades Into: Protector*

### Energetic
{:#energetic}

*You run longer, fight longer, and recover faster than most people can tolerate being around. You have 6 Fatigue levels instead of 5 and suffer ordinary Fatigue penalties as though you were one category lower while keeping the normal benefits of your actual Fatigue. Whenever you Rest, reduce Stamina Penalty by 1 additional point.*

*Your inability to sit still makes careful study harder. Reduce the maximum rank of every Skill by 2\.*

### Genius
{:#genius}

*Ninja techniques make sense to you in a way they simply do not to most people. Reduce the XP cost of Jutsu you learn by 25%, rounded up. You may learn Jutsu one Rank higher than your normal Rank limit, but this never bypasses a Talent, Ability, Aptitude, Heritage, or technique-specific requirement.*

*Additionally, you gain advantage (2d20kh1) when rolling to make any roll relating to learning new things.* 

*A Jutsu you could not use without Genius costs \+3 Chakra or Stamina each time you use it.*

### Juggernaut
{:#juggernaut}

*You are built to absorb punishment and keep advancing. Use STR/14 instead of STR/18 for Physical Damage Bonus. Gain DR equal to STR/5 and reduce Stun applied to you by STR/20, after other reductions.*

*All movement you receive is reduced by 10%, including ordinary movement, movement from Jutsu, and forced movement.*

### Large
{:#large}

*You are bigger, heavier, or simply built on a scale most shinobi are not. Use STR/10  instead of STR/18 for Physical Damage Bonus. Physical Attacks gain \+1 yard Reach, add RES to maximum Vitality and maximum HP, and gain \+3 on Stamina rolls.*

*Attacks benefiting from this Unique have \+1 Base Speed, and gain 1 Delay.*

*Incompatible With: Small*

### Lucky
{:#lucky}

*Things have an irritating habit of working out for you. Outside combat, roll 2d20 and keep the higher result on ordinary d20 rolls.*

*You have 2 \+ total XP/750 Luck rerolls each in-character day, rounded down. Spend 1 after a roll to reroll your own eligible roll or a willing ally's; spend 2 to force a hostile creature to reroll an eligible roll. When you have no Luck rerolls remaining, suffer \-2 Accuracy and \-2 on d20 rolls until they refresh.*

### Naturalist
{:#naturalist}

*You understand the living world better than books ever taught you. Increase maximum Survival ranks by 3 and gain \+3 to detect, disarm, or avoid traps. Use CHA/13 instead of CHA/18 for Ninjutsu Damage Bonus. Gain \+1 on Chakra Exhaustion rolls per Rank of the Jutsu being used. You also gain the ability to communicate simple ideas with beasts, creatures and natural animals such as birds and bears.*

*When rolling checks related to studying non-human creatures, you gain advantage.* 

*Survival may replace Research for prerequisites of ordinary Elemental Ninjutsu when field knowledge is relevant. Reduce maximum Research ranks by 3\.*

*You also suffer a \-3 to all Genjutsu Rolls*

### Nimble
{:#nimble}

*Your body is built around never being where the hit lands. Gain \+1 Accuracy and \+1 on Defensive rolls. Reduce Base Speed of your non-Variable, non-Interrupt Actions by 1, minimum 3\.*

*When you are hit cleanly, however, it hurts. Damage against you counts as 10% higher when determining Wounds.*

### Small
{:#small}

*You are short, light, and very difficult to pin down. Gain \+2 Dodge and your Dodge Interrupt is Speed 1\. If an effect raises Dodge Speed, you may still use Dodge even when that increase would normally make it too slow to be a valid Defense. Halve the Dodge penalty caused by being unable to reach the edge of an Area Attack, maximum penalty \-10.*

*Reduce maximum Vitality by RES.*

*Incompatible With: Large*

### Skilled Shinobi
{:#skilled-shinobi}

*You are not necessarily the most dangerous person in the room, but there are very few situations where you have nothing useful to contribute. Gain additional Skill Points equal to total XP/75, rounded down, and increase the maximum rank of every Skill by XP/500 , minimum 0\.*

*Suffer \-1 Accuracy.*

### Specialist
{:#specialist}

*You have chosen one part of shinobi combat and pushed it further than most people ever will. Choose Taijutsu, Ninjutsu, or Genjutsu as your Specialty. Choose one of the remaining two as your Incompetence.*

*Taijutsu Specialty: \+2 Taijutsu Accuracy, \-2 Stamina cost with Taijutsu and Kenjutsu, minimum 1, and \+10% Final Damage with them.*

*Ninjutsu Specialty: \+2 Ninjutsu Accuracy, \-2 Chakra cost with Ninjutsu, minimum 1, and \+10% Final Damage with them.*

*Genjutsu Specialty: \+3 on Genjutsu offense rolls and TNs, \-2 Chakra cost with Genjutsu, minimum 1, and \+10% Final Damage where applicable.*

*Your Incompetence suffers \-2 Accuracy, or \-3 Genjutsu offense, costs \+2 Chakra or Stamina, and deals \-10% Final Damage where applicable. You also suffer a \-2 to all defensive rolls against your Incompetence, or \-3 to Genjutsu Defence.*

*Upgrades Into: Pinnacle*

### Strong-Willed
{:#strong-willed}

*When things go wrong, you have a habit of continuing anyway. Reduce the total temporary penalty to any Accuracy, Stamina, Chakra Exhaustion, or other d20 roll by 1\. This does not reduce penalties printed as part of the Action or Jutsu itself, nor penalties from another Unique.*

*You may ignore the mechanical penalties of one Wound you currently possess. When you suffer a new Wound, choose whether to continue ignoring the old one or ignore the new one instead. Gain \+2 Genjutsu Defense. After failing Genjutsu Defense against a Genjutsu you recognized, spend 1 Willpower as a Speed 0 Interrupt to immediately end that Genjutsu on yourself.*

*You cannot suffer from fear-based penalties.*

*Upgrades Into: Surging Power*

### Surgeon
{:#surgeon}

*You know exactly where a body fails. Damaging Attacks count as \+20% higher for determining Wounds. Non-AoE Called Shots suffer no additional Accuracy penalty. When making a Called Shot, you may declare a maximum Wound severity, including none; a Wound above that maximum is reduced by one severity.*

*Begin with 5 Medicine ranks, or raise Medicine to 5 if it was lower, without spending Skill Points. Gain another Medicine rank for every full 200 total XP, up to your normal maximum. Suffer \-1 on Defensive rolls.*

*Upgrades Into: Combat Medic*

### Warder
{:#warder}

*You have trained to meet attacks head-on and brush them aside. Parry becomes a Speed 2 Interrupt and gains \+2. Jutsu-based defenses that use an Accuracy roll gain \+2 Accuracy and reduce Base Speed by 2, minimum 1\. You may Parry weapons with your bare hands and Parry unarmed Attacks with a weapon.*

*Whenever you parry an attack with your hands or a weapon, you may immediately make an attack of a speed equal to your Parry’s Final Speed, with an Accuracy of your Parry Roll. This attack deals damage equal to your Relevant Damage Bonus \* the final speed of the attack.*

*Suffer \-4 Dodge.*

*Upgrades From: Elementalist*

### False World
{:#false-world}

*Once your Genjutsu gets a foothold, reality becomes negotiable. Whenever a creature fails Genjutsu Defense against you, place 1 Thread on it, maximum 5\. Before another Genjutsu roll against that creature, spend up to 3 Threads; each gives \+1 to your roll or \-1 Seal Speed. When a creature successfully Realizes or releases one of your Genjutsu, keep 1 Thread on it.*

*Once per battle, when a creature with 4+ Threads fails Genjutsu Defense, spend 4 Threads and 1 Willpower to immediately establish a different C-rank or lower Genjutsu you know against it. Pay all Chakra and Upkeep, but not the second technique's Action or Seal Speed. Add \+10 final Speed to your next non-Interrupt Action.*

*Upgrades From: Cunning*

### Living Fortress
{:#living-fortress}

*Hitting you is frequently the least productive choice available. Gain DR equal to RES/4 and increase Block's damage reduction by 10%. Take the Hit reduces its final Speed by 3 and may include up to 3 yards of movement to reach the protected creature. When you successfully Take the Hit, reduce Final Damage to you by 20%; if you suffer a Wound, ignore that Wound's penalties until the battle ends. While you are Prepare Blocking, adjacent allies improve their own Block damage reduction by 10%.*

*Upgrades From: Protector*

### Master Surgeon
{:#master-surgeon}

*Requires: Medicine 15*

*You can keep someone alive through injuries that should have ended the fight. Increase healing you provide to others by 35%, and reduce final Speed of non-damaging Medical Jutsu targeting others by 4\.*

*Resuscitate — Speed 0 Interrupt, once per battle: when an adjacent ally would be incapacitated but not killed, spend 1 Willpower and 25 Chakra and make Medicine TN 30\. On success the ally remains conscious at 1 HP and one Wound's penalties are suppressed for 20 IC. This cannot restore a dead creature or undo destruction incompatible with survival.*

*Upgrades From: Combat Medic*

### Perfect Technique
{:#perfect-technique}

*At the start of each in-character day, choose three B-rank or lower Jutsu you know as Signature Techniques. When using one, pay 5 additional Chakra or Stamina and choose one: \-3 final Speed, \+3 Accuracy, \+15% Final Damage, or \+4 on its Stamina/Chakra Exhaustion roll. Once per battle, pay 10 additional cost to choose two different options instead. If the Signature Technique requires handseals, you may perform them one-handed.*

*Upgrades From: Jutsu Master, Single-Handed Seals*

- *Precision, \+3 Accuracy*

### Regenerative Chakra
{:#regenerative-chakra}

*Your chakra recovers almost as quickly as you spend it. Gain \+5 on Chakra Exhaustion rolls. Every 10 IC, reduce Chakra Exhaustion penalty by 1\. Reduce total Upkeep you personally pay by 1 \+ CHA/30. Once per battle, after failing Chakra Exhaustion, advance one fewer Fatigue category. Increase Ninjutsu Damage Bonus by current Fatigue level.*

*At the start of battle, gain Chakra Points equal to maximum Willpower. After a Chakra Exhaustion roll, spend any number of Chakra Points; each adds current Fatigue \+ 1 to the result. Chakra Points refresh at the start of the next battle.*

*Upgrades From: Boundless Chakra*

### Relentless
{:#relentless}

*Momentum is the only defense you care about. Each time you hit with a damaging Attack, gain \+2 Damage Bonus on your next damaging Attack, up to half your normal Damage Bonus. Missing resets this bonus. Whenever you miss with a damaging Attack, determine when you next act as though the missed Attack had half its final Speed, rounded up. This does not change the Attack's damage or any other effect based on its Speed. Each consecutive miss also gives \+1 Accuracy to your next damaging Attack, maximum \+5; hitting resets it.*

*Upgrades From: Bruiser*

### Super Novice
{:#super-novice}

*You never settled into one specialty, so you learned to make that everyone else's problem. Gain a 10% XP bonus. Whenever you are Wounded, gain 1 temporary Willpower. This temporary Willpower lasts until the next time you are Wounded; if you have not spent it, it is replaced, and if you have spent it, you gain a new point.*

*Upgrades From: Any 1 Point or 2 Point Unique except Elementally Gifted or Elementalist*

### Surging Power
{:#surging-power}

*The worse your condition becomes, the harder you are to shut down. Whenever you spend Willpower, halve current Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion penalties; instead, you may spend Willpower specifically to remove one of those penalties entirely. Spend 1 Willpower to ignore all penalties from one Wound until the battle ends. Reduce Fatigue penalties to Accuracy and Defensive rolls by half maximum Willpower, rounded down.*

*Upgrades From: Boundless Chakra, Endless Energy, Tenacious*

### Tactician
{:#tactician}

*You win by deciding what the fight is going to look like before anyone else notices there was a choice. The first time you use a specific Action in a battle, it gains \+2 Accuracy or \+2 to its relevant d20 roll. If that Action is an Attack and hits, gain half its final Speed in AP, without exceeding your maximum.*

*Spend 10 AP before one of your Actions rolls Accuracy to roll 2d20 and keep the higher result. As Speed 0, exchange your next scheduled IC with the next scheduled IC of a willing, unstunned ally. Pay 2 AP per IC between those counts, or 1 AP per IC if you also spend 1 Willpower. You may not use this after either of those scheduled Actions has been declared.*

*Upgrades From: Leader*

### Vanishing Point
{:#vanishing-point}

*You do not so much escape notice as leave the part of the fight where attention can find you. Gain \+3 Dodge and \+4 Stealth. After a full Dodge or successful Hide, immediately move the distance you normally cover in 5 IC. At the end of that movement you may draw, sheathe, or Hide at Speed 0 if legal. Your first Attack within 10 IC gains \+3 Accuracy and \-2 final Speed.*

*Upgrades From: Exceptionally Fast, Skirmisher*
