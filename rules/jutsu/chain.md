# Chain Weapon Bukijutsu
{:#chain-weapon-bukijutsu}

*Chain weapons are the control weapon: their dedicated Bukijutsu turn the existing Grapple and Control system into a ranged weapon discipline*.

## Weapon Grapples
{:#weapon-grapples}

Chain Weapon Bukijutsu use the existing Grapple system rather than a separate restraint system. When a technique says it initiates a Weapon Grapple, the Chain weapon acts as the limbs maintaining the hold. The user is still the grappler for Grapple Offense, Grapple Defense, Control, Pressure, Grip Fighting and every other rule that modifies Grapples.

Initiating the Grapple: If a Chain Bukijutsu says it initiates a Weapon Grapple, resolve its weapon Attack first. On the required hit, the target enters a Clinch with the Chain weapon as though it had been hit by Grab, except the Grapple may be established at the Chain weapon's Range. Determine starting Control using the normal opposing Grapple checks. This is not a second attack or Defense.

Using Grapple Jutsu through the Chain: Chain Bukijutsu with Pummel, Trapping, Takedown, Ground Fighting, or Submission use those tags exactly as written in the normal Grapple rules. The Chain weapon supplies the limbs required to perform those techniques, so these Bukijutsu ignore the normal requirement that all four of the user's limbs be free. Ordinary Grapple jutsu do not gain range merely because you are holding a Chain weapon unless another rule says they do.

Remote Clinch: The target suffers the normal effects of a Clinch. The wielder does not suffer the Clinch's Dodge/Parry, movement, Ignite-transfer or Stealth restrictions solely because the Chain is holding the target at range. The wielder still loses Control normally for actions they take while maintaining the Grapple. The target may use Break Clinch and other legal escape effects against the wielder's normal Grapple values.

Weapon Pin: When a Chain Bukijutsu successfully uses Takedown, the target becomes Pinned by the Chain. The target suffers the normal effects of a Pin, including the normal escape rules, but the wielder does not become Prone and does not have their Speed reduced to 0 simply because the weapon is maintaining the Pin. Ground Fighting and Submission techniques delivered by Chain Bukijutsu function normally from this state.

Occupied Weapon: A Chain weapon maintaining a Weapon Grapple is occupied. It may only make attacks or use techniques that explicitly function during that Weapon Grapple. Releasing, losing, destroying, or using that same Chain weapon to establish a different Weapon Grapple ends the first.

Range and Movement: A Weapon Grapple does not cause the wielder and target to automatically move together as a normal Clinch does. The target cannot voluntarily end an Action farther from the wielder or current anchor than the Chain weapon's Range allows. If either participant is moved so the Chain can no longer reach, the Weapon Grapple ends unless a technique says otherwise.

## E-Rank
{:#e-rank-14}

#### Casting Lash
{:#casting-lash}

The chain is cast past the target and tensioned back through them, so Range and pull are part of the same action. The goal is to make the opponent fight the line you chose, not the one they began on.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Chain, Attack  
Effects: Gain \+4 Range. On a full hit, initiate a Weapon Grapple with the target as described above.

#### Ankle Catch
{:#ankle-catch}

The lowest loop of the chain is used to attack footing rather than flesh. The user can trade damage for a trip or short Immobilization depending on whether immediate posture or continued movement matters more.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+1dS  
Speed: \+0  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Chain, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, you may deal 20% less final damage and initiate a Weapon Grapple. The target takes \-2 on the opposing Grapple check used to determine starting Control. While the Weapon Grapple remains a Clinch, the wrapped leg inflicts Immobilization 1\.

#### Reel and Strike
{:#reel-and-strike}

The chain does not go slack after the first contact. It reels the target toward the user, and if the target cannot be moved the stored tension is converted into extra impact instead.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Chain, Attack  
Effects: If the target is in your Weapon Grapple, this Attack gains Pummel and Trapping 2\. On a successful Grapple roll, pull the target toward you by up to half your current Control in yards, rounded down, maximum 5\. If it cannot be moved because it is already adjacent or firmly anchored, add \+2dS instead.

#### Neck Loop
{:#neck-loop}

The loop is placed high across the neck and shoulder line, where a small mistake becomes bleeding, shock, and a brutal loss of posture.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+5  
Accuracy: \-2  
Special: Stun 2  
Tags: Chain 3, Chain, Attack  
Effects: Called Shot to Head/Neck with its penalty reduced by 1\. Apply Stun 2 normally. If the target is in your Weapon Grapple, this technique gains Takedown; on a successful Grapple roll, the target becomes Pinned by the Chain after the Attack resolves.

#### Moving Coil
{:#moving-coil}

The chain is kept alive around the body like a moving perimeter. It buys both projectile defense and extra threat range, but a badly failed parry can collapse the whole pattern.

Rank: E  
Stamina: 5  
Upkeep: 1  
Speed: 6  
Tags: Chain X, Chain, Utility  
Effects: While maintained, gain \+1 Parry against Ranged Taijutsu and \+3 Range on Chain attacks and Weapon Grapples. Ends if you fully fail a Parry by 5+.

#### Wide Lash
{:#wide-lash}

The chain sweeps across a lane instead of hunting one body. A strong hit can pull a target toward the centre of that lane, shaping the group rather than only damaging it.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+6  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+4  
Line: 2, 8  
Special: Knockback 3  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Chain, Attack  
Effects: Attack all chosen enemies in the line. Resolve the Knockback Special separately against each target hit; successful Knockback moves the target toward the centre of the Line rather than away from you. Wide Lash cannot initiate a Weapon Grapple.

## D-Rank
{:#d-rank-14}

#### Cross Snare
{:#cross-snare}

Two lines of chain close around one limb. The user decides whether taking away an arm’s clean Accuracy or a leg’s movement is more valuable than trying to control the entire body at once.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+3  
Accuracy: \-1  
Tags: Chain 2, Chain, Attack  
Effects: If the target is in your Weapon Grapple, this technique gains Trapping 4\. On a successful Grapple roll choose an arm or leg. Until the Weapon Grapple ends: arm \= \-2 Accuracy on actions requiring it; leg \= Immobilization 2\. Only one limb may be affected by Cross Snare from you at a time; a new application replaces the previous one.

#### Double Bind
{:#double-bind}

The chain separates into several committed lines, each loop hunting a different limb or body as the user works the weapon across the field.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+13  
Damage: \+4dS  
Speed: \+6  
Accuracy: \-2  
Tags: Chain 3, Chain, Attack  
Effects: Requires the target to be in your Weapon Grapple. This technique gains Takedown. On a successful Grapple roll, the target becomes Pinned by the Chain and you may choose up to two limbs; each chosen arm takes \-2 Accuracy on actions requiring it and each chosen leg applies Immobilization 2 while the Pin remains.

#### Side Anchor
{:#side-anchor}

Scenery becomes part of the weapon. By routing the chain around an anchor, the user can attack from an angle cover was not protecting and make line-of-effect a tactical choice.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 5  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Chain, Utility  
Effects: Anchor the chain around scenery or change its attack angle. Your next Chain attack or Weapon Grapple action may originate from the anchor for line of effect. If the target uses the Cover Interrupt against it, reduce Cover’s \+5 bonus by 2\.

#### Coiled Guard
{:#coiled-guard}

The weapon is coiled into a defensive orbit close enough to parry melee attacks while still threatening at range. A clean defense can be turned into a small positional correction on the attacker.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 9  
Upkeep: 2  
Speed: 8  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Chain, Utility  
Effects: Gain \+1 Parry against Melee Attacks and \+6 Range with the chain. Once per 20 IC after a full Parry, you may immediately make a Grapple Offense roll against the attacker’s Grapple Defense. On success, move it up to 3 yards to a legal space within your Chain weapon’s Reach.

## C-Rank
{:#c-rank-14}

#### Weapon Snare
{:#weapon-snare}

The chain wraps a held weapon instead of the target’s body, turning their own reach and grip against them.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+9  
Damage: \+4dS  
Speed: \+3  
Accuracy: \-1  
Tags: Chain 2, Chain, Attack  
Effects: Requires the target to be in your Weapon Grapple. This technique gains Trapping 5\. On a successful Grapple roll choose one held weapon and choose Attack or Parry; rolls of that type made with the snared weapon take \-3 while the Weapon Grapple remains. At 8+ Control, the target cannot use that weapon for the chosen purpose until it successfully reduces your Control.

#### Slip Step
{:#slip-step}

The user moves through their own slack instead of dragging the weapon behind them. The next cast begins from a longer, stranger line because the chain was never allowed to settle.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 7  
Speed: 2  
Tags: Chain X, Chain, Utility, Movement  
Effects: Move up to 4 yards while keeping the chain in motion. This movement does not end a Weapon Grapple, and your next Chain attack or Weapon Grapple action before 20 IC gains \+4 Range.

#### Tension Line
{:#tension-line}

Tension is fixed to the environment and used as movement, leverage, or retrieval. The same technique can pull the user through space or bring an unattended object to them, including vertically.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 11  
Speed: 7  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Chain, Utility, Movement  
Effects: Attach the chain to an anchor within 20 yards and pull yourself to it, or pull an unattended object up to STR/5 weight units to you. If you currently have a target in a Weapon Grapple, you may instead make a Grapple Offense roll against its Grapple Defense; on success, pull it up to 5 yards toward you or the anchor. This movement may be vertical.

## B-Rank
{:#b-rank-14}

#### Intercepting Coil
{:#intercepting-coil}

The chain whips into the path of incoming fire and keeps moving after the deflection, carrying your footing with it.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 10  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Chain, Utility, Interrupt, Movement  
Effects: Parry a Ranged Taijutsu or Projectile Attack with \+2. On a full success, you may move up to 2 yards without ending a Weapon Grapple.

#### Snap Return
{:#snap-return}

This is the recovery skill that separates a chain fighter from somebody who merely owns a chain. The weapon snaps back into control before a disarm, drop, or long cast can end the style.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 9  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Chain, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Use when your chain would be disarmed, dropped, or knocked away. Keep hold of it and move it back to your hand; if already thrown, retrieve it from up to 20 yards. If this prevents you from losing access to the chain, an existing Weapon Grapple is not ended.

## A-Rank
{:#a-rank-14}

#### Suspension Hold
{:#suspension-hold}

You cast one loop around the target and another around the battlefield, then pull both lines tight until their movement is dictated by the anchor.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+24  
Damage: \+8dS  
Speed: \+6  
Accuracy: \-2  
Tags: Chain 3, Chain, Attack  
Effects: Requires the target to be Pinned by the Chain. This technique gains Submission and has Control 7\. On a successful Grapple roll, choose a solid anchor within 6 yards of the target and within your Chain weapon’s Range. Pull the target up to 4 yards toward that anchor and suspend or tether them to it. While maintained, they take \-2 Dodge and cannot voluntarily move farther from the anchor. Each consecutive use automatically hits as normal for a maintained Submission and applies Suffocation 1\. Destroying the anchor or ending the Weapon Grapple ends this technique.

#### Tripwire Field
{:#tripwire-field}

You route the chain through several anchor points and turn the surrounding ground into a web of low, moving lines.

Rank: A  
Stamina: 18  
Speed: 8  
Range: 20  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Chain, Utility  
Effects: Choose up to three anchor points in Range and connect them with the chain for 20 IC. The first time a creature crosses one of those lines, make a Grapple Offense roll against its Grapple Defense. On success, apply Prone and Immobilization 2 until its next action. A creature already in your Weapon Grapple automatically fails this check. Tripwire Field cannot be maintained with the same chain that is holding a Weapon Grapple.
