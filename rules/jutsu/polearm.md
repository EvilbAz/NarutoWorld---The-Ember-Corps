# **Polearm Bukijutsu**
{:#polearm-bukijutsu}

*Polearm owns measure: Reach, approach denial, intercepts and repositioning are its main advantages*.

## **E-Rank**
{:#e-rank-12}

#### **Passing Thrust**
{:#passing-thrust}

The thrust is carried through a passing step so the point threatens beyond the weapon’s usual measure. The user can finish closer or farther away depending on where they want the next exchange to happen.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+0  
Tags: Chain 1, Polearm, Attack, Movement  
Effects: Gain \+2 Reach for this Attack. You may use Move with an Attack either before or after the strike, and that movement may be toward or away from the target.

#### **Butt-End Check**
{:#butt-end-check}

The rear of the weapon becomes a second striking end. A compact blunt check can create room, punish somebody who crowded the shaft, and immediately restore the distance where the polearm is strongest.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+1dS  
Speed: \-1  
Accuracy: \+1  
Special: Knockback 2  
Tags: Chain 1, Polearm, Attack, Movement  
Effects: Treat the attack as Blunt. On a successful Special, you may immediately set your distance to the edge of your normal Reach.

#### **Threading Thrust**
{:#threading-thrust}

The point is threaded through a small defensive window rather than swung around the guard. The technique makes late Interrupts harder and rewards a clean line by slipping part of the impact past DR.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+1  
Accuracy: \-1  
Tags: Chain 2, Polearm, Attack  
Effects: Defensive Interrupts against this Attack count as \+1 Base Speed for Defense Validity. On full hit, ignore 5% DR.

#### **Driving Thrust**
{:#driving-thrust}

This is a committed line attack that spends the polearm’s reach aggressively.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+5  
Accuracy: \-2  
Tags: Chain 3, Polearm, Attack  
Effects: Gain \+1 Reach for this Attack. \+5% Wounding.

#### **Set Spear**
{:#set-spear}

The user stops chasing the opponent and makes the opponent enter the weapon instead. The polearm is already aligned before the approach begins, turning movement into the trigger for the counterattack.

Rank: E  
Stamina: 5  
Speed: 5  
Tags: Chain X, Polearm, Attack, Interrupt  
Effects: When a creature enters your Reach, make a Polearm Basic Weapon Attack at 85% final damage. It is Chain X.

#### **Rotation Strike**
{:#rotation-strike}

The whole polearm is rotated through a controlled arc. It can claim space against several enemies, or the same mechanics can be tightened around one target for better Accuracy.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+7  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+5  
Area: 2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Polearm, Attack  
Effects: Attack all chosen enemies in Reach. If only one creature is targeted, gain \+1 Accuracy instead.

## **D-Rank**
{:#d-rank-12}

#### **Catch and Turn**
{:#catch-and-turn}

The hook, blade, or shaft catches posture instead of simply striking it. Once contact is made, the user chooses whether to turn the target into a new space or sacrifice damage to put them on the ground.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+2  
Special: Knockback 4 or Prone  
Tags: Chain 2, Polearm, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one: Turn — resolve Knockback 4, but you may choose any legal direction that keeps the target within your Reach; or Sweep — resolve Prone normally.

#### **Pinning Thrust**
{:#pinning-thrust}

You brace the haft and drive the point through the target’s line with enough commitment that the impact itself disrupts their movement. The technique is dangerous because it gives up easy recovery in exchange for keeping the point exactly where the opponent least wants it.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+12  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+4  
Accuracy: \-2  
Special: Immobilization 2  
Tags: Chain 3, Polearm, Attack  
Effects: This Attack deals Piercing wounds and gains \+10% Wounding. Immobilization from this Special lasts 15 IC.

#### **Haft Guard**
{:#haft-guard}

The shaft receives an incoming attack across a broad, strong surface. A successful defense is used to choose the new measure, putting the attacker either uncomfortably close or back at the point.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 5  
Speed: 5  
Tags: Chain X, Polearm, Utility, Interrupt, Movement  
Effects: Parry a Melee Attack with \+1. On full success, step 1 yard and choose whether the attacker is now at minimum or maximum legal Reach.

#### **Extended Guard**
{:#extended-guard}

The weapon is kept extended as a permanent obstacle. The stance is less about striking first than making an opponent spend more of their movement just getting to a place where they can threaten you.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 9  
Upkeep: 2  
Speed: 7  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Polearm, Utility  
Effects: Your Polearm attacks gain \+1 Reach. When a creature uses Move with an Attack to move from outside to inside that Reach, reduce the free movement granted by that maneuver by 2 yards, minimum 0\.

## **C-Rank**
{:#c-rank-12}

#### **Weapon Hook**
{:#weapon-hook}

The head or shaft catches the opponent’s weapon and drags its line away from where they want to use it.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+9  
Damage: \+4dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Polearm, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one held weapon. Choose Attack or Parry; the next roll of that type made with the hooked weapon before 20 IC takes \-3.

#### **Shaft Pivot**
{:#shaft-pivot}

This is circular footwork performed around the weapon’s ideal measure. The user changes side without collapsing the distance or giving the target a clean line to answer from.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 6  
Speed: 2  
Tags: Chain X, Polearm, Utility, Movement  
Effects: Move up to 5 yards around a creature in your Reach without leaving Reach.

#### **Leaping Pole**
{:#leaping-pole}

The shaft is planted and used as a lever for the body. The vault creates unusual vertical or lateral attack lines and rewards a follow-up made from above rather than serving as movement for its own sake.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 12  
Speed: 8  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Polearm, Utility, Movement  
Effects: Vault up to 8 yards horizontally or 4 vertically using the polearm. Your next Polearm Attack before 20 IC counts as \+4 Speed higher for damage only if it was made from above the target.

## **B-Rank**
{:#b-rank-12}

#### **Crossbar Catch**
{:#crossbar-catch}

You use the length of the shaft to catch a projectile or ranged weapon line before it reaches the body.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 10  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Polearm, Utility, Interrupt, Movement  
Effects: Parry a Ranged Taijutsu or Projectile Attack with \+2. On a full success, you may step up to 2 yards.

#### **Sliding Measure**
{:#sliding-measure}

The hands slide rather than the feet. By changing grip length, the user decides whether the next exchange values reach or a heavier striking die, making measure an active resource.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 9  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Polearm, Utility  
Effects: Until your next action choose one: Long Measure — gain \+3 Reach and reduce the weapon’s die size by 2; or Short Measure — lose 1 Reach and increase the weapon’s die size by 4\.

## **A-Rank**
{:#a-rank-12}

#### **Through-Line**
{:#through-line}

You carry the thrust through the chained target and keep moving with it, using the opening to either pass their body or send the point into the next threat on the same line.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+26  
Damage: \+14dS  
Speed: \+5  
Accuracy: \-2  
Tags: Chain 3, Polearm, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one: Pass Through — after the Attack resolves, use any remaining Move with an Attack distance in any legal direction, including to a legal space on the far side of the target; or Second Point — strike one creature directly behind the target and within Reach for 60% final damage. The secondary hit uses the same Accuracy result and cannot establish or advance Chain.

#### **Levering Throw**
{:#levering-throw}

You catch the attack on the haft, drop your weight under it, and use the polearm as a lever to throw the attacker off their base.

Rank: A  
Stamina: 13  
Speed: 4  
Special: Knockback 4, Prone  
Tags: Chain X, Polearm, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Parry a Melee Attack with \+2. On a full success, make one Athletics Status Roll against the attacker’s Resistance. On success, apply Prone and Knockback 4; you may choose the Knockback direction, but it must end in a legal space within your Reach.
