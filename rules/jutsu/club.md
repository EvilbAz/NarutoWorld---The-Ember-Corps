# Club Bukijutsu
{:#club-bukijutsu}

*Club is the force weapon: it wins through impact, resisted Specials, armor pressure, collisions and terrain*.

## E-Rank
{:#e-rank-11}

#### Driving Blow
{:#driving-blow}

The blow is thrown through the target rather than at them. Its real payoff is deciding how far the impact carries the opponent and whether you chase into the space you just created.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+1  
Special: Knockback 3  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a successful Special, you may follow the target up to half the distance it was actually moved as part of the Attack.

#### Rising Blow
{:#rising-blow}

The head of the weapon rises under the target’s posture, using shock to lift the chin, ribs, or guard. Against somebody already rattled, the same leverage can be spent on damage instead of more Stun.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+4  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+2  
Accuracy: \-1  
Special: Stun 3  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack  
Effects: Apply Stun 3 normally. If the target was already Stunned before this Attack hit, do not apply this Stun and add \+2dS damage instead.

#### Returning Swing
{:#returning-swing}

This swing uses the opponent’s recent aggression as information. The user lets the counterweight and return path of the club erase awkward angles that would normally spoil Accuracy, then turns that recovered structure into force.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Club, Attack  
Effects: If the target attacked you in the last 20 IC, ignore up to 1 point of Accuracy penalties on this Attack. For each ignored point, gain \+1 Damage Bonus for this Attack.

#### Overhead Blow
{:#overhead-blow}

This is a deliberately obvious commitment from above. The user accepts the risk of a missed heavy swing in exchange for putting the club where a head-level impact can matter immediately.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+5  
Accuracy: \-2  
Special: Stun 5  
Tags: Chain 3, Club, Attack  
Effects: Must be a Called Shot to the Head. \+5% Wounding. On a full miss, your next Defense before your next action takes \-1.

#### Brace
{:#brace}

The club is braced with the body behind it, turning the weapon into a short piece of mobile cover. The point is to survive the collision without sacrificing the tool to durability damage.

Rank: E  
Stamina: 4  
Speed: 5  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Block or Parry a Melee Attack with \+1. The first time each Round the club would gain WDP from that Block or Parry, reduce the WDP gained by 1, to a minimum of 0\.

#### Wide Swing
{:#wide-swing}

The weapon is allowed to travel through a wide arc and threaten several enemies at once. The user gives up some concentrated force in exchange for making nearby opponents respect the same swing.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+6  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+3  
Area: 2  
Special: Knockback 2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Club, Attack  
Effects: Attack all enemies in reach. Divide Speed×PDB evenly among targets hit. Resolve the Special separately against each fully hit target.

## D-Rank
{:#d-rank-11}

#### Rib Shot
{:#rib-shot}

The strike is aimed where breathing, posture, and balance meet. Depending on what matters more, the user can make the target’s next attack clumsy or simply rattle their frame with Stun.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+4dS  
Speed: \+1  
Tags: Chain 2, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one: Smother the Breath — the target takes \-2 Accuracy on its next Attack before 20 IC, increased to \-3 if it was already Stunned when this Attack hit; or Rattle the Frame — apply Stun 4\.

#### Armor Crack
{:#armor-crack}

Instead of slipping around armor or a barrier, the club attacks the thing protecting the target. The technique is about putting mass through structure until the defense itself becomes damaged terrain.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+12  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+4  
Accuracy: \-1  
Tags: Chain 3, Club, Attack  
Effects: Before damaging the target, deal 65% of this Attack's final damage to one Armor piece, Barrier, or Construct directly between you and the target.

#### Body Check
{:#body-check}

The shoulder joins the weapon system. A body check can move an opponent without spending the club’s striking surface, preserving the weapon for the next beat while still changing distance.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 5  
Speed: 4  
Special: Knockback 3  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility, Movement  
Effects: If adjacent to your chained target, resolve the Knockback Special without dealing damage. On success you may move into the space the target left.

#### Head-Heavy Grip
{:#head-heavy-grip}

Grip and chakra bias the weapon toward the striking head, exaggerating leverage and reach. Each swing carries more of the weapon’s mass through the head, turning broad motion into crushing displacement.

Rank: D  
Chakra: 14  
Upkeep: 3  
Speed: 8  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Club, Utility  
Effects: One blunt weapon gains \+1 Reach. Whenever one of its Bukijutsu has a printed Knockback Special, increase that printed Knockback by 1 before rolling it. It may deal Blunt wounds even if another property would change its wound type.

## C-Rank
{:#c-rank-11}

#### Low Sweep
{:#low-sweep}

You drive the club through the target’s base instead of through their centre, forcing them to choose between footing and impact.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+9  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+3  
Accuracy: \-1  
Special: Knockback 4 or Prone  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, choose Knockback 4 or Prone, then resolve that Special normally.

#### Short Grip
{:#short-grip}

A heavy weapon becomes dangerous to its owner when the grip gets too long, too short, or too awkward. This technique is the practiced reset that restores leverage without giving up the chain.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 6  
Speed: 2  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility  
Effects: Remove up to 3 points of temporary Accuracy penalty affecting your next Club Chain attack, or reduce its Delay by 2 (minimum 0).

#### Wallbreaker
{:#wallbreaker}

This is the answer to doors, walls, armor, and anything else that assumes being hard is the same as being safe. The technique is built to keep meaningful damage on the target even when ordinary hits would be absorbed.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+15  
Damage: \+8dS  
Speed: \+6  
Accuracy: \-2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Club, Attack  
Effects: Against Armor, Barriers and unattended objects, this Attack deals \+45% final damage and cannot have its damage reduced below 50% by Partial Success.

## B-Rank
{:#b-rank-11}

#### Guard Rattle
{:#guard-rattle}

You strike through the defending line hard enough that the weapon or arm catching the blow becomes the problem.

Rank: B  
Stamina: \+15  
Damage: \+8dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, if the target used a weapon to Block or Parry, that weapon gains \+3 WDP and takes \-3 Parry for 10 IC. Otherwise, apply Stun 5\.

#### Ground Shock
{:#ground-shock}

The ground is treated like another target. By spending the strike on terrain instead of a body, the user can make footing, approach lines, and movement worse for everybody forced through the area.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 10  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility  
Effects: Strike the ground; Area 5\. Create Rocky Terrain 2 for 20 IC. No damage is dealt.

## A-Rank
{:#a-rank-11}

#### Collision Breaker
{:#collision-breaker}

You drive the target through the space behind them, turning walls, trees, wreckage, and other bodies into part of the finishing blow.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+26  
Damage: \+13dS  
Speed: \+6  
Accuracy: \-2  
Special: Knockback 6  
Tags: Chain 3, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a successful Special, if the target collides with solid terrain before travelling the full distance, stop the Knockback and apply Stun 4\. If it collides with another creature, that creature must defend against 50% of this Attack’s final damage; this secondary hit cannot Wound or advance Chain.

#### Juggernaut Rush
{:#juggernaut-rush}

You put the whole body behind the weapon and carry the attack through the space the target thought they controlled.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+22  
Damage: \+10dS  
Speed: \+4  
Accuracy: \-1  
Special: Knockback 6  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack, Movement  
Effects: Before the Attack, you may use Move with an Attack in a straight line, but calculate the distance using the Attack’s full final Speed instead of half. On a successful Knockback Special, you may move into the target’s former space.
