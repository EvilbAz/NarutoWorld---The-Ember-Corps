# **Fan Bukijutsu**
{:#fan-bukijutsu}

*Fan shapes the battlefield: it moves groups, creates lanes, controls ranged space and turns wind into positioning*.

## **E-Rank**
{:#e-rank-13}

#### **Opening Gust**
{:#opening-gust}

The first gust is modest but highly controllable. You can spend the airflow on driving the target away, or ride the return current yourself to change the angle of the exchange.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Chakra: 3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Range: 6  
Speed: \+1  
Special: Knockback 4 (Drive) or Knockback 2 (Ride the Gust)  
Tags: Chain 1, Fan, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one: Drive — resolve Knockback 4; or Ride the Gust — resolve Knockback 2, then after the Attack resolves you may use any remaining Move with an Attack distance in any legal direction whether or not the target resisted the Knockback.

#### **Folding Edge**
{:#folding-edge}

The fan snaps shut and is used like a short baton or edged frame. The sudden change from broad wind pressure to close weapon contact is meant to make a parrying defender late on the next answer.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \-1  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Fan, Attack  
Effects: Melee only. On a full hit, the target takes \-1 Parry against your next Fan Chain attack before 20 IC.

#### **Reversing Gust**
{:#reversing-gust}

The fan snaps the current back toward you, pulling the target into a shorter and less comfortable distance.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Chakra: 5  
Damage: \+3dS  
Line: 3, 10  
Speed: \+2  
Special: Knockback 3  
Tags: Chain 2, Fan, Attack  
Effects: Resolve the Knockback Special normally, but successful Knockback moves the target toward you rather than away from you.

#### **Full Fan Gust**
{:#full-fan-gust}

The fan opens fully and the entire span contributes to one committed burst, driving a wall of air through the target hard enough to carry both body and guard with it.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Chakra: 8  
Damage: \+5dS  
Line: 4, 15  
Speed: \+4  
Special: Knockback 5  
Tags: Chain 3, Fan, Attack  
Effects: \+5% Wounding.

#### **Wind Screen**
{:#wind-screen}

The fan catches a projectile in moving air instead of trying to meet it edge-on. A successful screen keeps enough momentum to let the user drift away from the original line of fire.

Rank: E  
Chakra: 7  
Speed: 5  
Tags: Chain X, Fan, Utility, Interrupt, Movement  
Effects: Parry a Ranged Taijutsu or Projectile Attack with \+1. On full success, you may move up to 2 yards.

#### **Broad Sweep**
{:#broad-sweep}

The fan is used to claim a broad patch of battlefield with one sweep. The user gives up concentrated Speed×PDB so several enemies have to answer the same displacement at once.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+6  
Chakra: 5  
Damage: \+2dS  
Area: 4  
Speed: \+4  
Special: Knockback 2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Fan, Attack  
Effects: Attack all chosen enemies in Area. Speed×PDB is divided among targets hit. Resolve the Special separately against each fully hit target.

## **D-Rank**
{:#d-rank-13}

#### **Backdraft**
{:#backdraft}

The current is turned across the target rather than straight through them. The technique is as much about lateral placement as force, shifting both fighters into a new line for the rest of the chain.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Chakra: 7  
Damage: \+3dS  
Range: 12  
Speed: \+1  
Special: Knockback 4  
Tags: Chain 2, Fan, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a successful Knockback Special, choose a sideways direction for the forced movement. After the Attack resolves, you may use any remaining Move with an Attack distance in any legal direction.

#### **Dust Sweep**
{:#dust-sweep}

The sweep is designed to clear a lane whether the attack connects cleanly or not. A full Dodge still has to respect the moving air, while a hit leaves the target fighting through disturbed visibility.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+10  
Chakra: 14  
Damage: \+5dS  
Area: 7  
Speed: \+5  
Tags: Chain 3, Fan, Attack  
Effects: Targets that fully Dodge are moved to the Area edge; targets hit take Visibility 1 for 10 IC.

#### **Wind Sense**
{:#wind-sense}

Tiny changes in pressure, cloth, dust, hair, and projectile flight are read as information. The fan catches those currents and turns them into a clearer picture of movement through obscured space.

Rank: D  
Chakra: 7  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Fan, Utility  
Effects: Make a Search using Chakra Control or Awareness with \+2. Until your next action, ignore up to 2 points of Visibility penalty on Fan attacks.

#### **Guided Wind**
{:#guided-wind}

The fan is held as a stable channel for both weapon pressure and Fuuton. The user is choosing to make range their advantage, with a small burst of Knockback available when the timing matters most.

Rank: D  
Chakra: 15  
Upkeep: 3  
Speed: 8  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Fan, Utility  
Effects: Fan Bukijutsu gain \+4 Range. Fuuton cast through this fan gains \+10 Range. Once per 20 IC, one Fan Bukijutsu or Fuuton with a printed Knockback Special may increase that printed Knockback by 1 before rolling it.

## **C-Rank**
{:#c-rank-13}

#### **Shearing Wake**
{:#shearing-wake}

The attack leaves a clean tailwind behind it, turning the space you just cut through into a lane for movement.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+8  
Chakra: 8  
Damage: \+4dS  
Range: 14  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 1, Fan, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, the attack path remains a tailwind until your next action. You and allies moving in that direction gain \+25% movement while within the path.

#### **Crosswind Step**
{:#crosswind-step}

The user lets the fan pull their body through a short movement while preserving the chain. Ending farther away stretches the next threat line and makes the weapon feel larger than it is.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 7  
Speed: 2  
Tags: Chain X, Fan, Utility, Movement  
Effects: Move up to 5 yards. If you end farther from your chained target than you began, your next Fan Chain attack gains \+2 Range.

#### **Closed Guard**
{:#closed-guard}

The fan closes into a compact guard that can actually receive melee force. A clean block or parry leaves the weapon ready to reopen immediately into the first attack of a new fan sequence.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 11  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Fan, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Block or Parry a Melee Attack with the closed fan at \+2. On full success, your next Fan Chain 1 before 20 IC has its final Speed reduced by 1\.

## **B-Rank**
{:#b-rank-13}

#### **Returning Gale**
{:#returning-gale}

The return current drags the target across your line while carrying you into the space their balance just abandoned.

Rank: B  
Stamina: \+15  
Chakra: 13  
Damage: \+7dS  
Range: 20  
Speed: \+2  
Special: Knockback 5  
Tags: Chain 2, Fan, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a successful Knockback Special, choose a sideways direction for the forced movement. Afterward, you may use your Move with an Attack movement to follow along the path the target vacated.

#### **Seamless Wind**
{:#seamless-wind}

The fan catches the rhythm of a Fuuton and carries it through the current exchange, allowing wind chakra to pass through the sequence without forcing the weapon flow to reset.

Rank: B  
Chakra: 11  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Fan, Utility  
Effects: Once per Chain, your next Fan-tagged Fuuton used before 20 IC may be used without breaking Chain. It does not change or refresh Chain, and cannot trigger an effect that grants an additional weapon attack after using Fuuton.

## **A-Rank**
{:#a-rank-13}

#### **Windbreak**
{:#windbreak}

You split the battlefield with one violent current and leave the air behind it too unstable for clean projectile fire.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+26  
Chakra: 24  
Damage: \+12dS  
Line: 8, 35  
Speed: \+5  
Special: Knockback 6  
Tags: Chain 3, Fan, Attack  
Effects: The Line remains turbulent until your next action; Ranged Taijutsu and Projectile Attacks crossing it take \-2 Accuracy.

#### **Perfect Current**
{:#perfect-current}

You shape a single clean current around an obstacle, letting the next attack arrive from a line the target cannot simply read from your stance.

Rank: A  
Chakra: 14  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Fan, Utility  
Effects: Your next Fan attack before 20 IC may bend once by up to 90 degrees around solid cover. Ignore up to 3 points of Visibility penalty; if the target uses the Cover Interrupt, reduce Cover’s \+5 bonus by 3\. The bend must remain within the attack’s Range.
