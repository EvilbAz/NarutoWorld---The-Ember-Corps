# **Dual Bukijutsu**
{:#dual-bukijutsu}

*Dual overloads defensive choices: two weapons force the opponent to repeat, split or expose a defensive answer*.

## **E-Rank**
{:#e-rank-15}

#### **Double Cut**
{:#double-cut}

Both weapons attack during the same entry, but they do not have to succeed together. The style begins its chain as soon as either hand finds a useful line.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+4  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 1, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Make one Attack with each held weapon at 55% final damage each. Roll Accuracy separately. If either hits, Chain 1 is established.

#### **Scissor Step**
{:#scissor-step}

The two weapons close like shears while the feet circle the target. A parry against one line can leave the defender’s weapon committed in exactly the place the next paired attack wants it.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+0  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Dual, Attack, Movement  
Effects: Requires two weapons. After the Attack resolves, you may use any remaining Move with an Attack distance in any legal direction. If the target Parried this Attack, its next Parry against your Dual Chain attack before 20 IC takes \-2.

#### **Alternating Cuts**
{:#alternating-cuts}

The hands alternate in a broken rhythm, changing line and timing with every swing. The target has to reorient from one weapon to the other instead of settling into a single defensive cadence.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+1  
Tags: Chain 2, Dual, Attack  
Effects: If the immediately previous Chain 2 you used in this Chain was a different Dual technique, gain \+2 Accuracy.

#### **Closing Pair**
{:#closing-pair}

The finisher commits both hands to the same exchange. Hits are resolved separately for defense but brought together when the game asks how serious the combined wound is.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Speed: \+5  
Tags: Chain 3, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Make one Attack with each weapon at 65% final damage each. Roll separately; combine successful hits only when checking Wound severity.

#### **Off-Hand Catch**
{:#off-hand-catch}

The off-hand weapon exists to solve the problems the leading weapon creates. A successful catch leaves either hand free to become the next attacker without wasting time on a formal lead change.

Rank: E  
Stamina: 4  
Speed: 5  
Tags: Chain X, Dual, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Parry with the off-hand weapon at \+1. On full success, the next Dual Chain attack may use either weapon without any swap cost.

#### **Turning Flurry**
{:#turning-flurry}

The user turns both weapons through the surrounding space at once, each hand carving a separate line through the enemies close enough to be caught in the rotation.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+6  
Speed: \+5  
Area: 2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Make one Accuracy roll with each weapon against every chosen enemy in Area; each successful weapon deals 50% final damage.

## **D-Rank**
{:#d-rank-15}

#### **Split Guard**
{:#split-guard}

Each weapon asks a separate defensive question. If the target answers both with the same action, the second line is deliberately timed to arrive when that repeated defense is least comfortable.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Speed: \+3  
Tags: Chain 2, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Make one Attack with each weapon at 60% final damage. The target chooses and resolves a Defense against each Attack. If it uses the same broad Defense against both, its second roll takes \-2. If it uses two different broad Defenses, each successful hit gains \+2dS. Chain advances if either Attack hits.

#### **Cross Finish**
{:#cross-finish}

You cross both attack lines through different parts of the target, forcing the defender to contain two wound lines at once before either hand can recover.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+12  
Damage: \+6dS  
Speed: \+6  
Accuracy: \-2  
Tags: Chain 3, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Requires two weapons. On a full hit, choose two different wound locations; if the Attack wounds, apply one wound at the normal location and a second wound one severity lower at the other location. The secondary wound cannot exceed Minor severity.

#### **Hand Feint**
{:#hand-feint}

Two visible threats are used to split attention before the attack starts. The user chooses which defensive habit—Dodge or Parry—they are trying to make less reliable on the next Chain action.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 5  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Dual, Utility  
Effects: Opposed Espionage vs Awareness. On success, choose Dodge or Parry; that defense takes \-2 against your next Dual Chain attack.

#### **Paired Flow**
{:#paired-flow}

The weapons settle into a shared rhythm, one hand recovering while the other remains live. The alternating cadence makes both parries and follow-up attacks tighter and more economical.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 9  
Upkeep: 2  
Speed: 7  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Dual, Utility  
Effects: While wielding two weapons, gain \+1 Parry. Once per 20 IC, reduce the Speed of one Dual Chain attack by 2 after all other modifiers, minimum 3\.

## **C-Rank**
{:#c-rank-15}

#### **Split Pursuit**
{:#split-pursuit}

Each hand takes a different opponent, opening pressure on two fronts before the user decides which target to pursue.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+8  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 1, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Make one Attack with each weapon at 60% final damage. The attacks must target different creatures. If either hits, choose one hit creature as your chained target.

#### **Changing Lead**
{:#changing-lead}

The style changes which weapon is “leading” without changing what is held. This lets the user decide which damage type and hand-specific penalties matter for the next few beats.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 5  
Speed: 1  
Tags: Chain X, Dual, Utility  
Effects: Swap which held weapon is leading. Until your next action, use either weapon's damage type for your Dual Chain effects and ignore one hand-specific penalty of up to 3\.

## **B-Rank**
{:#b-rank-15}

#### **Weapon Lock**
{:#weapon-lock}

The two weapons close around the line the defender committed, trapping that weapon between them for the next exchange.

Rank: B  
Stamina: \+14  
Damage: \+6dS  
Speed: \+3  
Accuracy: \-1  
Tags: Chain 2, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Requires two weapons. On a full hit, if the target used a weapon to Block or Parry, that weapon cannot be used to Block or Parry your next Dual Chain attack before 20 IC.

#### **Counterpair**
{:#counterpair}

A full parry with one hand opens the line for the other. Before the attacker can recover their weapon, the off-hand snaps through the space their attack just exposed.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 10  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Dual, Attack, Interrupt  
Effects: After fully Parrying a Melee Attack, make an off-hand Basic Weapon Attack against that attacker at 65% final damage. This is Chain X.

## **A-Rank**
{:#a-rank-15}

#### **Crossed Execution**
{:#crossed-execution}

One hand attacks the target while the other deliberately destroys the guard protecting them.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+26  
Speed: \+6  
Tags: Chain 3, Dual, Attack  
Effects: Requires two weapons. Make one Attack with each. One weapon attacks the target at 75% final damage; the other attacks one held weapon or Armor piece at 75% final damage. Roll and defend separately.

#### **Divergent Current**
{:#divergent-current}

Each weapon carries a different chakra treatment, letting the pair answer two different defenses in the same exchange.

Rank: A  
Chakra: 26  
Upkeep: 6  
Speed: 12  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Dual, Utility  
Effects: Apply two different weapon-coating effects you know, one to each held weapon. Pay this Upkeep plus only the higher additional Upkeep among those effects.
