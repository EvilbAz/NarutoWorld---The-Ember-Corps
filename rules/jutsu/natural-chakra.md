# Natural Chakra
{:#natural-chakra}

Natural Chakra is the chakra of the living world: the pressure in the earth, the motion of water, the heat of the air and the life that refuses to die. A shinobi normally produces chakra from within. A Sage learns to become still enough that the world can answer back.

Natural Chakra is a Capability Talent built around preparation and balance. You draw Natural Chakra into yourself, enter Sage Mode, then decide when to preserve that balance and when to spend it on Sage Arts.


# AT A GLANCE
{:#at-a-glance}

- Natural Chakra is a Capability Talent and requires a valid Source.

- Gather Natural Chakra to gain Sage Chakra; stored Sage Chakra is temporary.

- Enter Sage Mode once you hold at least 2 Sage Chakra.

- Sage Mode improves your combat and senses according to your Sage Rank.

- Spend Sage Chakra on Sage Arts and Sage Kata while deciding how long you can maintain the Mode.

- Each Deepening raises your Sage Rank and teaches one additional Sage Art or Sage Kata. Three Developments grant Perfect Communion; a fourth Deepening can raise Sage Rank to 5\.

Capability Talent

## Core Talent
{:#core-talent}

Choose a Sage Tradition from a creature, Summon Tribe or other Source capable of teaching Natural Chakra. Gain Sage Rank 1, a maximum Sage Chakra of 4, Gather Natural Chakra, Sage Mode, your Tradition's Signature Trait, and one Sage Art or Sage Kata you meet the requirements for.

Sage Chakra is a separate resource. It cannot pay normal Jutsu Chakra Costs and spending it does not cause Chakra Exhaustion unless an effect specifically says otherwise. Sage Chakra is not Chakra, Stamina, AP or Willpower for effects which restore or modify those resources.

## Deepening Natural Chakra
{:#deepening-natural-chakra}

Each time you Deepen Natural Chakra, increase your Sage Rank by 1 and learn one additional Sage Art or Sage Kata. Each Art or Kata learned this way is one Natural Chakra Development for the normal Talent rules; the Art or Kata granted by the Core Talent is not a Development. Natural Chakra may be Deepened up to four times, to a maximum Sage Rank of 5\. You gain Perfect Communion after the third Development as normal; the fourth Deepening remains available after Mastery, still costs a Talent Point, and is how Sage Rank 5 is normally reached. Unless an Art or Kata says otherwise, the same one cannot be selected more than once.

When you have taken three Natural Chakra Developments, you automatically gain Perfect Communion, the Talent's Mastery.

## Sage Progression
{:#sage-progression}

Sage Rank 1 — Maximum Sage Chakra 4; Sage Power 1; Sage Damage \+10%; Sage Sense \+5.

Sage Rank 2 — Maximum Sage Chakra 5; Sage Power 1; Sage Damage \+15%; Sage Sense \+5.

Sage Rank 3 — Maximum Sage Chakra 6; Sage Power 2; Sage Damage \+20%; Sage Sense \+10.

Sage Rank 4 — Maximum Sage Chakra 7; Sage Power 2; Sage Damage \+25%; Sage Sense \+10.

Sage Rank 5 — Maximum Sage Chakra 8; Sage Power 3; Sage Damage \+30%; Sage Sense \+15.

Sage Power is used by Sage Mode and several Sage Arts. Sage Damage is the Final Damage bonus granted by Sage Mode. Sage Sense is the bonus Sage Mode grants to Awareness and Survival in the circumstances described below.


# SAGE CHAKRA
{:#sage-chakra}

Sage Chakra represents Natural Chakra that has been successfully drawn in and balanced with your own physical and spiritual energy.

You cannot hold more Sage Chakra than your maximum. Excess Sage Chakra is lost. If you become Incapacitated, you immediately lose all Sage Chakra and Sage Mode ends.

Outside Sage Mode, Sage Chakra is unstable. If you take a Rest, leave the scene, or spend 20 consecutive IC without successfully using Gather Natural Chakra, all Sage Chakra you are holding disperses. Entering Sage Mode suspends this dispersal. When Sage Mode ends, any Sage Chakra you still hold is lost.

## GATHER NATURAL CHAKRA
{:#gather-natural-chakra}

\[Speed 10, Delay 8, Utility, Self, Abort\]

You stop forcing chakra through your coils and allow the Natural Chakra around you to settle into them.

Until this Action resolves, your Movement Speed is 0\. You cannot declare another non-Interrupt Action. If you voluntarily move or use an Interrupt, Gather Natural Chakra is aborted after that movement or Interrupt resolves.

If you take damage while Gathering, make a Chakra Control roll using the normal interruption TN for maintaining Handseals. On a failure, Gather Natural Chakra is aborted.

When Gather Natural Chakra resolves, gain 1 Sage Chakra, up to your maximum.

Gather Natural Chakra is not a Jutsu and causes no Chakra Exhaustion. Any effect which reduces Gather Natural Chakra's Speed also reduces its Delay by the same amount. AP may reduce both values in this way; this is a specific exception to the normal rules for reducing Delay. Neither value may normally be reduced below 5\. If an effect specifically lowers Gather Natural Chakra's minimum Speed, it lowers its minimum Delay by the same amount. Gather's final Speed can never be lower than its current Delay; if an effect would cause this, increase its final Speed to match its Delay. You may Gather while already in Sage Mode.

## SAGE MODE
{:#sage-mode}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Self, Upkeep 2\]

Requires: At least 2 Sage Chakra.

Balance the Natural Chakra within your body and enter Sage Mode. Entering Sage Mode does not spend Sage Chakra.

While Sage Mode is active:

- Gain Sage Power to Accuracy with Taijutsu and Ninjutsu Attacks.

- Gain Sage Power to Dodge and Parry rolls.

- Damaging Taijutsu and Ninjutsu gain Sage Damage to Final Damage.

- Gain Sage Sense to Awareness rolls made to detect, Search for or track living creatures, chakra sources and Natural Chakra.

- Gain Sage Sense to Survival rolls made to track creatures, navigate natural terrain or interact with hazards and environments through instinct and observation.

- You may use Sage Arts and Sage Kata which require Sage Mode.

- Any Attack or technique modified by a Sage Art counts as Senjutsu in addition to its normal types.

Sage Mode has Upkeep 2 and follows the normal Upkeep rules. This Upkeep does not spend Sage Chakra. Sage Mode does not remove normal Chakra Exhaustion, Fatigue or Stamina penalties, and it automatically ends when the current scene ends.

If you reach 0 Sage Chakra, Sage Mode ends after the effect which spent your final Sage Chakra resolves. You may end Sage Mode at Speed 0\. When Sage Mode ends for any reason, all remaining Sage Chakra is lost.


# SAGE TRADITIONS
{:#sage-traditions}

Natural Chakra does not look the same in every body. A Sage Tradition is the shape given to Natural Chakra by the creature, place or lineage that taught you to understand it.

Most Sage Traditions are taught by Summon Tribes. Your Tradition determines the visible changes caused by Sage Mode, grants one Signature Trait, and gives access to a small list of Tradition Sage Arts. A Tradition may also alter one narrow rule for Gathering or spending Sage Chakra.

Your Tradition changes what Natural Chakra rewards you for doing, but it does not replace the shared rules above. Unless the Tradition explicitly says otherwise, you still Gather Natural Chakra, use the same Sage Chakra pool, and enter Sage Mode normally.

## BECOMING A SAGE
{:#becoming-a-sage}

Most shinobi are taught that chakra begins inside the body. Physical and spiritual energy are mixed, shaped through the coils and forced outward into the world. Natural Chakra asks for the opposite instinct. It is already there: in roots pushing through old stone, in rainwater trapped beneath the earth, in the heat rolling over ruined roads and in every living thing stubborn enough to keep growing.

A Sage does not simply take that power. They learn to make room for it. The first lessons are often humiliatingly quiet: sitting until ordinary chakra circulation feels deafening, learning where the body ends and the environment begins, and recognising the moment Natural Chakra stops being background pressure and starts answering back.

In the wilds around the Havens, this is not an abstract philosophy. The world is saturated with things that have survived too long, grown too large or adapted too completely to their surroundings. Great Beasts, ancient Summon Tribes, living forests, fungal colonies and stranger phenomena can all develop their own ways of balancing Natural Chakra. Those methods become Sage Traditions.

### THE SOURCE
{:#the-source}

A Source is more than a scroll or a teacher willing to explain the theory. Natural Chakra has to be experienced under conditions where the student cannot fall back on ordinary habits. A Tortoise Sage may be made to hold a river crossing against impossible weight. A Roc Sage may be abandoned on a cliff with no safe path down. A Serpent Sage may spend days learning to distinguish patience from hesitation.

Different Sources can teach the same Tradition differently. What remains consistent is the part of that training that survives translation: the instincts, body changes and Senjutsu methods recognised practitioners eventually share.

### THE SHAPE OF SAGE MODE
{:#the-shape-of-sage-mode}

Sage Mode is visible. Natural Chakra does not sit politely inside a human body; it changes the way the body carries itself. Pupils alter, markings spread, skin or hair changes texture, the voice picks up an animal rasp, dust or spores trail from movement, or the air around the Sage seems to move a moment too late.

These changes are cosmetic unless a Tradition or effect says otherwise. They normally fade when Sage Mode ends. The closer a Sage comes to Perfect Communion, the less the transformation resembles a disguise and the more it looks like the body remembering a shape it was always capable of taking.

### TRADITIONS AND SUMMONS
{:#traditions-and-summons}

A Summon Contract does not automatically teach Natural Chakra. Some tribes guard their Sage teachings more fiercely than their contracts, some have lost the practice entirely, and some creatures capable of teaching Senjutsu have never belonged to a formal tribe at all. The Source requirement represents earning access to that knowledge in character.

## CHOOSING A SAGE TRADITION
{:#choosing-a-sage-tradition}

When you learn Natural Chakra, choose the Sage Tradition taught by your Source. Its entry lists the physical changes caused by Sage Mode, its Signature Trait, its exclusive Sage Arts or Kata, and any special rules it uses.

Sage Kata count as Sage Arts for the purpose of learning and selecting them. Whenever Natural Chakra lets you learn a Sage Art or Sage Kata, choose a General Sage Art, a General Sage Kata, or an Art or Kata from your Tradition for which you meet the requirements. You cannot normally select Arts or Kata from another Tradition unless an effect explicitly allows it.

If your Source does not belong to an established Sage Tradition, use Wild Sage.

## WILD SAGE
{:#wild-sage}

A Wild Sage learned from a place, phenomenon, solitary creature or incomplete tradition rather than an established Summon Tribe. Their Sage Mode visibly reflects the environment in which they mastered it.

### Open Communion — Signature Trait
{:#open-communion-signature-trait}

Whenever you enter Sage Mode, choose Body, Instinct or Spirit. The chosen benefit lasts until Sage Mode ends.

Body — Gain Sage Power to Resistance rolls.

Instinct — Increase Sage Sense by 5\.

Spirit — Gain Sage Power to Chakra Control rolls.

Wild Sage has no exclusive list of Tradition Arts or Kata. Its strength is flexibility: a Wild Sage builds entirely from General Sage Arts and Sage Kata, reflecting a discipline learned from a place, phenomenon or solitary teacher rather than a school with generations of named techniques.

The Traditions below are established examples known in the wider world. Their names are human conveniences. A cliff-dwelling bird tribe may never call its teaching "Roc Sage," and a fungal network may not think of what it does as teaching at all. The entries below are complete Traditions and may be taught by any appropriate Source.

## GREAT TOAD SAGE
{:#great-toad-sage}

Great Toad teachings are built on an uncomfortable contradiction: absolute stillness followed by absolute commitment. Their students are made to sit through rain, hunger, insects and insult without breaking concentration, then expected to cross a battlefield in one explosive movement the instant the moment is right.

A Toad Sage is rarely graceful in the conventional sense. They settle low, read the field through vibration and pressure, then move with enough force that the ground seems surprised to still be beneath them.

Sage Mode Appearance — The stance broadens and drops, the pupils flatten horizontally, and rough markings gather around the eyes, throat and shoulders. At higher Sage Ranks, hard landings kick up rings of dust or ripple standing water even when the Sage barely seems to move.

### Stone-Bellied Patience — Signature Trait
{:#stone-bellied-patience-signature-trait}

Whenever Gather Natural Chakra resolves while you are in Sage Mode, choose Steady or Springing.

Steady — Until the beginning of your next non-Interrupt Action, gain Sage Power to Resistance rolls and reduce Knockback you suffer by 2 × Sage Power yards, minimum 0\.

Springing — Your next Taijutsu Attack gains Sage Power Accuracy. On a full hit, it also deals 2 × Sage Power Knockback. This benefit ends when that Attack resolves or when you begin a different non-Interrupt Action.

### Pond-Leap — Sage Art
{:#pond-leap-sage-art}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Movement, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Leap up to 5 × Sage Power yards to a legal location you can perceive. You may pass over creatures, gaps, low obstacles and difficult ground without reducing this distance. You must end the movement somewhere that can support you.

### Ripple Strike — Sage Art
{:#ripple-strike-sage-art}

\[Speed \+1, Modifier, Taijutsu\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Add Ripple Strike to a damaging Melee Taijutsu Attack when it is declared. On a full hit, that Attack deals 2 × Sage Power Knockback. If the target is moved at least 3 yards by this Knockback, make an Athletics Status Roll against their Resistance. On a failure, they are knocked Prone.

### Still-Water Listening — Sage Art
{:#still-water-listening-sage-art}

While in Sage Mode and in contact with the ground or a continuous body of water, increase Sage Sense by 5 for Awareness rolls made to detect, Search for or track moving creatures within 10 × Sage Rank yards that are touching the same continuous surface. This does not grant line of sight or automatically defeat Stealth; hidden creatures still contest your Search normally.

### Broad-Backed Guard — Sage Art
{:#broad-backed-guard-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 3\.

After you successfully use Take The Hit, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra. Reduce the Final Damage you receive from the intercepted Attack by 10% \+ (5% × Sage Power). Apply this reduction before Blocking.

### Heaven-Shaking Landing — Sage Kata
{:#heaven-shaking-landing-sage-kata}

\[Speed 6, Attack, Taijutsu, Movement\]

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

Cost: 2 Sage Chakra.

Leap up to 5 × Sage Power yards, then make either a Basic Attack: Unarmed or a Basic Attack: Weapon using a melee weapon you are wielding against a legal target. Use the chosen Basic Attack under the Sage Kata rules. On a full hit, add Sage Power Damage Dice and deal 3 × Sage Power Knockback. After the Knockback resolves, make an Athletics Status Roll against the target's Resistance. On a failure, they are knocked Prone. This Attack counts as Senjutsu.

## SERPENT SAGE
{:#serpent-sage}

Serpent teachings treat safety as a habit to be broken. Distance can be crossed. Restraints can be slipped. Skin can be shed. A patient enemy can wait longer than you can. Their Sages learn to let Natural Chakra travel through the body in long, continuous lines rather than explosive bursts.

Their training favours control over spectacle. A Serpent Sage wants the opponent committed, poisoned, bound or standing exactly where the next strike expects them to be.

Sage Mode Appearance — Pupils narrow, the shoulders and spine seem unnaturally loose, and scale-like markings creep along the jaw, neck, arms or back. Canines may sharpen and movement becomes disturbingly economical.

### Coiled Reach — Signature Trait
{:#coiled-reach-signature-trait}

While in Sage Mode, your Melee Taijutsu Attacks may originate up to Sage Power yards beyond their normal reach. This does not increase your Threatened Area. This increased origin distance stacks with other effects which explicitly extend where an Attack may originate.

### Shed the Binding — Sage Art
{:#shed-the-binding-sage-art}

\[Speed 1, Utility, Interrupt, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Use this as a Speed 1 Utility Action while suffering physical Immobilization, or as a Speed 1 Interrupt immediately after you gain physical Immobilization from restraints, bindings, grappling effects or terrain. Reduce that Immobilization by 2 \+ Sage Power. Shed the Binding cannot reduce Paralysis, mental effects or Immobilization caused by a Wound.

### Venomous Communion — Sage Art
{:#venomous-communion-sage-art}

When a damaging Taijutsu or Ninjutsu Attack fully hits and deals at least 1 damage after mitigation, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra and make a Chakra Control Status Roll against the target's Resistance. On a failure, the target suffers Poison equal to 1 \+ Sage Power for 5 \+ (5 × Sage Power) IC. Reapplying this Poison refreshes its duration; it does not stack with itself.

### Constricting Coil — Sage Art
{:#constricting-coil-sage-art}

\[Speed \+1, Modifier, Taijutsu\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Add Constricting Coil to a Melee Taijutsu Attack when it is declared. On a full hit, make an Athletics Status Roll against the target's Resistance. On a failure, they suffer Immobilization equal to 2 \+ Sage Power.

### Patient Predator — Sage Art
{:#patient-predator-sage-art}

Using Gather Natural Chakra while in Sage Mode does not reduce your current Stealth TN merely for being an Action. All other Stealth rules still apply, including any rule that would reduce your Stealth level.

### Perfect Shedding — Sage Art
{:#perfect-shedding-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

Once per Sage Mode, as a Speed 3 Utility Action, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra to reduce one Bleed, Burn, Immobilization, Paralysis or Poison affecting you by 2 \+ Sage Power. Perfect Shedding cannot remove Wounds or penalties caused directly by a Wound.

## ROC SAGE
{:#roc-sage}

Roc teachings begin by rejecting the idea that the battlefield is flat. Cliffs are roads. Rooftops are footholds. Falling is movement with a deadline. Their students learn to read open air the way other hunters read tracks.

Roc Sages favour sudden relocation, rescue and attacks from angles ordinary footwork cannot create. Their greatest weakness is the same thing they prize: once enclosed, grounded or denied room to move, much of their advantage disappears.

Sage Mode Appearance — Hair, cloth and loose equipment lift as though caught in a constant updraft. The eyes sharpen, feather-like chakra patterns form around the shoulders and forearms, and fast turns leave brief pinion-shaped trails in the air.

### Sky-Borne Balance — Signature Trait
{:#sky-borne-balance-signature-trait}

While in Sage Mode, up to Sage Power yards of the distance you move during each Move Action may cross a gap or travel vertically through unsupported air, provided you reach a legal surface at the end of that portion. Whenever a Sage Art moves you, that movement may travel vertically and across unsupported air for its full distance. You must still end the movement in a legal location unless the Art says otherwise. If you end unsupported, you begin Falling normally.

### Updraft Step — Sage Art
{:#updraft-step-sage-art}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Movement, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Move up to 5 \+ (5 × Sage Power) yards. Sky-Borne Balance applies to this movement, and you may change direction once during it.

### Talon Dive — Sage Kata
{:#talon-dive-sage-kata}

\[Speed \+1, Modifier, Taijutsu\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Add Talon Dive to a damaging Melee Taijutsu Attack if you moved at least 5 yards downward immediately before declaring it. On a full hit, add Sage Power Damage Dice and increase Final Damage by 5% × Sage Power. The Attack counts as Senjutsu.

### Wing Guard — Sage Art
{:#wing-guard-sage-art}

\[Interrupt, Defensive, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Declare after becoming the target of a Ranged Attack or Area Attack but before rolling Dodge. Gain 2 \+ Sage Power to that Dodge roll. If the defence succeeds, move up to Sage Power yards after the Attack resolves without spending AP. This uses the normal Speed of the Dodge and does not create a separate defence.

### Carry on the Wind — Sage Art
{:#carry-on-the-wind-sage-art}

\[Speed 4, Utility, Movement\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Choose one willing creature in Melee. Move yourself and that creature up to 3 \+ (3 × Sage Power) yards. Sky-Borne Balance applies. Both creatures must end in legal locations and within Melee of one another.

### Hang Upon the Sky — Sage Art
{:#hang-upon-the-sky-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

You may use Gather Natural Chakra while unsupported in the air. While Gathering this way you remain suspended instead of Falling. Your Movement Speed is 0 unless another Sage Art specifically allows you to move while Gathering. When Gather resolves or is aborted, if you are still unsupported you immediately begin Falling.

## TORTOISE SAGE
{:#tortoise-sage}

Tortoise teachings are old, slow and deeply unimpressed by urgency. Their answer to danger is not always to avoid it. Sometimes the correct response is to become the place where the attack stops.

Students are taught that endurance is not passive. Holding a doorway, sheltering another hunter or refusing to be moved can shape a battle as decisively as any strike. A Tortoise Sage spends Natural Chakra like masonry: layer by layer, exactly where the pressure will land.

Sage Mode Appearance — The silhouette thickens, geometric or plate-like markings form across the back and limbs, breathing slows, and the feet seem to settle into the ground. At high ranks, impacts against the Sage can sound like blows against stone or old timber.

### Ancient Carapace — Signature Trait
{:#ancient-carapace-signature-trait}

While in Sage Mode, increase Block's percentage damage reduction by 5% × Sage Power, to the normal maximum of 75%. You also gain Sage Power to Resistance rolls made against an Attack you Block.

### Still Shell — Sage Art
{:#still-shell-sage-art}

While Gathering Natural Chakra, you are considered to be Preparing Blocking and may use Block. Block is still an Interrupt and therefore normally aborts Gather after the Block resolves.

### Stoneback — Sage Art
{:#stoneback-sage-art}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Gain DR equal to 5 \+ (5 × Sage Power) until the beginning of your next non-Interrupt Action. Your Movement Speed is reduced by 50% while this effect lasts.

### Root the World — Sage Art
{:#root-the-world-sage-art}

Before making a Resistance roll against Knockback, Prone, Immobilization or another effect that would physically move or displace you, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra to gain 5 \+ Sage Power to that roll. This does not create a Resistance roll where none would normally be made.

### Sheltering Carapace — Sage Art
{:#sheltering-carapace-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 3\.

After you successfully use Take The Hit, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra to Block the intercepted Attack even if you were not Preparing Blocking. This Block applies only to that Attack.

### Unbroken Shell — Sage Art
{:#unbroken-shell-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

Once per battle, using Block while Gathering Natural Chakra does not abort Gather. The Block still uses its normal Interrupt Speed and all other effects of the Attack resolve normally.

## MOTH SAGE
{:#moth-sage}

Moth teachings treat perception as something physical. Air carries scent, dust, heat and vibration. Light reveals, but it also blinds. A battlefield is full of information before anyone makes a Search roll; most people are simply too loud inside their own heads to notice it.

Moth Sages specialise in controlling that information. They obscure lines of sight, mark prey with almost invisible powder and move through confusion without surrendering their own awareness.

Sage Mode Appearance — The eyes become glassy or faintly luminous, powder-fine scales drift from the skin or clothing, and wing-like patterns spread across the back, neck or temples. A motionless Moth Sage can look less asleep than intensely, unnervingly attentive.

### Powder-Sense — Signature Trait
{:#powder-sense-signature-trait}

While in Sage Mode, reduce the final Visibility Penalty applied to your Awareness and Defensive rolls by Sage Power, minimum 0\. Powder-Sense does not reduce Visibility Penalty on your Attacks.

### Dust Veil — Sage Art
{:#dust-veil-sage-art}

\[Speed 4, Utility, Area\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Fill a radius of 2 \+ Sage Power yards centred on you with drifting scales. The area imposes a Visibility Penalty equal to 2 \+ Sage Power under the normal rules for area-based Visibility. The veil lasts until the beginning of your next non-Interrupt Action. Powder-Sense applies normally.

### Powder Mark — Sage Art
{:#powder-mark-sage-art}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Search\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Choose one creature you can currently detect. Until Sage Mode ends or you mark a new creature, reduce that creature's Stealth TN against your Searches by 2 \+ Sage Power. Powder Mark does not prevent Stealth and does not reveal the target automatically.

### Moonwing Drift — Sage Art
{:#moonwing-drift-sage-art}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Movement, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Move up to 3 \+ (3 × Sage Power) yards. If you began this movement in Stealth, the movement does not reduce your Stealth level. Declaring the Action still reduces your Stealth TN by 5 normally.

### Blinding Scales — Sage Art
{:#blinding-scales-sage-art}

When a damaging Taijutsu or Ninjutsu Attack fully hits and deals at least 1 damage after mitigation, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra and make a Chakra Control Status Roll against the target's Resistance. On a failure, they suffer a Visibility Penalty equal to 2 \+ Sage Power for 10 IC. If they are already suffering a greater Visibility Penalty, use the greater value rather than adding them together.

### Cocooned Stillness — Sage Art
{:#cocooned-stillness-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

You may use Gather Natural Chakra at Stealth Level 3 without reducing your Stealth level or Stealth TN for declaring Gather. All other Actions and all other causes of losing Stealth function normally.

## CARRION SAGE
{:#carrion-sage}

Carrion traditions are taught by scavengers, corpse-crows, bone-pickers and predators clever enough to understand that the cleanest kill is often the one somebody else already started. They are not necessarily cruel. They are simply honest about injury.

A Carrion Sage reads blood loss, limping footwork, ragged breathing and the tiny mistakes made by a creature trying to pretend it is not hurt. Their Senjutsu is strongest once the battlefield has become messy.

Sage Mode Appearance — Features sharpen, dark markings gather around the eyes and nose, the teeth become more pronounced, and the Sage's attention repeatedly catches on blood, torn cloth and uneven movement. Even fresh blood can seem unnaturally vivid to them.

### Blood on the Wind — Signature Trait
{:#blood-on-the-wind-signature-trait}

While in Sage Mode, increase Sage Sense by 5 for Awareness and Survival rolls made specifically to detect, Search for or track a creature suffering a Wound or Bleed. Against those creatures, also reduce your final Visibility Penalty by Sage Power, minimum 0\. A successful detection tells you that the creature is injured, not the exact Wound or its statistics.

### Red Trail — Sage Art
{:#red-trail-sage-art}

Requires: Nature Pulse.

Nature Pulse has double its normal range when used to Search for creatures suffering a Wound or Bleed. Against those creatures, reduce the final Visibility Penalty of the Search by an additional Sage Power, minimum 0\.

### Scavenger's Claim — Sage Art
{:#scavenger-s-claim-sage-art}

\[Speed \+1, Modifier\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Add Scavenger's Claim to a damaging Taijutsu or Ninjutsu Attack against a creature suffering a Wound or Bleed. That Attack gains 10% \+ (5% × Sage Power) Wounding. Scavenger's Claim does not increase the Attack's Final Damage.

### Circle the Wounded — Sage Art
{:#circle-the-wounded-sage-art}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Choose one detected creature suffering a Wound or Bleed. Until Sage Mode ends or you choose a new target with Circle the Wounded, gain Sage Power to Defensive rolls against that creature and increase the distance of Move Actions and Actions with the Movement tag made directly toward it by 25%.

### Scavenger's Communion — Sage Art
{:#scavenger-s-communion-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 3\.

Once per battle, when a hostile creature you can detect suffers a Major, Severe or Critical Wound, gain 1 Sage Chakra after that Wound resolves, up to your maximum.

### Last Descent — Sage Kata
{:#last-descent-sage-kata}

\[Speed \+2, Modifier, Taijutsu\]

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Add Last Descent to a damaging Taijutsu Attack against a creature at 0 Vitality or suffering a Severe or Critical Wound. On a full hit, add 1 \+ Sage Power Damage Dice. The Attack counts as Senjutsu.

## FUNGAL SAGE
{:#fungal-sage}

Fungal traditions begin with a lesson most humans dislike: the mushroom is not the organism. The visible body is temporary. The real creature is the network beneath it, spread through soil, root and rot, touching a hundred places at once.

Fungal Sages are taught to think the same way. Position matters more than posture. Ground can carry information. A small patch of prepared terrain can become an extension of the Sage's senses or a trap that keeps an enemy exactly where the network wants them.

Sage Mode Appearance — Pale branching lines spread beneath the skin, dust-like motes drift from movement, and the Sage may smell faintly of wet soil, bark or rain. Some develop temporary cap, lichen or shelf-like growths along hair, shoulders or clothing.

### Rooted Communion — Signature Trait
{:#rooted-communion-signature-trait}

While in Sage Mode and in contact with natural earth, living vegetation or an area created by your Mycelial Ground, gain Sage Power to Resistance rolls and Sage Power to Chakra Control rolls made to prevent Gather Natural Chakra from being aborted by damage.

### Mycelial Ground — Sage Art
{:#mycelial-ground-sage-art}

\[Speed 4, Utility, Area\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Choose a point on solid ground within 10 yards. A radius of 2 \+ Sage Power yards around that point becomes Rocky Terrain with severity equal to Sage Power as mycelial growth spreads across the surface. You ignore Immobilization caused by your own Mycelial Ground. The area lasts until Sage Mode ends or you create a new Mycelial Ground.

### Spore Veil — Sage Art
{:#spore-veil-sage-art}

\[Speed 4, Utility, Area\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Choose a point within 10 yards. A radius of 2 \+ Sage Power yards fills with spores and imposes a Visibility Penalty equal to 2 \+ Sage Power under the normal rules for area-based Visibility. The veil lasts until the beginning of your next non-Interrupt Action. Reduce the Visibility Penalty you personally suffer from this Art by Sage Power before applying other reductions.

### Hyphal Snare — Sage Art
{:#hyphal-snare-sage-art}

\[Speed 5, Attack, Ninjutsu\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Range: 10 \+ (5 × Sage Power) yards.

Make a Ninjutsu Attack against one creature standing on natural ground or within your Mycelial Ground. On a full hit, this Attack deals no damage; instead make a Chakra Control Status Roll against the target's Resistance. On a failure, they suffer Immobilization equal to 2 \+ Sage Power. Hyphal Snare counts as Senjutsu.

### Deep Roots — Sage Art
{:#deep-roots-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 3\.

While touching natural earth, living vegetation or your Mycelial Ground, reduce the base Speed of Gather Natural Chakra by 1\. Its minimum Speed is unchanged.

### The Network Listens — Sage Art
{:#the-network-listens-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 4 and Nature Pulse.

While touching natural earth, living vegetation or your Mycelial Ground, double the range of Nature Pulse. You also increase Sage Sense by 5 for Survival rolls made to read disturbances, tracks or recent movement through that terrain.

## SALAMANDER SAGE
{:#salamander-sage}

Salamander teachings are about surviving the part where survival stops looking heroic. Heat, poison, exhaustion, torn muscle and choking air are treated as temporary problems to be managed long enough to reach safety or finish the fight.

Their Sages are not immortal and do not regrow lost limbs or erase Wounds by wishing hard enough. What they learn is how to keep a damaged body useful: shed what can be shed, slow what can be slowed and spend Natural Chakra where the body is failing fastest.

Sage Mode Appearance — Skin takes on a slick or patterned sheen, old scars darken, breath may steam in cool air, and minor cuts bead rather than run. During stronger techniques a thin translucent layer can peel away like shed skin.

### Regenerative Balance — Signature Trait
{:#regenerative-balance-signature-trait}

Whenever a Rest successfully reduces your current Stamina Penalty while you are in Sage Mode, also reduce one Burn, Bleed or Poison affecting you by 1 \+ Sage Power, minimum 0\. A Status reduced to 0 this way ends. Regenerative Balance cannot heal Wounds.

### Shed Pain — Sage Art
{:#shed-pain-sage-art}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Reduce one Immobilization or Paralysis effect affecting you by 2 \+ Sage Power, or stand from Prone. Shed Pain cannot reduce a Status or penalty caused directly by a Wound. If you use Shed Pain to stand, you may also move up to Sage Power yards.

### Hardy Blood — Sage Art
{:#hardy-blood-sage-art}

While in Sage Mode, gain Sage Power to Resistance rolls made to resist Burn, Poison, Suffocation and environmental conditions caused by extreme heat, extreme cold, fumes or contaminated terrain. Hardy Blood does not create a Resistance roll where none would normally be allowed.

### Regrowth — Sage Art
{:#regrowth-sage-art}

\[Speed 5, Utility, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Restore Vitality equal to 5% × Sage Power of your maximum Vitality, up to your normal maximum. Regrowth does not restore HP, heal Wounds or remove the effects of a Wound.

### Molt Away — Sage Art
{:#molt-away-sage-art}

\[Speed 5, Utility, Self\]

Requires: Sage Rank 3\.

Cost: 2 Sage Chakra.

Choose one non-Wound Bleed, Burn, Immobilization, Paralysis or Poison affecting you with severity no greater than 5 \+ Sage Power. End that Status. Molt Away cannot remove an effect which explicitly cannot be reduced or removed.

### Refuse the Ash — Sage Art
{:#refuse-the-ash-sage-art}

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

Once per battle, when a Stamina or Chakra Exhaustion roll would advance you beyond maximum Fatigue and Incapacitate you, you may spend 2 Sage Chakra after the roll. The triggering Action resolves and you remain at maximum Fatigue instead, even if the normal Fatigue rules would cause that Action or technique to fail to activate. Refuse the Ash does not prevent Incapacitation caused by HP, Wounds or another effect, and you must have at least 2 Sage Chakra to use it.


# SAGE ARTS
{:#sage-arts}

Unless an Art says otherwise, you must be in Sage Mode to use it. If an Art scales using Sage Power, use your current Sage Power when the effect is declared.

## DEEP RESERVOIR
{:#deep-reservoir}

\[Stackable, Maximum 2\]

Increase your maximum Sage Chakra by 2\.

## SWIFT COMMUNION
{:#swift-communion}

Reduce the base Speed of Gather Natural Chakra by 2\. Its minimum Speed remains 5\.

## STABLE BALANCE
{:#stable-balance}

Reduce Sage Mode's Upkeep by 1, minimum 0\.

## NATURE'S EYES
{:#nature-s-eyes}

Increase Sage Sense by 5\. When you Search for a living creature or active chakra source, reduce your final Visibility Penalty by Sage Power, minimum 0\. This never reveals a creature which still successfully contests your Search.

## NATURE'S UNDERSTANDING
{:#nature-s-understanding}

Gain \+5 on Chakra Control and Survival rolls made specifically to identify, understand, manipulate or endure Natural Chakra, Senjutsu, Sage creatures or environments saturated with Natural Chakra. At Sage Rank 4+, this bonus becomes \+10.

## SAGE'S CORRECTION
{:#sage-s-correction}

After making an Accuracy Roll, Defensive roll, Chakra Control roll, Resistance roll or other Skill roll but before its result is resolved, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra to add 2 \+ Sage Power to the roll. Sage's Correction does not modify static Accuracy and may be used only once per roll.

## SAGE'S BREATH
{:#sage-s-breath}

When you make a Chakra Exhaustion or Stamina roll caused by an Action you are taking, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra before rolling to gain \+5 \+ Sage Power to that roll. This is a bonus, not a reroll.

## NATURAL KATA
{:#natural-kata}

While in Sage Mode, your Melee Taijutsu Attacks may originate up to 1 \+ Sage Power yards beyond their normal reach as Natural Chakra follows through the strike. This does not increase your Threatened Area.

When a Melee Taijutsu Attack made through this extended reach fully hits, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra to add Sage Damage again to that Attack's Final Damage. This additional Sage Damage does not stack with itself.

## SENJUTSU INFUSION
{:#senjutsu-infusion}

When declaring a damaging Ninjutsu Attack, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra. Choose one:

- Gain 1 \+ Sage Power Accuracy for that Attack; or

- Increase its Final Damage by 10% \+ (5% × Sage Power).

Choose before making the Attack roll. The technique counts as Senjutsu.

## NATURE GUARD
{:#nature-guard}

\[Interrupt, Defensive, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Declare after you become the target of an Attack but before your defence is rolled. Gain 2 \+ Sage Power to one Dodge or Parry roll against that Attack. Nature Guard uses the normal Speed of the Defensive Interrupt you choose; it does not create a separate defence.

## PREDATOR'S STEP
{:#predator-s-step}

When you declare a Move Action or another Action with the Movement tag, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra to increase the distance you can move by 25% × Sage Power. This changes distance, not Speed.

## NATURE PULSE
{:#nature-pulse}

\[Speed 4, Utility, Search\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Make an Awareness Search for living creatures and active chakra sources within 10 × Sage Rank yards. You may Search even without line of sight, although walls, dense terrain, distance and overwhelming ambient chakra may still apply Visibility Penalty. Hidden creatures contest the Search normally with Stealth.

On success, learn direction and rough distance. Nature Pulse does not reveal statistics, Jutsu, identity or other information that could not be inferred from the sensed chakra.

## SAGE'S RECOVERY
{:#sage-s-recovery}

When you complete a Rest while in Sage Mode, you may spend 1 Sage Chakra. Improve the Rest's reduction to your current Stamina Penalty by Sage Power.

## STILLNESS IN MOTION
{:#stillness-in-motion}

Requires: Sage Rank 3\.

When you declare Gather Natural Chakra, you may choose to remain mobile. If you do, Gather's normal Movement Speed 0 restriction is replaced: during its Delay, you may move each IC as though you were taking a Move Action, but at 25% of your normal movement rate. This movement is part of Gather, does not count as declaring another Action, and does not abort Gather. You still cannot declare another non-Interrupt Action until Gather resolves, and all other interruption rules apply.

## FLOWING COMMUNION
{:#flowing-communion}

Requires: Sage Rank 4\.

When Gather Natural Chakra resolves while you are in Sage Mode, you may immediately spend 1 AP. If you do, gain 1 additional Sage Chakra. You cannot gain more than 2 Sage Chakra from the same Gather Action.

## SAGE RESERVOIR
{:#sage-reservoir}

Requires: Sage Rank 4 and Deep Reservoir.

Once per battle, when an effect would spend your final Sage Chakra, reduce that Sage Chakra cost by 1, minimum 0\. If this prevents you from reaching 0 Sage Chakra, Sage Mode continues.

## NATURE WITHOUT THOUGHT
{:#nature-without-thought}

Requires: Sage Rank 5\.

Once per battle, the first Sage Art or Sage Kata you use after entering Sage Mode costs 1 less Sage Chakra, minimum 0\. This cannot reduce a cost which is already 0\.


# SAGE KATA
{:#sage-kata}

Sage Kata are active combat techniques fuelled by Sage Chakra. For learning and selection, every Sage Kata is a Sage Art. They are not Jutsu unless their text says otherwise and require no Handseals by default. Each Kata follows the normal rules for its listed Action and Attack type.

When a Sage Kata tells you to make a Basic Attack, keep that Basic Attack's normal Accuracy modifiers, Damage Dice, Damage Type, Reach, Stamina cost and applicable weapon or unarmed properties, but use the Kata's listed Speed and Delay. If the Kata lists no Delay, its Delay is 0\. Calculate Damage Bonus using the Kata's final Speed as normal unless the Kata says otherwise.

## SAGE STRIKE
{:#sage-strike}

\[Speed 4, Attack, Taijutsu\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Range: Melee.

Make either a Basic Attack: Unarmed or a Basic Attack: Weapon using a melee weapon you are wielding. Use the chosen Basic Attack under the Sage Kata rules above. On a full hit, add 1 \+ Sage Power Damage Dice and increase Final Damage by 5% × Sage Power. This Attack counts as Senjutsu.

## SAGE REVERSAL
{:#sage-reversal}

Trigger: Successful Parry of a Melee Attack.

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

After the triggering Attack resolves, move up to Sage Power yards without spending AP. Sage Reversal is part of the successful Parry; it does not create a second Interrupt or change that Parry's Speed. This movement must follow a legal path and cannot move you through occupied spaces or terrain you could not normally cross.

## EARTHEN ROOT
{:#earthen-root}

\[Speed 4, Utility, Self\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Until the beginning of your next non-Interrupt Action, gain 3 \+ Sage Power to Resistance rolls against forced movement, being knocked Prone, or effects which would physically displace you. Your Movement Speed is 0 while this effect lasts.

## HUNTER'S SENSE
{:#hunter-s-sense}

\[Speed 3, Utility, Search\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Choose one creature you can currently detect. Until Sage Mode ends or you choose a new target with Hunter's Sense, gain Sage Power Accuracy against that creature and add Sage Sense to Awareness rolls made to keep track of it. You gain no benefit while you cannot detect the target by any sense.

## NATURAL SHOCK
{:#natural-shock}

\[Speed 5, Attack, Ninjutsu\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Range: 10 \+ (5 × Sage Power) yards.

Make a Ninjutsu Attack against one creature. On a full hit, deal 2 \+ Sage Power Damage Dice \+ NDB. Natural Shock counts as Senjutsu and has no Element unless your Tradition gives it one.

## SAGE BREAK
{:#sage-break}

\[Speed \+2, Modifier\]

Cost: 1 Sage Chakra.

Add Sage Break to a damaging Taijutsu or Ninjutsu Attack when it is declared. The Attack gains Sage Power Penetration and ignores 5% × Sage Power of Final Damage reduction applied by defensive effects, to a minimum of 0% reduction.


# MASTERY — PERFECT COMMUNION
{:#mastery-perfect-communion}

After taking three Natural Chakra Developments, your body no longer has to fight every step of the balancing process.

Reduce the base Speed of Gather Natural Chakra by 1 and its minimum Speed by 1\. Reduce Sage Mode's Upkeep by 1, minimum 0\.

The first time you enter Sage Mode in each battle, gain 1 Sage Chakra after Sage Mode resolves. This cannot take you above your maximum.

Perfect Communion does not remove the need to Gather. A mastered Sage is faster at finding balance, not permanently full of Natural Chakra.


# NATURAL IMBALANCE
{:#natural-imbalance}

You normally cannot Gather beyond your maximum Sage Chakra. If an effect forces more Natural Chakra into you than you can safely hold, you become Imbalanced until your Sage Chakra is at or below its maximum.

While Imbalanced, you cannot Gather Natural Chakra, suffer \-2 to Chakra Control rolls, and your Movement Speed is reduced by 50%. At the end of each of your non-Interrupt Actions, lose 1 Sage Chakra until you are no longer above your maximum.

If an individual Tradition, monster or technique can cause petrification, mutation or another severe consequence from Natural Imbalance, that consequence is written into that effect. Permanent transformation is not a default risk of using Natural Chakra normally.


# RULES CLARIFICATIONS
{:#rules-clarifications}

Chakra Exhaustion — Sage Chakra does not replace a Jutsu's Chakra Cost. A Jutsu modified by a Sage Art still makes its normal Chakra Exhaustion roll.

AP — AP may reduce Gather Natural Chakra and Sage Mode normally unless an effect says otherwise. Their minimum Speeds still apply.

Clones — A Clone has no Sage Chakra unless its own rules explicitly say it can hold that resource. Sage Chakra held by a Clone is separate from its creator and cannot normally be transferred.

Summons — A Summon does not gain its summoner's Natural Chakra Talent. Sage creatures may possess their own Natural Chakra rules and may act as a Source for the Talent.

Modes — Sage Mode may coexist with another Mode unless either effect says otherwise. All Upkeep is added normally.

Genjutsu — Sage Mode does not inherently increase Genjutsu Accuracy or damage. Sage sensory effects may help locate a Genjutsu user but do not automatically identify or break a Genjutsu.

Senjutsu — Senjutsu is a tag for an effect empowered by balanced Natural Chakra. The tag has no automatic numerical benefit of its own.
