# CINDER RELEASE
{:#cinder-release}

***Katon → Cinder | Scar: Ash***

*Katon is about the flame. Cinder Release is about what survives it. Soot, embers and half-dead fire turn the battlefield into fuel for the next ignition.*

## Feral Conversion
{:#feral-conversion}

After a damaging Feralized Katon Jutsu resolves, apply Ash equal to its Feral Intensity. An AoE applies that Ash to its Area. A single-target Attack can apply the Ash directly to the creature.

When a Cinder Jutsu affects a creature or Area carrying Ash, consume Ash from one matching source up to the Jutsu's Feral Intensity. Each Ash consumed adds \+5% Final Damage and \+3 Ignite. A Jutsu with no printed Ignite gains Ignite equal to 3 × Ash consumed.

## Ash
{:#ash}

### Scarred — Ash 1–2
{:#scarred-ash-1-2}

An Area containing Ash imposes Visibility equal to half its Ash Severity, rounded up. Ash carried directly by a creature moves with them.

Cinder Attacks gain \+1 Accuracy against a creature affected by Ash 2+.

### Feral — Ash 3–4
{:#feral-ash-3-4}

At Ash 3+, effects that reduce Ignite remove half as much Ignite, rounded down. Cinder Attacks against the creature gain \+10% Final Damage and \+1 Accuracy.

Whenever a creature affected by Ash 3+ takes Ignite Damage, it takes additional Damage equal to NDB × its Ash Severity. This additional Damage does not Wound. At Ash 4, the Cinder Final Damage bonus becomes \+15%.

### Overrun — Ash 5
{:#overrun-ash-5}

At Ash 5, the creature's Ignite holds its Severity instead of naturally reducing. Cinder Attacks against it gain \+25% Final Damage and \+2 Accuracy.

When a Cinder Attack hits a creature at Ash 5, consume all 5 Ash to add another \+25% Final Damage and immediately resolve its current Ignite once after the Attack without reducing that Ignite.

## Apex Mutation — The Last Ember
{:#apex-mutation-the-last-ember}

Ash never interferes with your vision. Each Ash you consume adds \+4 Ignite instead of \+3. Consuming 5 Ash from a single creature or Area leaves Ash 1 on that source after the Action resolves.

## Native Feral Jutsu
{:#native-feral-jutsu}

### Cinder Release: Ashen Wake
{:#cinder-release-ashen-wake}

***C-Rank***

*A dirty wave of flame crawls across the battlefield. The fire lasts only a moment. The glowing ash left behind is the real technique.*

***Chakra 20 | Damage 10d12 | Range 25 | Area 12 | Speed 14 | Seal Speed 15 | Ignite 3***

***Tags: Attack, AoE, Energy, Environmental, Katon, Cinder***

When Ashen Wake consumes Ash through Cinder Release, increase its Area by 2 per Ash consumed. The expanded Area counts as affected by the Jutsu. After the Attack resolves, the full Area gains Ash 1\.

### Cinder Release: Ashfall
{:#cinder-release-ashfall}

***B-Rank***

*A slow fall of soot and ember fills the air. Every surface darkens, every breath tastes burnt and the whole field waits for a spark.*

***Chakra 30 | Upkeep 6 | Range 30 | Area 20 | Speed 14 | Seal Speed 22***

***Tags: Utility, AoE, Environmental, Katon, Cinder***

On activation, the Area gains Ash 2\. Ashfall Anchors Ash in its Area while its Upkeep is maintained.

Every 10 IC after activation, increase that Area's Ash by 1, to a maximum of 5\. Ignite affecting creatures inside Ashfall gains \+1 Severity whenever the Area's Ash increases.

### Cinder Release: Funeral Pyre
{:#cinder-release-funeral-pyre}

***A-Rank***

*Every ember and soot-covered surface lifts into the air. For one horrible instant the battlefield glows orange. Then all of it catches.*

***Chakra 46 | Damage 40d10 | Range 40 | Area 20 | Speed 22 | Seal Speed 26 | Ignite 8***

***Tags: Attack, AoE, Energy, Environmental, Katon, Cinder***

Before resolving the Attack, consume up to 5 Ash from the Area. Every creature hit takes \+7% Final Damage and \+3 Ignite per Ash consumed.

Against a creature carrying Ash directly, you can consume its Ash instead or combine it with Area Ash, to a maximum of 5 Ash used against that creature. Consuming 5 Ash for a creature immediately resolves its existing Ignite once after Funeral Pyre without reducing that Ignite.

After the Attack resolves, Funeral Pyre applies Ash 1 to its Area.
