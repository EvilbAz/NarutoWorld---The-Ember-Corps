# GLASS RELEASE
{:#glass-release}

***Doton → Glass | Scar: Shards***

*Doton users build things to last. Glass Release builds them to break. Earth flashes into brilliant blades and structures whose destruction is part of the technique.*

## Feral Conversion
{:#feral-conversion}

Damaging Feralized Doton Jutsu inflict Slashing Wounds. A damaging Glass Jutsu affecting an Area applies Shards equal to its Feral Intensity to that Area. A single-target Glass Attack creates an Area 1 of Shards beneath the target.

A Glass Barrier or Construct applies Shards equal to its Feral Intensity across its occupied Area when it ends or is destroyed.

## Shards
{:#shards}

### Scarred — Shards 1–2
{:#scarred-shards-1-2}

An Area containing Shards counts as Rocky Terrain equal to its Shards Severity and provides suitable earth for Glass and Doton Jutsu.

When declaring a Glass Jutsu whose origin or affected Area overlaps Shards, consume Shards up to that Jutsu's Feral Intensity. Each Shard consumed reduces final Chakra cost by 1 and final Seal Speed by 1, minimum 0\.

### Feral — Shards 3–4
{:#feral-shards-3-4}

At Shards 3+, Glass Attacks originating inside or targeting the Area gain \+1 Accuracy and \+10% Wounding. Glass Barriers and Constructs created there gain \+10 DR per Shards Severity present when created.

At Shards 4, Glass Attacks also gain \+10% Final Damage.

### Overrun — Shards 5
{:#overrun-shards-5}

Glass Attacks against creatures inside a Shards 5 Area gain \+20% Final Damage and \+2 Accuracy.

Once per Action when a Glass Attack hits a creature inside the Area, consume any number of Shards from that Area. Each Shard consumed deals an additional 4d10 Slashing Damage. If all 5 Shards are consumed at once, combine that bonus Damage with the Attack's Final Damage for Wounding and add \+25% Wounding.

## Apex Mutation — Perfect Fracture
{:#apex-mutation-perfect-fracture}

Shards never impede your movement. Each Shard consumed for Glass Release's Chakra cost and Seal Speed reduction reduces both by 2 instead of 1\. Consuming 5 Shards in a single Action adds \+10% Wounding to that Action.

## Native Feral Jutsu
{:#native-feral-jutsu}

### Glass Release: Splinter Lance
{:#glass-release-splinter-lance}

***C-Rank***

*The ground flashes white and translucent before bursting upward into a needle of razor glass. Fragments scatter across the impact site.*

***Chakra 20 | Damage 13d10 | Range 30 | Area 5 | Speed 14 | Seal Speed 15***

***Tags: Attack, Projectile, Slashing, Ground-Based, Doton, Glass***

Splinter Lance gains \+2 Accuracy against a target standing within Shards. After the Attack resolves, its Area gains Shards 1\.

### Glass Release: Mirror Bastion
{:#glass-release-mirror-bastion}

***B-Rank***

*A perfectly smooth wall of translucent glass erupts from the earth. Every impact sends another web of fractures racing through the battlefield around it.*

***Chakra 30 | Upkeep 6 | Range 20 | Speed 16 | Seal Speed 22***

***Tags: Utility, Barrier, Ground-Based, Doton, Glass***

Create a Glass wall up to 12 yards long, 6 yards high and 1 yard wide. It has HP equal to (NDB \+ 20\) × 8 and 15 DR. The wall remains while its Upkeep is maintained.

Mirror Bastion Anchors Shards within 5 yards of the wall. Once per Action when the wall takes 50 or more Final Damage, apply Shards 1 to an Area 3 touching the wall on the attacker's side.

Reducing the wall to 0 HP ends Mirror Bastion's Upkeep. When Mirror Bastion ends, apply Shards 2 within 5 yards of the wall. If the wall was destroyed, every creature within 3 yards defends against a Speed 10 Attack using your Ninjutsu Accuracy. On hit, it deals 18d10 Slashing Damage without Speed × NDB.

### Glass Release: Cathedral of Knives
{:#glass-release-cathedral-of-knives}

***A-Rank***

*The landscape becomes transparent. Stone, rubble and earth vanish beneath rising glass spires. A heartbeat later the entire cathedral begins to crack.*

***Chakra 48 | Damage 38d10 | Range 40 | Area 25 | Speed 24 | Seal Speed 27 | Immobilization 5***

***Tags: Attack, AoE, Slashing, Environmental, Ground-Based, Doton, Glass***

After the initial Attack resolves, Cathedral of Knives applies Shards 3\. You may immediately Shatter any number of Shards from the Area and make one additional Attack at \-2 Accuracy against every creature still inside. The Shatter deals 8d10 Slashing Damage per Shard consumed and adds no Speed × NDB.

Consuming 5 Shards combines Cathedral's initial Final Damage and the Shatter's Final Damage for Wounding and adds \+25% Wounding.
