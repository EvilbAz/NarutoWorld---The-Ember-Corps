# **Sword Bukijutsu**
{:#sword-bukijutsu}

*Sword is the dueling weapon: it reads defensive habits, changes angle and punishes predictable answers*.

## **E-Rank**
{:#e-rank-10}

#### **Rising Cut**
{:#rising-cut}

The cut is meant to raise the opponent’s hands and eyes. A clean hit gives you the choice to press that lifted guard or take the outside angle before they can square up again.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \-1  
Tags: Chain 1, Sword, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one: Guard Lift — note the Defense used against this Attack; if the target uses that same broad Defense against your next Sword Chain attack before 20 IC, that Defense takes \-2; or Take the Outside — after the Attack resolves, you may use any remaining Move with an Attack distance in any legal direction.

#### **Draw Cut**
{:#draw-cut}

The sheath is used as part of the attack rather than as storage. You can spend the draw on raw timing, or let the same motion carry you past the target instead of stopping in front of them.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+4  
Damage: \+1dS  
Speed: \+0  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Sword, Attack, Movement  
Effects: If the sword began sheathed, choose one before rolling: Flash Draw — reduce this Attack’s final Speed by 2 after modifiers; or Passing Draw — do not gain that Speed reduction, but after a full hit you may use any remaining Move with an Attack distance in any legal direction. The sword ends drawn either way.

#### **Descending Cut**
{:#descending-cut}

This is the weight of the sequence coming down after the entry has already forced the guard upward. The user decides whether to cash that opening out as damage or smother the defender’s weapon line.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+1  
Tags: Chain 2, Sword, Attack  
Effects: If your previous Chain attack was Chain 1, choose one when this Attack fully hits: Drop Weight — treat this Attack as 4 Speed higher for damage only; or Smother the Guard — note the Defense used against this Attack; that same broad Defense takes \-2 against your next Sword Chain attack before 20 IC.

#### **Finishing Cut**
{:#finishing-cut}

This is where the sword chain is meant to end. The user commits the hips and shoulders to a cut chosen for wound placement, not simply for making another contact.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+3  
Accuracy: \-1  
Tags: Chain 3, Sword, Attack  
Effects: \+10% Wounding. You may make this a Called Shot; reduce that Called Shot penalty by 2\.

#### **Half-Step**
{:#half-step}

The step changes the user’s relationship to the target without putting the sword away or letting the chain go neutral. It is footwork for changing shoulders, hips, and escape lanes while the blade remains live.

Rank: E  
Stamina: 4  
Speed: 2  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility, Movement  
Effects: Move up to 3 yards without breaking your Chain. You may move around the chained target while remaining within its Reach.

#### **Iaidō**
{:#iaido}

A draw-cut performed from stillness, relying on timing and a clean line rather than a long wind-up. The sheath, hips, and first step all become part of the same motion, so the technique is strongest when used before the sword has already been brought into a wide exchange.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+6  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \-2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Requires a sheathed sword. On the first use against a creature this combat, its Defensive Interrupts count as \+2 Base Speed for Defense Validity. Ends drawn.

## **D-Rank**
{:#d-rank-10}

#### **Reversing Cut**
{:#reversing-cut}

The blade crosses the line the previous attack established, forcing the defender to decide whether to repeat a familiar answer or abandon it and give up position.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Sword, Attack, Movement  
Effects: Read the Defense used against your previous Chain attack. If the target uses the same broad Defense again, that Defense takes \-2. If it changes to a different broad Defense instead, after this Attack resolves you may use any remaining Move with an Attack distance in any legal direction.

#### **Reverse Edge**
{:#reverse-edge}

The blade never truly resets after the previous exchange. A recovery that would normally bring the sword home is turned into a second cutting line, especially punishing a defense that only barely caught the first.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+10  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \-1  
Tags: Chain 3, Sword, Attack  
Effects: If the previous Chain attack was partially defended, treat the target's Defense as 1 point worse for this Attack.

#### **Bind and Recover**
{:#bind-and-recover}

The incoming weapon is received on the strong of the blade, then guided rather than simply stopped. Good contact leaves your own edge pointed somewhere useful for the answer.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 6  
Speed: 5  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Parry a Melee Attack with \+1. On a full success, your next Sword Chain attack against that attacker gains \+2 Accuracy.

#### **Tempered Edge**
{:#tempered-edge}

Chakra is run along the cutting geometry of the weapon, tightening the edge and keeping it from skidding or twisting on hard contact.

Rank: D  
Chakra: 12  
Upkeep: 2  
Speed: 7  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Sword, Utility  
Effects: One sword gains \+10% Wounding. This Wounding bonus does not stack with a Wounding bonus printed on a Sword Bukijutsu; use the higher value.

## **C-Rank**
{:#c-rank-10}

#### **Half-Sword Thrust**
{:#half-sword-thrust}

You shorten the sword and drive the point through a seam in the target’s protection.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+9  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+2  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Treat this Attack as Piercing. Reduce the target Armor’s Absorption by 10% for this Attack, to a minimum of 0%.

#### **Broken Rhythm**
{:#broken-rhythm}

The user sells one line with the eyes, shoulders, or early blade movement and attacks the reaction to it. The technique is strongest when the opponent believes they recognized the first intention.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 7  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility  
Effects: Opposed Espionage vs Awareness against your chained target. On success, its next Defense against your Sword Chain attack before 20 IC takes \-2.

#### **Circular Draw**
{:#circular-draw}

The draw opens into a broad arc that claims space rather than chasing one perfect guard. The user decides how much of the motion is spent threatening several bodies and how much remains for recovery.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+13  
Damage: \+6dS  
Speed: \+4  
Line: 3, 1  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Attack every chosen enemy in the line with one Accuracy roll; divide Speed×PDB evenly among targets hit.

## **B-Rank**
{:#b-rank-10}

#### **Intercepting Draw**
{:#intercepting-draw}

The draw and the defense are the same motion; the blade clears the sheath as it catches the incoming line.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 12  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Attack, Interrupt  
Effects: Requires a sheathed sword. Parry a Melee Attack with \+2. On a full success, immediately make a Sword Basic Weapon Attack against the attacker at 60% final damage. End drawn.

#### **Draw Reset**
{:#draw-reset}

The sheath becomes a timing tool. A practiced resheath threatens another draw while preserving the rhythm of the current exchange instead of signalling that the fight has reset.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 8  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility  
Effects: Sheathe one sword you hold. Your next Sword Chain 1 before 20 IC has \-1 Speed.

## **A-Rank**
{:#a-rank-10}

#### **Final Measure**
{:#final-measure}

You commit to the exact line the exchange has exposed, choosing whether to destroy the guard or carry the target through the cut.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+24  
Damage: \+12dS  
Speed: \+4  
Accuracy: \-1  
Special: Knockback 5 (Carry Through only)  
Tags: Chain 3, Sword, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one: Sever Guard — if a weapon was used to Block or Parry this Attack, it gains 4 WDP after the Defense resolves; or Carry Through — resolve the Knockback Special in the direction of the Attack. If it succeeds, you may use your Move with an Attack movement to follow the target, ending adjacent if you can reach it.

#### **Perfect Bind**
{:#perfect-bind}

You catch the attacking weapon close to its strong and turn the opponent with the bind rather than simply stopping them.

Rank: A  
Stamina: 13  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Parry a Melee Attack with \+2. On a full success, choose one: Turn the Line — make an Athletics Status Roll against the attacker’s Resistance; on success, move the attacker up to 3 yards to a legal space within your Reach; or Seal the Line — the attacking weapon takes \-3 Parry until the attacker’s next action.
