# Thrown Bukijutsu
{:#thrown-bukijutsu}

*Thrown weapons are the toolbox: ricochets, wire, multiple targets and prepared gear reward adapting to the battlefield*.

## E-Rank
{:#e-rank-17}

#### Double Cast
{:#double-cast}

Two compatible tools leave the hand on nearly the same line. A full hit rewards the double release, while a Partial Success still catches only one weapon cleanly and can establish the chain.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 1, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Throw two compatible weapons on one line. One Accuracy roll; on a full hit both land for 55% damage each. On a partial hit only one lands for full damage. Chain 1 is established if either hits.

#### Needle Lead
{:#needle-lead}

The first small projectile is placed where a minor injury can matter tactically. The user chooses whether the hit should bleed or simply spoil the target’s next attack.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+1dS  
Speed: \-1  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Requires Kunai, Shuriken or Senbon. On a full hit choose one: Bleed — make the normal Athletics Status Roll for Bleed 2; or Disrupt — the target takes \-2 Accuracy on its next Attack before 20 IC.

#### Manipulated Shuriken
{:#manipulated-shuriken}

Wire keeps a missed weapon relevant. Instead of accepting the first failed trajectory, the user spends the committed line to make the target answer a second, weaker approach.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Speed: \+3  
Tags: Chain 2, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Requires Ninja Wire equal to Range. If your thrown Attack is successfully defended against, force the target to repeat that same Defense once at no additional Stamina, Chakra or AP cost. If the repeated Defense fails, the redirected Attack hits, but treat its final Speed as half as high for damage only. The wire is committed until recovered.

#### Fuuma Barrage
{:#fuuma-barrage}

Fuuma shuriken are committed in sequence rather than as one oversized hit. Repeated misses help the user walk the line onto the target, while each successful heavy weapon contributes its own share of the finisher.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Speed: \+6  
Tags: Chain 3, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Fuuma Shuriken only. Throw 2 weapons in succession; one Accuracy roll starts at \-2 and improves by \+1 for each previous miss. Each hit deals an equal share of the attack’s base weapon dice plus 2dS. Divide Speed×PDB evenly among the two weapons.

#### Hidden Mouth Needle
{:#hidden-mouth-needle}

A concealed senbon is produced without the ordinary draw that warns people a thrown attack is coming. The motion hides in breath, speech, and head movement until the needle is already in flight.

Rank: E  
Chakra: 6  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Thrown, Utility  
Effects: Produce and spit one concealed Senbon without drawing ammunition. You may include it in a Senbon Multi-Throw if you can normally multi-throw Senbon. The first time in a battle you use Hidden Mouth Needle against a creature that has not seen you use it, that Attack is treated as a Surprise Attack even if you were not hidden.

#### Field Volley
{:#field-volley}

The throw widens into a spread of tools that blankets an area, the user fanning their release so several targets are threatened by the same handful.

Rank: E  
Stamina: 6  
Speed: 9  
Area: 4 within normal thrown Range  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Make one multi-throw Accuracy roll against every chosen creature in Area. Each target can be hit by at most 1 weapon.

## D-Rank
{:#d-rank-17}

#### Banked Throw
{:#banked-throw}

The throw is aimed at hard terrain on purpose. A banked angle can ignore some of the defense granted by cover and lets the user choose where the weapon finishes for later retrieval.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+7  
Damage: \+4dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Requires a hard surface within 10 yards of the target. Attack from the ricochet angle. If the target uses the Cover Interrupt, reduce Cover’s \+5 bonus by 3\. On a full hit you may redirect the weapon to an adjacent empty space for retrieval.

#### Handful Throw
{:#handful-throw}

The user empties a full handful in one practiced release, splitting the weapons between targets as the throw opens across the battlefield.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+12  
Speed: \+6  
Tags: Chain 3, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Multi-throw up to 3 weapons beyond your normal limit. Split them among any targets in Range. Each target defends separately; weapons against the same target combine only for Wounding.

#### Return Flick
{:#return-flick}

A missed or deflected weapon is treated as recoverable equipment rather than lost ammunition. The user reads the rebound or wire line and pulls the tool back before the fight moves on.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 4  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Thrown, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: After a thrown weapon misses or is deflected, retrieve one weapon within 10 yards if a plausible line exists.

#### Throwing Form
{:#throwing-form}

Years of throwing have stripped wasted motion from the draw, grip, and release. Several tools can be indexed between the fingers and launched without the hands tangling or the stance collapsing.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 9  
Upkeep: 2  
Speed: 7  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Thrown, Utility  
Effects: Reduce the Speed added by multi-throw by 1 and increase your multi-throw limit by 2\. This cannot reduce a multi-throw below total Speed 5 or below any other normal system minimum.

## C-Rank
{:#c-rank-17}

#### Crossfire Cast
{:#crossfire-cast}

You split the throw across two opponents, using separate lines so neither target can read the whole release from one hand motion.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+8  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 1, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Throw one compatible weapon at each of two different creatures at 60% final damage. Roll separately. If either hits, choose one hit creature as your chained target.

#### Corner Wire
{:#corner-wire}

Ninja Wire is attached or routed before the next throw so the projectile can travel farther and turn a corner the hand alone could not produce. The line must still make physical sense on the battlefield.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 7  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Thrown, Utility  
Effects: Attach or manipulate Ninja Wire. Your next Thrown Chain attack gains \+15 Range and may turn one corner of up to 90 degrees if line can be physically routed.

#### Rigged Weapon
{:#rigged-weapon}

Tags, wire loops, poisons, and other legal attachments are prepared before the throw instead of awkwardly during it.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 11  
Speed: 8  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Thrown, Utility  
Effects: Prepare one thrown weapon with up to two compatible attachments such as a paper tag, wire loop, poison applicator, or similar legal tool without increasing the Speed of the next throw. All normal item costs and restrictions still apply.

## B-Rank
{:#b-rank-17}

#### Returning Arc
{:#returning-arc}

The weapon continues through its first line and curves toward a second target before it loses momentum.

Rank: B  
Stamina: \+14  
Damage: \+7dS  
Speed: \+3  
Tags: Chain 2, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: After resolving this Attack, choose a second creature within 5 yards of the first target. Make a second Accuracy roll at \-1; on hit, deal 65% final damage. The second hit cannot establish or advance Chain.

#### Scatter Run
{:#scatter-run}

The user moves while preparing the next handful, drawing and indexing tools between steps so the next release is already assembled when the new angle opens.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 9  
Speed: 2  
Tags: Chain X, Thrown, Utility, Movement  
Effects: Move up to 5 yards while drawing or readying thrown weapons. Your next multi-throw before 20 IC gains \+2 Accuracy and may include one additional weapon beyond your normal multi-throw limit.

## A-Rank
{:#a-rank-17}

#### Fuuma Reaper
{:#fuuma-reaper}

You send a Fuuma Shuriken through a continuous cutting route, forcing several targets to solve the same spinning weapon from different angles.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+26  
Speed: \+8  
Tags: Chain 3, Thrown, Attack  
Effects: Fuuma Shuriken only. Choose up to three creatures along one continuous path within Range. Roll Accuracy separately; divide Speed×PDB evenly among them. Each hit deals an equal share of the attack’s base weapon dice plus 4dS.

#### Wire Cage
{:#wire-cage}

Four thrown anchors pull Ninja Wire tight around a patch of battlefield, turning movement through it into a hazard.

Rank: A  
Stamina: 20  
Speed: 10  
Area: 6 within normal thrown Range  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Thrown, Utility  
Effects: Requires four thrown weapons and 20 yards of Ninja Wire. For 20 IC, creatures inside the Area have 30% less movement. The first time each creature leaves the Area, it makes Athletics vs your Athletics; on failure, apply Prone and Immobilization 2 until its next action.
